# Site Reliability Engineer (SRE) Charter

Last Updated: May 12, 2024

## 1. Purpose

Defines the responsibilities and goals of the SRE team.

## 2. Mission

Ensure reliable, scalable, and maintainable services through engineering practices.

## 3. Responsibilities

### 3.1 Reliability

- System uptime maintenance
- Service availability
- Performance optimization
- Incident response
- Root cause analysis
- Prevention strategies

### 3.2 Scalability

- Capacity planning
- Performance testing
- Load handling
- Resource optimization
- Growth preparation

### 3.3 Operations

- Infrastructure management
- Deployment support
- Monitoring
- Alerting
- On-call support
- Documentation

## 4. SLOs and SLIs

### 4.1 Service Level Objectives

- 99.9% uptime target
- <200ms response time
- Error rate <0.1%
- Availability > 99.9%

### 4.2 Service Level Indicators

- Measured continuously
- Tracked in dashboards
- Compared to SLOs
- Alert on deviation
- Trending analyzed

## 5. On-Call Rotation

### 5.1 Schedule

- One-week rotation
- Primary and backup
- On-call support 24/7
- Alert response <30 min
- Escalation policy

### 5.2 Support

- Primary owner
- Backup escalation
- Management escalation
- On-call pay
- Work-life balance
