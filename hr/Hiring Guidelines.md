# Hiring Guidelines

Last Updated: May 12, 2026

## 1. Recruitment Overview

### 1.1 Purpose

These guidelines ensure Lutervyn recruits qualified candidates through fair, consistent, and legally compliant processes.

### 1.2 Core Principles

- Equal opportunity for all candidates
- Merit-based selection
- Diversity and inclusion focus
- Efficient hiring process
- Transparent communication

### 1.3 Hiring Authority

Only authorized recruiters and hiring managers may conduct hiring. HR approval required for all offers.

## 2. Job Requisition Process

### 2.1 Approval Requirements

Before recruitment begins:
- Department head approval
- Budget confirmation
- HR review
- Job description finalization
- Compensation benchmarking

### 2.2 Job Posting

**Content Required**
- Job title and reporting structure
- Responsibilities and key duties
- Required qualifications
- Preferred qualifications
- Compensation range
- Benefits overview
- Application process

**Posting Locations**
- Lutervyn careers page
- LinkedIn
- Industry job boards
- Referral program
- University partnerships

### 2.3 Posting Duration

- Minimum: 14 days
- Standard: 30 days
- Extended: 60+ days for specialized roles
- Internal posting: 5 days before external

## 3. Application & Screening

### 3.1 Application Process

- Online application portal required
- Resume required
- Cover letter encouraged
- Portfolio optional
- Application deadline posted

### 3.2 Application Screening

Screening criteria:
- Education requirements met
- Experience level appropriate
- Hard skills match
- Geographic location (if relevant)
- Communication quality

### 3.3 Screening Timeline

- Applications reviewed within 5 business days
- Qualified candidates contacted
- Rejection emails sent to others
- Screening notes documented

### 3.4 Candidate Database

- All applications stored 1 year minimum
- Contact maintained for future opportunities
- Candidate feedback collected
- Privacy maintained

## 4. Interview Process

### 4.1 Interview Stages

**Phone Screen (30 minutes)**
- Conducted by recruiter
- Role overview provided
- Candidate background discussed
- Next steps explained

**First Interview (60 minutes)**
- With hiring manager
- Behavioral questions
- Role-specific questions
- Company overview
- Opportunity to ask questions

**Second Interview (90 minutes)**
- With team members (2-3 people)
- Technical/functional assessment
- Team fit evaluation
- Work scenario discussion
- Questions answered

**Third Interview (60 minutes)**
- Senior manager or executive
- Leadership questions
- Culture fit assessment
- Compensation discussion
- Final questions

### 4.2 Interview Questions

Approved question types:
- Background and experience
- Problem-solving scenarios
- Behavioral questions (STAR method)
- Technical assessments
- Cultural fit questions

Prohibited questions:
- Age or birth date
- Marital/family status
- Religious beliefs
- Disability (unless job-relevant)
- National origin
- Gender identity
- Personal medical history
- Criminal history (except certain roles)

### 4.3 Interview Evaluations

Interviewers provide:
- Scores on defined criteria (1-5 scale)
- Comments on strengths/weaknesses
- Recommendation (hire/no hire/maybe)
- Any concerns noted

### 4.4 Interview Timeline

- Phone screen decision: same day
- First interview feedback: within 24 hours
- Second interview decision: within 2 business days
- Third interview decision: within 1 business day
- Offer decision: within 2 business days

## 5. Assessment & Testing

### 5.1 Skills Assessment

**Available Assessments**
- Technical coding assessments
- Writing samples
- Design portfolio review
- Problem-solving tests
- Domain-specific tests

**Assessment Guidelines**
- Used for all roles of same type
- Applied consistently
- Results documented
- Scoring rubric provided
- Results reviewed by multiple people

### 5.2 Background Checks

**What's Verified**
- Employment history
- Education credentials
- Criminal records (where legal)
- Credit history (finance roles only)
- Driver's license (driving roles)

**Process**
- Authorized third-party provider
- Candidate consent required
- Written disclosure provided
- Results reviewed by HR
- Adverse action process if concerns

**Timeline**
- Initiated post-offer
- Results within 5-10 business days
- Conditional offer pending results
- Discrepancies addressed

### 5.3 Reference Checks

**Process**
- 2-3 references contacted
- Supervisor reference preferred
- Written questionnaire or verbal
- Information documented
- Candidate informed of contacts

**Reference Questions**
- Employment dates confirmed
- Position and responsibilities
- Performance rating
- Would rehire?
- Reason for leaving

## 6. Job Offers

### 6.1 Offer Authorization

Offers authorized by:
- Hiring manager
- Department head
- HR director
- CEO (C-level positions)

### 6.2 Offer Letter Content

- Position title
- Start date
- Reporting manager
- Compensation and bonus structure
- Benefits eligibility
- Contingency conditions
- Offer expiration date (typically 7 days)
- At-will employment statement

### 6.3 Compensation Benchmarking

- Market rates researched
- Salary bands established
- Internal equity considered
- Experience factored
- Budget constraints applied

### 6.4 Offer Communication

- Verbal offer made first
- Candidate given 24 hours to consider
- Written offer provided
- Offer expiration date stated
- Acceptance confirmation required

### 6.5 Contingencies

Standard contingencies:
- Background check clearance
- Education verification
- Reference checks
- Legal right to work confirmation
- Drug test (if applicable)
- Medical examination (if applicable)

## 7. Onboarding

### 7.1 Onboarding Process

**Pre-Start (1 week before)**
- Workspace prepared
- Equipment ordered
- Systems access set up
- Team introduction sent
- First-day logistics confirmed

**First Day**
- Welcome meeting
- Workspace tour
- IT setup and access
- Badge/keys provided
- Benefits overview
- Emergency contacts form

**First Week**
- Department introduction
- Role overview and expectations
- Meeting key colleagues
- System training
- Policy review

**First Month**
- Project assignments
- Mentor assignment
- Comprehensive training
- 30-day check-in meeting
- Feedback gathering

**First 90 Days**
- Regular 1-on-1s with manager
- Progress reviews at 30, 60, 90 days
- Feedback from colleagues
- Adjustment period completion
- Performance expectations clarified

## 8. Diversity & Inclusion

### 8.1 D&I Commitment

Lutervyn is committed to:
- Diverse workforce recruitment
- Inclusive hiring practices
- Equitable opportunity
- Bias reduction
- Representation goals

### 8.2 Unconscious Bias Training

All hiring managers complete:
- Annual bias training
- Scenario-based learning
- Interview training
- Legal compliance training
- Updates on new research

### 8.3 Diversity Goals

Target representation:
- Women: 40% of workforce
- Underrepresented minorities: 30%
- LGBTQ+: supportive environment
- Disabled individuals: 5% representation
- Neurodiversity: accommodation support

### 8.4 Accessibility

Accommodations available:
- Video interviews option
- ASL interpreters
- Accessibility features
- Extended deadlines if needed
- Dietary accommodations during events

## 9. Rejection & Post-Hiring

### 9.1 Candidate Rejection

**Timeline**
- Rejected promptly after decision
- Respectful communication
- Feedback offered when possible
- Future opportunity mentioned

**Communication**
- Email preferred for efficiency
- Personal call for final candidates
- Positive tone maintained
- Door left open for future

### 9.2 Applicant Tracking

- Applications stored 1 year
- Candidate pool maintained
- Talent pipeline developed
- Passive candidates contacted
- Skills match reviewed for openings

### 9.3 Hiring Metrics

Tracked metrics:
- Time to hire: target 30 days
- Quality of hire: 90-day retention 90%+
- Diversity hiring: tracking representation
- Source effectiveness: which channels work
- Offer acceptance rate: target 80%+

## 10. Compliance

### 10.1 Legal Compliance

- EEOC regulations followed
- ADA accommodations provided
- FCRA compliance for background checks
- State/local hiring laws observed
- OFCCP requirements met

### 10.2 Documentation

All hiring documented:
- Job posting and dates
- Application materials
- Interview feedback
- Assessment results
- Reference check notes
- Offer and acceptance
- Hiring decision rationale

### 10.3 Record Retention

- Hiring records kept 3 years minimum
- Digital storage preferred
- Secure access controls
- Regular backups
- Compliance audit trail
