# Employee Handbook

Last Updated: May 12, 2026

## 1. Welcome to Lutervyn

### 1.1 About This Handbook

This handbook outlines policies, benefits, and expectations for all Lutervyn employees. It applies to all full-time, part-time, and contract employees.

### 1.2 Company Overview

Lutervyn is a forward-thinking technology company committed to ethical innovation and employee success. Founded in 2024, we've grown to a team of passionate professionals dedicated to creating positive impact through technology.

### 1.3 Company Values

- **Integrity**: Honesty in all dealings
- **Excellence**: High-quality work
- **Innovation**: Creative problem-solving
- **Inclusion**: Diverse perspectives
- **Responsibility**: Ethical practices

## 2. Employment Relationship

### 2.1 At-Will Employment

Employment with Lutervyn is at-will, meaning either party may terminate the relationship at any time, for any lawful reason, with or without notice.

### 2.2 Employment Categories

**Full-Time**: 40+ hours weekly, eligible for benefits
**Part-Time**: Less than 40 hours weekly, limited benefits
**Contract**: Fixed-term independent contractors, no benefits

### 2.3 Equal Opportunity

Lutervyn is an equal opportunity employer. We do not discriminate based on race, color, religion, sex, national origin, age, disability, or any other protected status.

## 3. Compensation & Pay

### 3.1 Pay Periods

- Employees are paid bi-weekly
- Direct deposit available
- Pay stubs provided electronically

### 3.2 Overtime

Non-exempt employees receive overtime pay (1.5x regular rate) for hours exceeding 40 per week. Overtime requires manager approval.

### 3.3 Performance Bonuses

- Annual bonuses based on performance
- Eligibility: full-time employees after 6 months
- Range: 0-20% of base salary

### 3.4 Compensation Review

Compensation reviewed annually during performance reviews. Adjustments considered based on performance, market conditions, and budget availability.

## 4. Benefits

### 4.1 Health Insurance

- Medical, dental, vision coverage
- Company covers 80% of premium
- Plans available for employee + dependents
- 30-day waiting period

### 4.2 Retirement Plan

- 401(k) plan available
- Company match: 3% of salary
- Vesting schedule: immediate
- Plan year: January 1 - December 31

### 4.3 Paid Time Off

**Vacation Days**
- Year 1-2: 15 days
- Year 3-5: 20 days
- Year 5+: 25 days

**Sick Days**
- 10 days annually
- Can accumulate up to 30 days

**Holidays**
- 10 federal holidays
- Holiday pay at regular rate

### 4.4 Parental Leave

- 12 weeks for primary caregiver
- 4 weeks for secondary caregiver
- 80% pay during leave
- Job reinstatement guaranteed

### 4.5 Disability Insurance

- Short-term: 60% pay, up to 6 weeks
- Long-term: 60% pay after 6 weeks
- Waiting period: varies by plan

### 4.6 Life Insurance

- Basic life: $50,000 company-paid
- Supplemental life: available for purchase
- Dependent life: available

### 4.7 Professional Development

- $2,000 annual education budget
- Conference attendance support
- Certification program reimbursement
- Training courses covered

### 4.8 Wellness Program

- Gym membership subsidy: $50/month
- Mental health support: EAP available
- Wellness challenges: quarterly
- Annual wellness screening

## 5. Work Schedule

### 5.1 Standard Hours

Standard work hours are 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM, Monday - Friday, with 1-hour lunch break.

### 5.2 Flexibility

- Flexible start/end times with manager approval
- Core hours: 10:00 AM - 3:00 PM
- Alternate schedules available upon request

### 5.3 Break Time

- 15-minute breaks: morning and afternoon (non-paid)
- 1-hour lunch break (unpaid)
- Compliance with state break laws required

## 6. Remote Work

### 6.1 Remote Work Policy

- Eligible employees: individual contributor level and above
- Up to 3 days remote weekly
- 2 days in-office required
- Manager approval required

### 6.2 Home Office Setup

Company provides:
- $500 one-time equipment stipend
- Monitor, keyboard, mouse
- Desk chair reimbursement

Responsibilities:
- Secure internet connection
- Quiet work environment
- Cybersecurity compliance

### 6.3 Remote Work Communication

- Daily Slack status updates
- Video conference for meetings
- Responsive during core hours
- Equipment inventory tracking

## 7. Performance Management

### 7.1 Performance Reviews

**Frequency**
- New employees: 90-day review
- Annual reviews: every 12 months
- Mid-year check-ins: 6 months

**Process**
- Self-evaluation
- Manager feedback
- 1-on-1 discussion
- Written documentation

### 7.2 Performance Expectations

Employees are expected to:
- Complete assigned work on time
- Maintain professional conduct
- Support team goals
- Communicate effectively
- Contribute to company culture

### 7.3 Performance Improvement Plan

If performance is unsatisfactory:
1. Documented conversation
2. Written improvement plan
3. 30-60 day review period
4. Reassessment meeting
5. Potential termination if unmet

## 8. Code of Conduct

### 8.1 Professional Behavior

Employees must:
- Treat others with respect
- Maintain professional environment
- Avoid harassment or discrimination
- Comply with all laws and policies
- Report violations promptly

### 8.2 Confidentiality

Employees must:
- Protect company information
- Not disclose trade secrets
- Not share client data
- Maintain customer confidentiality
- Return materials upon termination

### 8.3 Conflicts of Interest

Employees must disclose:
- Financial interests in vendors
- Family relationships with employees
- External business interests
- Competing employment
- Significant personal relationships

### 8.4 Workplace Safety

- Report hazards immediately
- Follow safety procedures
- Participate in safety training
- Use protective equipment when required
- Maintain clean work area

### 8.5 Substance Abuse

- No illegal drugs
- Alcohol prohibited during work
- Prescription medication disclosure required
- Reasonable suspicion testing allowed
- Treatment resources available

## 9. Attendance & Punctuality

### 9.1 Attendance Expectations

- Regular, punctual attendance required
- Absences require notification
- Excessive absenteeism grounds for termination
- Perfect attendance recognized annually

### 9.2 Tardiness

- Three tardies = one absence
- Patterns documented and addressed
- Exceptions for public transportation delays

### 9.3 Absence Notification

- Notify manager before start time
- Text or call preferred
- Follow-up email within 1 hour
- Return to work note may be required after 3 consecutive days

## 10. Workplace Policies

### 10.1 Dress Code

Professional business casual:
- No jeans (except casual Fridays)
- No graphic t-shirts
- No athletic wear
- No visible tattoos (optional)
- Closed-toe shoes required

### 10.2 Personal Device Usage

- Limited personal device use during work
- Restricted on company premises
- Breaks designated for personal use
- No photography without approval

### 10.3 Social Media

- Don't post about confidential company information
- Don't represent company without authorization
- Personal accounts acceptable if clear distinction made
- Company accounts managed by marketing

### 10.4 Email & Internet Usage

- Company email for business purposes
- Limited personal use allowed
- Monitoring and filtering in place
- No harassment or explicit content
- Archives retain 7 years

### 10.5 Company Property

- All equipment remains company property
- Responsible for items issued
- Return required upon termination
- Personal devices not required
- Damage beyond normal wear charged to employee

## 11. Termination & Separation

### 11.1 Resignation

- Written notice preferred: 2 weeks minimum
- Return all company property
- Final paycheck includes accrued PTO
- COBRA information provided

### 11.2 Termination for Cause

Grounds for immediate termination:
- Theft or dishonesty
- Violence or threats
- Gross misconduct
- Policy violations
- Incompetence despite support

### 11.3 Termination Without Cause

- Company may terminate at any time
- Final paycheck within 48 hours
- Severance offered based on tenure
- Unused PTO paid out
- COBRA details provided

### 11.4 Exit Process

1. Return badge, keys, equipment
2. Change IT access
3. Forward work items
4. Collect personal belongings
5. Sign separation agreement if applicable

## 12. Workplace Conduct Violations

### 12.1 Disciplinary Process

First offense: Verbal warning
Second offense: Written warning
Third offense: Final written warning
Fourth offense: Termination

### 12.2 Serious Violations

Immediate suspension or termination:
- Theft
- Workplace violence
- Drug/alcohol use
- Fraud
- Harassment

### 12.3 Appeal Process

Employees may appeal discipline:
- Within 5 business days of notification
- Written appeal to HR
- Appeal meeting scheduled within 10 days
- Final decision within 5 days
