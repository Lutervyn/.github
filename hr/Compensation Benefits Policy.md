# Compensation & Benefits Policy

Last Updated: May 12, 2026

## 1. Compensation Philosophy

### 1.1 Purpose

Lutervyn's compensation program is designed to:
- Attract and retain top talent
- Reward performance fairly
- Maintain market competitiveness
- Align with company values
- Ensure equity and transparency

### 1.2 Core Principles

- Merit-based compensation
- Market-competitive salaries
- Performance recognition
- Internal equity
- Transparency in pay practices

## 2. Salary Structure

### 2.1 Salary Bands

Each position has an established salary band:
- Minimum: Entry-level for role
- Midpoint: Market rate with experience
- Maximum: Senior/high performer level

Position bands reviewed annually and adjusted for market changes.

### 2.2 Salary Determination

New hire salary based on:
- Job level and responsibility
- Required experience
- Educational credentials
- Geographic location (if applicable)
- Relevant skills and background
- Market data and benchmarking

### 2.3 Salary Review

**Timing**
- Conducted annually in January
- Aligned with performance review cycle
- Budget allocation completed in Q4

**Considerations**
- Performance rating
- Tenure with company
- Market salary changes
- Internal equity
- Budget availability
- Role criticality

**Potential Increases**
- Minimum: 2% (cost of living)
- Standard: 3-5% (with performance)
- Maximum: 10%+ (promotion/significant role change)

### 2.4 Promotion Increases

Promotion increases:
- Minimum: 10% salary increase
- Average: 15% increase
- Senior roles: 20%+ increase
- Effective: next pay period or first of month

### 2.5 Pay Equity

Lutervyn is committed to pay equity:
- Annual analysis by role and demographics
- Adjustments made for identified inequities
- Promotion opportunities equitable
- Transparent pay ranges
- External market checks

## 3. Bonuses & Variable Compensation

### 3.1 Annual Bonus Program

**Eligibility**
- Full-time employees only
- Minimum 6 months with company
- Employment through year-end

**Structure**
- Target: 10-20% of base salary
- Based on individual and company performance
- Performance threshold: meets expectations (3.0 rating)
- Company financial performance factor

**Performance Metrics**
- Individual goals: 50% of bonus
- Company goals: 30% of bonus
- Team goals: 20% of bonus

**Payment**
- Paid in February
- Calculated and finalized by January 31
- Withheld for cause terminations

### 3.2 Spot Bonuses

Ad-hoc bonuses for:
- Exceptional projects
- Problem-solving efforts
- Employee referrals ($1,000-$5,000)
- Customer recovery
- Process improvements

**Process**
- Manager nomination
- HR approval required
- 1-2 week turnaround
- Included in next paycheck

### 3.3 Sales Commissions

Applicable to: Sales roles

**Commission Structure**
- 5-15% of annual quota value
- Monthly payouts
- Year-to-date tracking
- Quota reset annually

**Draw Program**
- Quarterly advance: 25% of expected earnings
- Repaid from commissions earned
- Negative balance owed at termination

## 4. Equity Compensation

### 4.1 Stock Options

**Eligibility**
- Full-time employees
- Non-founders and investors eligible
- Equity committee approval required

**Grants**
- Based on role and level
- 4-year vesting schedule
- 1-year cliff
- Accelerated on change of control

**Strike Price**
- Set at fair market value (FMV)
- Board approved valuation
- No discounted options
- Documented valuation report

**Exercise**
- 10-year exercise window
- Monthly vesting (1/48th per month)
- Early exercise allowed
- Cashless exercise option available

### 4.2 Restricted Stock Units (RSUs)

**Grant Structure**
- Alternative to options
- No exercise price
- Full-value grants
- 4-year vesting typical

**Vesting Schedule**
- 25% cliff at 1 year
- Monthly vesting thereafter
- Double-trigger acceleration
- Acceleration on death/disability

### 4.3 Equity Plan Documentation

- Equity plan: reviewed by legal counsel
- Option agreement: individual agreements
- Supplemental documentation: SAFE agreements
- Exchange agreements: secondary sales
- 83(b) elections: filed timely

### 4.4 Equity Administration

- Cap table maintained and updated
- Exercise price tracking
- Vesting schedule management
- Shareholder records maintained
- Tax reporting (Forms 3921, 3922)

## 5. Benefits Overview

### 5.1 Health Insurance

**Coverage Types**
- Medical: comprehensive coverage
- Dental: preventive and restorative
- Vision: exams and eyewear

**Plans Available**
- HMO: lower cost, network required
- PPO: higher deductible, flexibility
- HSA-eligible: high deductible, tax advantage

**Company Contribution**
- Medical: 80% of premium
- Dental: 70% of premium
- Vision: 70% of premium

**Eligibility**
- Full-time employees
- Immediately upon hire
- Effective date: first of following month

**Dependent Coverage**
- Spouse/domestic partner: same rates
- Children: same rates until age 26
- Domestic partner requires affidavit

### 5.2 Retirement Plan (401k)

**Plan Details**
- Defined contribution plan
- Plan year: calendar year
- Plan administrator: external provider
- Investment options: 40+ mutual funds

**Contributions**
- Employee elective deferral: up to $23,500/year (2024)
- Employer match: 3% of salary
- Catch-up contributions: age 50+
- Automatic enrollment: 3% default

**Vesting**
- Employee deferrals: 100% vested immediately
- Employer match: 100% vested immediately
- Cliff vesting: not applicable

**Distributions**
- At separation: immediate eligibility
- In-service: after age 59.5 or hardship
- Required minimum: age 73
- Loan provisions: available

**Administration**
- Employee portal access
- Annual statements provided
- Education sessions quarterly
- Rebalancing tools available

### 5.3 Health Savings Account (HSA)

**Eligibility**
- Enrolled in HSA-eligible health plan
- No other health coverage
- Employee is not dependent on another plan
- Not enrolled in Medicare

**Contribution Limits (2024)**
- Individual: $4,150/year
- Family: $8,300/year
- Catch-up age 55+: additional $1,000
- Company contribution: up to $1,000

**Use of Funds**
- Eligible medical expenses
- Preventive care
- Prescriptions
- Dental and vision
- Mental health services

**Funds Carry Over**
- No "use it or lose it"
- Investment option available
- Portable upon separation
- Beneficiary designation available

### 5.4 Flexible Spending Account (FSA)

**Medical FSA**
- $3,200/year limit (2024)
- Dependent care: $5,000/year
- Use-it-or-lose-it: carryover limited
- Claims period: 2.5 months after year-end

### 5.5 Life Insurance

**Company-Paid Life Insurance**
- Basic benefit: $50,000
- Automatic enrollment
- Coverage continues if terminated
- No medical underwriting

**Supplemental Life Insurance**
- Employee purchase available
- Multiple of salary options: 1x to 5x
- Spouse coverage available: $10,000-$100,000
- Children coverage available: $5,000-$10,000
- Medical underwriting above thresholds

**Accidental Death & Dismemberment**
- Optional rider
- Coverage for specific incidents
- Benefit schedule provided

### 5.6 Disability Insurance

**Short-Term Disability (STD)**
- Company-paid coverage
- Benefit: 60% of salary
- Duration: up to 6 weeks
- Waiting period: 7 days

**Long-Term Disability (LTD)**
- Company-paid coverage
- Benefit: 60% of salary
- Duration: to age 65
- Elimination period: 90 days
- Definition: unable to perform own occupation

**Claim Process**
- Report to HR immediately
- Submit claim form
- Medical documentation required
- Integrated with workers' comp
- Benefit review at 6 months

## 6. Paid Time Off

### 6.1 Vacation Days

**Accrual**
- Year 1-2: 15 days annually
- Year 3-5: 20 days annually
- Year 5+: 25 days annually
- Accrued monthly (1.25-2.08 days)

**Usage**
- Advance notice: 2 weeks preferred
- Blackout dates: rare
- Manager approval: typical
- Rollover: 5 days max to next year
- Payout: unused at termination

### 6.2 Sick Days

**Accrual**
- 10 days annually
- Accrued monthly (0.83 days)
- Cumulative: up to 30 days
- Payout: not guaranteed at termination

**Usage**
- Personal illness
- Illness of family member
- Medical appointments
- Preventive care
- Paid in 4-hour increments

**Documentation**
- Doctor's note: required after 3 consecutive days
- Tracking: HR maintained
- Integration: may integrate with FMLA

### 6.3 Holidays

**Company Holidays**
1. New Year's Day
2. MLK Jr. Day
3. Presidents' Day
4. Memorial Day
5. Independence Day
6. Labor Day
7. Thanksgiving (2 days)
8. Christmas Eve
9. Christmas Day
10. New Year's Eve

**Holiday Pay**
- Non-exempt: straight time
- Exempt: no deduction
- Required work: 1.5x pay
- Part-time: prorated

### 6.4 Floating Holidays

- 2 additional holidays
- Employee choice dates
- 30 days' notice required
- Must be taken annually (no carryover)

### 6.5 Bereavement Leave

**Immediate Family**
- 5 days paid leave
- Travel time: 2 additional days
- Spouse, children, parents, siblings

**Extended Family**
- 3 days paid leave
- Grandparents, in-laws, nieces/nephews

**Additional Time**
- Unpaid leave: up to 2 weeks
- Job protection: guaranteed
- Communication: contact HR

### 6.6 Jury Duty & Witness

- Paid leave: up to 5 days

## 7. Parental Leave

### 7.1 Eligibility

- Full-time employees
- 12+ months employment required
- Biological, adoptive, or foster parent
- Same-sex, opposite-sex couples

### 7.2 Leave Duration

**Primary Caregiver**
- 12 weeks paid leave
- Up to 80% salary
- Job reinstatement guaranteed

**Secondary Caregiver**
- 4 weeks paid leave
- Up to 80% salary
- Concurrent or sequential option

**Additional Leave**
- 4 weeks unpaid
- Job protection maintained
- Health insurance continues

### 7.3 Timing

- Must begin within 12 months of birth/adoption
- Multiple children: separate leave
- Adoption: same as biological birth
- Foster placement: eligible

### 7.4 Benefits During Leave

- Health insurance: continues
- 401k: contributions suspended
- Paid leave: not accrued
- Bonuses: prorated

### 7.5 Return to Work

- Same position or equivalent
- Salary and benefits restored
- No retaliation for leave taking
- Flexible return schedule available
