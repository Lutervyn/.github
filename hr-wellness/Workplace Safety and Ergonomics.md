# Workplace Safety and Ergonomics

Last Updated: May 12, 2026

## 1. Overview

Lutervyn is committed to providing a safe and healthy work environment for all employees, whether working in the office or remotely.

## 2. Office Safety

### 2.1 General Safety
- Keep aisles and emergency exits clear at all times.
- Report any physical hazards (loose carpet, broken chairs) to the [Operations team](../operations/Office%20and%20Facilities%20Management.md).
- Use proper lifting techniques for heavy items (or ask for assistance).

### 2.2 Emergency Procedures
- Follow the posted evacuation maps in the event of a fire or other emergency.
- Do not use elevators during a fire alarm.

## 3. Ergonomics

### 3.1 Setup Guidelines
To prevent repetitive strain injuries (RSI):
- **Chair**: Adjust height so feet are flat on the floor and knees are at 90 degrees.
- **Monitor**: Top of the screen should be at or slightly below eye level.
- **Keyboard**: Arms should be relaxed at your side with elbows at 90 degrees.
- **Take Breaks**: Use the 20-20-20 rule (Every 20 minutes, look at something 20 feet away for 20 seconds).

### 3.2 Equipment
- Lutervyn provides ergonomic keyboards, mice, and monitor stands upon request.
- Height-adjustable desks are available in all main office locations.

## 4. Remote Work Safety

- Employees working from home are encouraged to perform a self-assessment of their workspace using our "Remote Ergonomics Checklist."
- Reimbursable home office equipment is covered under the [Expense Policy](../finance/Expense%20Policies.md).

## 5. Reporting Injuries

- All workplace injuries, however minor, must be reported to HR within 24 hours to ensure proper care and compliance with insurance requirements.
