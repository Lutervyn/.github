# Mental Health and Wellness Programs

Last Updated: May 12, 2026

## 1. Overview

At Lutervyn, we believe that employee well-being is fundamental to our success. We provide various resources to support the mental and physical health of our team.

## 2. Mental Health Support

### 2.1 Employee Assistance Program (EAP)
- Confidential, 24/7 support for personal or professional issues.
- Provides up to 6 free counseling sessions per year for employees and their families.

### 2.2 Mental Health Days
- In addition to standard PTO, employees are encouraged to take "Mental Health Days" as needed to rest and recharge. No explanation is required.

### 2.3 Subscriptions
- Free premium access to wellness apps (e.g., Headspace, Calm) for all employees.

## 3. Physical Wellness

### 3.1 Gym Reimbursement
- Up to **$50/month** toward gym memberships, yoga classes, or other fitness activities.

### 3.2 Healthy Snacks
- For those in the office, we provide a variety of healthy snack and beverage options in the kitchen.

## 4. Work-Life Balance

- **Meeting-Free Blocks**: Respect the [Scheduling and Calendar Policies](../operations/Scheduling%20and%20Calendar%20Policies.md).
- **Flexible Hours**: Focus on outcomes rather than specific "clock-in" times (subject to team requirements).
- **Social Connection**: Optional monthly team-building events and social Slack channels.

## 5. Wellness Stipend

- An annual **$500 Wellness Stipend** that can be used for items such as standing desks, fitness trackers, or ergonomic equipment not covered by the standard IT budget.

## 6. Stigma-Free Workplace

- We encourage open conversations about mental health.
- Managers receive training on how to support team members experiencing burnout or stress.
