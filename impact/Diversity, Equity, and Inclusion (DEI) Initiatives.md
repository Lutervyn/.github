# Diversity, Equity, and Inclusion (DEI) Initiatives

Last Updated: May 12, 2026

## 1. Our Commitment

Lutervyn thrives on the diversity of our team. We are committed to creating an inclusive environment where everyone, regardless of their background, feels they belong and has the opportunity to succeed.

## 2. Hiring and Recruitment

- **Inclusive Sourcing**: We actively seek candidates from underrepresented groups through targeted job boards and partnerships.
- **Unbiased Interviewing**: All interviewers undergo training to identify and mitigate unconscious bias.
- **Structured Interviews**: We use standardized questions and rubrics to ensure every candidate is evaluated fairly.

## 3. Workplace Inclusion

### 3.1 Employee Resource Groups (ERGs)
- We support the formation of ERGs (e.g., Women in Tech, LGBTQ+, Neurodiversity) to provide community and advocacy.
- ERGs have an annual budget and an executive sponsor.

### 3.2 Inclusive Benefits
- We offer benefits that support diverse needs, including gender-affirming care, parental leave for all parents, and flexible holidays.

## 4. Equity and Advancement

- **Pay Equity**: We conduct annual audits to ensure equal pay for equal work across the company.
- **Fair Promotion**: Our [Career Development Paths](../hr-development/Career%20Development%20Paths.md) are designed to be transparent and based on objective impact metrics.

## 5. Education and Awareness

- **DEI Training**: Mandatory annual training on topics such as microaggressions, allyship, and inclusive leadership.
- **External Speakers**: We host regular "Diversity Spotlights" featuring guest speakers from various backgrounds.

## 6. Accountability

- We track and internally share our diversity metrics annually.
- DEI goals are integrated into our [Annual Planning Process](../strategic-planning/Annual%20Planning%20Process.md).
