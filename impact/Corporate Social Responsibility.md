# Corporate Social Responsibility

Last Updated: May 12, 2026

## 1. Overview

Lutervyn is committed to being a positive force in the communities where we live and work. We believe that business success and social responsibility go hand-in-hand.

## 2. Core Pillars

### 2.1 Education and STEM
Supporting the next generation of engineers and innovators through:
- Local coding bootcamps.
- Mentoring high school students in underserved areas.
- Donating retired hardware to educational non-profits.

### 2.2 Charitable Giving
- **Employee Matching**: Lutervyn matches employee donations to registered 501(c)(3) organizations up to $500 per year.
- **Corporate Grants**: Annual grants to organizations aligned with our mission of AI ethics and accessibility.

### 2.3 Volunteering
- **Volunteer Time Off (VTO)**: Employees are granted 16 hours (2 days) of paid time off per year to volunteer at a charity of their choice.
- **Team Volunteer Events**: Quarterly organized events for teams to volunteer together.

## 3. Ethical Technology

- We prioritize projects that have a positive social impact.
- We strictly adhere to our [Ethics Guidelines](../ethics/Ethics.md) to ensure our technology is developed and used responsibly.

## 4. Reporting

- We publish an annual "Impact Report" summarizing our CSR activities, donations, and volunteer hours.
