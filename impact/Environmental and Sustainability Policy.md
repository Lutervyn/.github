# Environmental and Sustainability Policy

Last Updated: May 12, 2026

## 1. Overview

Lutervyn recognizes its responsibility to minimize its environmental footprint and contribute to a sustainable future.

## 2. Digital Infrastructure

- **Cloud Efficiency**: We prioritize cloud providers with strong carbon-neutral commitments (e.g., Google Cloud, AWS).
- **Code Optimization**: We write efficient code to minimize the energy consumption of our applications and infrastructure.
- **Data Retention**: By following our [Data Retention Procedures](../data-governance/Data%20Retention%20and%20Deletion%20Procedures.md), we reduce unnecessary storage and the energy required to maintain it.

## 3. Office Operations

- **Waste Reduction**: We aim for a paperless office environment. Where paper is used, it must be recycled.
- **Energy Conservation**: Our offices use LED lighting and motion sensors. Employees are encouraged to turn off equipment when not in use.
- **E-Waste**: All retired electronics are recycled through certified partners as per our [Device Management Policy](../it-ops/Device%20Management%20and%20Asset%20Lifecycle.md).

## 4. Travel and Commuting

- **Remote-First**: Our remote-first culture significantly reduces carbon emissions from daily commuting.
- **Virtual Meetings**: We prioritize virtual meetings over business travel whenever possible.
- **Commuter Benefits**: We offer subsidies for public transit and support for employees who cycle to work.

## 5. Sustainable Procurement

- We favor vendors who have clear, documented sustainability practices and certifications.

## 6. Commitment to Improvement

- We set annual targets for reducing our carbon footprint and report our progress in our annual Impact Report.
