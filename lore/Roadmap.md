## Feature Request Process

1. **Submit**: Create issue in feature-requests repo
2. **Discuss**: Community discusses idea
3. **Vote**: Community votes on priority
4. **Review**: Team reviews top ideas
5. **Plan**: Accepted features added to roadmap
6. **Implement**: Feature developed
7. **Release**: Feature shipped

## Release Schedule

- **Major Releases**: Quarterly (v1.0, v2.0, etc.)
- **Minor Releases**: Monthly (v1.1, v1.2, etc.)
- **Patch Releases**: Weekly (v1.0.1, v1.0.2, etc.)
- **Hotfixes**: As needed for critical issues

## Deprecation Policy

- Features are deprecated with 6 months notice
- Replacement features provided
- Migration guides published
- Support during transition
- Sunsetting after support ends
