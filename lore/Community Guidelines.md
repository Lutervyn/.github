# Community Guidelines

Effective Date: January 1, 2024
Last Updated: May 12, 2024

## 1. Our Community

Lutervyn's community is made up of developers, designers, users, and enthusiasts from around the world. We're committed to fostering an inclusive, respectful, and collaborative environment for everyone.

## 2. Our Values

### 2.1 Respect

- Treat everyone with respect
- Value different perspectives
- Listen actively
- Be kind and considerate
- Acknowledge mistakes

### 2.2 Inclusivity

- Welcome everyone
- Embrace diversity
- Create safe spaces
- Address barriers
- Amplify underrepresented voices

### 2.3 Collaboration

- Work together
- Share knowledge
- Help each other
- Build on each other's ideas
- Celebrate collective success

### 2.4 Transparency

- Be open and honest
- Share decisions
- Explain reasoning
- Acknowledge problems
- Communicate clearly

## 4. Expected Behavior

### 4.1 Be Respectful

- Use inclusive language
- Be kind and patient
- Assume good intentions
- Address disagreement respectfully
- Don't take things personally

### 4.2 Be Constructive

- Provide helpful feedback
- Suggest improvements
- Share knowledge
- Help others learn
- Contribute to solutions

### 4.3 Be Honest

- Share your perspective truthfully
- Admit when you're wrong
- Give credit where due
- Disclose conflicts of interest
- Be transparent about limitations

### 4.4 Be Safe

- Protect others' privacy
- Don't share personal information
- Report safety concerns
- Maintain confidentiality
- Respect boundaries

## 5. Unacceptable Behavior

We do not tolerate:

### 5.1 Harassment

- Unwanted sexual attention
- Intimidation or threats
- Stalking or doxxing
- Targeted exclusion
- Trolling and baiting

### 5.2 Discrimination

- Based on race, color, ethnicity
- Based on national origin
- Based on sex or gender identity
- Based on sexual orientation
- Based on disability
- Based on age
- Based on religion
- Based on political views

### 5.3 Abuse

- Personal attacks
- Name-calling
- Insults and slurs
- Violent language
- Dehumanizing language

### 5.4 Spam and Self-Promotion

- Excessive self-promotion
- Unrelated advertising
- Spam messages
- Chain messages
- Multi-level marketing

### 5.5 Illegal Content

- Child exploitation
- Copyrighted material
- Malware or exploits
- Illegal goods
- Instructions for illegal activities

## 6. Reporting Issues

### 6.1 How to Report

**For GitHub Issues:**
1. Click report button on comment/issue
2. Describe violation
3. Provide evidence if possible
4. Submit report

**Email Reports:**
- community@lutervyn.com
- Include context and evidence
- Report as soon as possible

**Anonymous Reporting:**
- https://lutervyn.pages.dev/report-issue
- No personal information required
- Can receive updates anonymously

### 6.2 What to Include

- Description of violation
- Links to relevant content
- Screenshots if helpful
- Context about the situation
- Your contact info (optional)
- Any relevant history

## 7. Enforcement

### 7.1 Response

We investigate reports:

- Within 24-48 hours for most issues
- Promptly for critical issues
- Thoroughly and fairly
- With confidentiality
- Without retaliation

### 7.2 Possible Actions

**Warning:**
- Explained violation
- Request for change
- Education offered
- Opportunity to correct

**Moderation:**
- Content removal
- Comment hiding
- Temporary restrictions
- Time-limited actions

**Suspension:**
- Temporary ban from spaces
- Access restricted
- Duration based on severity
- Opportunity to appeal

**Permanent Removal:**
- Account termination
- Banning from community
- Legal referral if needed
- Only for severe/repeated violations

### 7.3 Appeals

- Appeals accepted within 30 days
- Reviewed by different moderator
- Fair and thorough review
- Final decision provided
- Limited to one appeal

## 8. Safety and Privacy

### 8.1 Personal Information

## 14. Examples

### 14.1 Good Interactions

- "Great question! Here's what I found..."
- "I see your point. However, consider..."
- "That's a good idea. We could improve it by..."
- "I'm new to this. Can someone help?"
- "Thanks for the clarification!"

### 14.2 Interactions to Avoid

- "That's the dumbest idea ever"
- "People like you always..."
- "You're clearly incompetent"
- Dismissive eye-roll emojis
- Aggressive all-caps responses
