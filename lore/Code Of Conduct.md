# Code of Conduct

Last Updated: May 12, 2024

## Our Commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming, inclusive, and respectful community.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and inclusive
- Use inclusive language
- Be patient and supportive
- Focus on constructive feedback
- Respect different viewpoints
- Acknowledge mistakes
- Focus on what's best for community

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment or discrimination
- Hate speech or slurs
- Personal attacks
- Unwelcome advances
- Threats or intimidation
- Sharing others' private information
- Other unethical conduct

## Diversity and Inclusion

We welcome:
- All backgrounds
- All experience levels
- All identities
- All perspectives
- All contributions

## Reporting

### How to Report

1. Email: conduct@lutervyn.com
2. Include:
   - Incident description
   - Individuals involved
   - Witnesses (if any)
   - Date and location

### Confidentiality

- Identity protected
- Details confidential
- No retaliation
- Fair process

## Enforcement

### Response

1. Investigation
2. Corrective action
3. Follow-up
4. Appeal process

### Consequences

- Warning
- Temporary suspension
- Permanent ban
- Escalation to authorities

## Appeal

- Right to appeal
- Review by different party
- Documented process
- Final decision

## Contact

- Code of Conduct: conduct@lutervyn.com
- Incidents: incidents@lutervyn.com
- Appeals: appeals@lutervyn.com
