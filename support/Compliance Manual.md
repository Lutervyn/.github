## 5. Breach Notification

### 5.1 Notification Timeline

**GDPR:**
- Notify supervisory authority within 72 hours
- Notify affected individuals (if high risk)
- Personal data breach register maintained

**CCPA:**
- Notify California AG if 100+ California residents
- Notify consumer without unreasonable delay
- No specific timeline

**State Laws:**
- Most states: "without unreasonable delay"
- Some states: 30-60 days
- Check applicable state laws

### 5.2 Notification Content

- Nature of the breach
- Data involved
- Affected individuals
- Likely consequences
- Measures taken
- Recommendations
- Contact information
