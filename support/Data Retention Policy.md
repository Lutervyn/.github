# Data Retention Policy

Last Updated: May 12, 2024

## 1. Purpose

Defines how long data is retained and when it's deleted.

## 2. Retention Schedule

### 2.1 Active Data

- **User accounts**: Duration of service
- **User content**: Duration of service
- **Transactions**: 7 years
- **Logs**: 12 months
- **Analytics**: 24 months

### 2.2 Backup Data

- **Daily backups**: 7 days
- **Weekly backups**: 4 weeks
- **Monthly backups**: 12 months
- **Archive**: 7 years

### 2.3 Personal Data

- **PII**: Until deletion requested
- **Usage data**: 24 months
- **Payment data**: 7 years
- **Communications**: 12 months

## 3. Deletion

### 3.1 Automatic Deletion

- Scheduled deletion
- Per retention schedule
- Automated process
- No manual intervention

### 3.2 User Deletion

- Right to deletion
- Submit request
- Processed within 30 days
- Confirmation provided

### 3.3 Legal Hold

- If litigation:
  - Data preserved
  - Until resolved
  - Legal requirement

## 4. Legal Retention

Retain longer if:
- Tax law requirement
- Legal obligation
- Court order
- Regulatory requirement
- Contractual obligation

## 5. Secure Deletion

- Encrypted deletion
- Secure erasure
- Verified destruction
- Certificate issued
- Audit trail maintained
