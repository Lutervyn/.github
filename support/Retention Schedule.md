# Data Retention Schedule

Last Updated: May 12, 2024

## Monthly Review

Data retention reviewed and purged monthly according to schedule.

## Retention Periods

### Immediate Deletion

- Temporary files
- Session data
- Cache files
- Logs (after archival)

### Short-term (1-3 months)

- Email
- Chat logs
- Support tickets (closed)
- Analytics data

### Medium-term (3-12 months)

- Activity logs
- Backup logs
- Audit trails
- Performance data

### Long-term (1-7 years)

- Financial records (7 years)
- Personnel files (5 years)
- Contracts (5-7 years)
- Legal documents (7 years)

### Permanent

- Core business records
- Compliance documents
- Historical records
- Archives

## Deletion Process

1. Identify items for deletion
2. Review compliance
3. Verify no legal hold
4. Initiate secure deletion
5. Verify deletion
6. Document process
7. Archive logs
