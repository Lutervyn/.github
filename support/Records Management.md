# Records Management

Last Updated: May 12, 2024

## 1. Purpose

Manage creation, retention, and disposal of business records.

## 2. Record Types

### 2.1 Financial
- Invoices
- Receipts
- Ledgers
- Bank statements
- Tax returns

### 2.2 Personnel
- Employee files
- Performance reviews
- Training records
- Salary records
- Benefits enrollment

### 2.3 Legal
- Contracts
- Agreements
- Correspondence
- Litigation files
- Compliance docs

### 2.4 Operational
- Board minutes
- Policies
- Procedures
- Project files
- Vendor files

## 3. Retention Schedule

| Record Type | Retention | Disposal |
|------------|-----------|----------|
| Financial | 7 years | Shred |
| Personnel | 5 years | Shred |
| Contracts | 5 years+ | Shred |
| Email | 3 years | Delete |
| Logs | 1 year | Delete |

## 4. Storage

### 4.1 Physical
- Secured filing
- Locked cabinets
- Fire protection
- Climate control
- Access restricted

### 4.2 Digital
- Encrypted storage
- Backed up
- Version control
- Access logged
- Secure deletion

## 5. Disposal

- Scheduled deletion
- Secure destruction
- Certification
- Documentation
- No data retention
