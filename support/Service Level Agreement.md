# Service Level Agreement (SLA)

Last Updated: May 12, 2024

## 1. Service Coverage

- All core services
- API endpoints
- Web platform
- Infrastructure
- Data persistence

## 2. Availability Targets

### 2.1 Monthly Availability

- **Standard**: 99% (7.2 hours downtime)
- **Pro**: 99.5% (3.6 hours downtime)
- **Enterprise**: 99.9% (43 minutes downtime)

### 2.2 Measurement

- Measured in UTC
- From Lutervyn monitoring
- Automated detection
- Global perspective

## 3. Response Time

### 3.1 Performance

- **API Response**: <200ms average
- **Web Load**: <2 seconds
- **Page Render**: <3 seconds
- **Upload**: Per file size

### 3.2 SLA Credits

- <99%: 10% credit
- <98%: 25% credit
- <95%: 50% credit
- <90%: 100% credit

## 4. Maintenance Windows

### 4.1 Scheduled

- **Frequency**: Monthly
- **Duration**: 4 hours maximum
- **Notice**: 7 days advance
- **Timing**: Off-peak hours
- **Not counted**: Toward SLA

### 4.2 Emergency

- **Notice**: Best effort
- **Duration**: As needed
- **Frequency**: Rare
- **Counted**: Toward SLA

## 5. Support

### 5.1 Support Hours

- **Standard**: Business hours (8-5 ET)
- **Pro**: Extended (7-7 ET)
- **Enterprise**: 24/7

### 5.2 Response Times

- **Critical**: 1 hour
- **High**: 4 hours
- **Medium**: 8 hours
- **Low**: 24 hours

## 6. Remedies

### 6.1 Service Credits

- Automatic application
- Applied to next invoice
- Cannot be refunded
- Cannot be transferred
- Sole remedy

### 6.2 Requesting Credits

- Must request within 30 days
- Provide incident details
- Support will review
- Credit calculated

## 7. Exclusions

- Customer configuration
- Customer misuse
- DDoS attacks (unmitigated)
- Third-party services
- Force majeure
- Maintenance windows

## 8. Contact

- SLA Issues: sla@lutervyn.com
- Support: support@lutervyn.com
- Billing: billing@lutervyn.com
