# Whistleblower Protection Policy

Last Updated: May 12, 2024

## 1. Purpose

Protect employees who report violations or concerns.

## 2. What to Report

- Legal violations
- Ethical breaches
- Policy violations
- Safety concerns
- Financial fraud
- Discrimination
- Harassment
- Other misconduct

## 3. How to Report

### 3.1 Internal Channels

- Manager or supervisor
- HR department
- Ethics officer
- Compliance team
- Anonymous hotline

### 3.2 External Channels

- Regulatory agencies
- Law enforcement
- Legal counsel
- Media (if appropriate)

## 4. Protection

### 4.1 Anti-Retaliation

- No retaliation allowed
- No adverse action
- No discrimination
- Protection guaranteed
- Clear policy

### 4.2 Confidentiality

- Identity protected
- Information confidential
- Anonymous reporting
- Minimal disclosure
- Need-to-know basis

## 5. Investigation

- Prompt investigation
- Independent review
- Fair process
- Documented findings
- Corrective action
