# Governance Charter

Last Updated: May 12, 2024

## 1. Board of Directors

### 1.1 Composition

- CEO: Executive leadership
- CFO: Financial oversight
- General Counsel: Legal
- Independent directors: Governance
- Diverse expertise
- Term limits

### 1.2 Responsibilities

- Strategic direction
- Risk oversight
- Compliance
- Stakeholder representation
- Performance evaluation

## 2. Committees

### 2.1 Audit Committee

- Financial audit
- Internal audit
- Risk assessment
- Compliance verification
- Independent members

### 2.2 Compensation Committee

- Executive compensation
- Pay equity
- Benefits programs
- Performance evaluation

### 2.3 Governance Committee

- Board structure
- Policies
- Shareholder matters
- Sustainability

## 3. Meetings

- Quarterly Board meetings
- Committee meetings monthly
- Annual shareholder meeting
- Special meetings as needed
- Minutes recorded
