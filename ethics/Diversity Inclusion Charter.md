# Diversity & Inclusion Charter

Effective Date: January 1, 2024
Last Updated: May 12, 2024

## 1. Vision

Lutervyn envisions a world where technology is created and shaped by people from all backgrounds and experiences. Diversity and inclusion are not just ethical imperatives—they're fundamental to innovation, better decision-making, and our mission to serve all users equitably.

## 2. Principles

### 2.1 Respect and Dignity

- Every person deserves respect
- Dignity must be protected
- Differences are valued
- Discrimination is unacceptable
- Safe environment for all

### 2.2 Equal Opportunity

- Equal access to opportunities
- Meritocracy-based advancement
- Bias-free hiring and promotion
- Equal pay for equal work
- Accessible career development

### 2.3 Representation

- Diverse workforce at all levels
- Leadership representation
- Equitable hiring and promotion
- Mentorship for underrepresented groups
- Accountability for goals

### 2.4 Belonging

- Everyone feels they belong
- Inclusive culture
- Psychological safety
- Open communication
- Community and connection

## 3. Scope

This charter applies to:

- All employees and contractors
- Hiring and recruitment
- Promotion and advancement
- Compensation and benefits
- Workplace culture
- Products and services
- Community and outreach
- Supply chain

## 4. Commitments

### 4.1 Workforce Diversity

We commit to:

- Recruiting diverse talent
- Building diverse leadership
- Retaining diverse employees
- Creating inclusive environment
- Supporting employee resource groups
- Tracking and reporting metrics

**Goals (by 2025):**
- 50% gender diversity
- Representative racial/ethnic diversity
- 5% employees with disabilities
- LGBTQ+ inclusive workplace
- Neurodiversity representation

### 4.2 Inclusive Culture

We foster:

- Psychological safety
- Open dialogue
- Diverse perspectives valued
- Inclusive meetings
- Accessible communication
- Mental health support

### 4.3 Equitable Policies

We maintain:

- Bias-free hiring
- Transparent advancement
- Equitable compensation
- Flexible work arrangements
- Parental leave
- Family medical leave
- Accessibility accommodations

### 4.4 Product Inclusion

We build:

- Accessible products
- Inclusive user experience
- Multiple language support
- Culturally appropriate
- Representative imagery
- Inclusive documentation

## 5. Protected Characteristics

We provide equal opportunity regardless of:

- Race and ethnicity
- Color and national origin
- Sex and gender identity
- Sexual orientation
- Age
- Disability
- Religion and belief
- Marital and family status
- Military status
- Political affiliation
- Socioeconomic background
- Neurodiversity
- Immigration status
