# Social Media Policy

Last Updated: May 12, 2024

## 1. Purpose

Guidance for company and employee social media use.

## 2. Official Channels

- Twitter: @Lutervyn
- LinkedIn: /company/lutervyn
- GitHub: /lutervyn
- Facebook: Lutervyn
- Instagram: @lutervyn

## 3. Approved Posts

- Company updates
- Product announcements
- Blog links
- Event promotion
- Team highlights
- Industry news
- Community engagement

## 4. Prohibited

- No personal attacks
- No discrimination
- No confidential info
- No client data
- No false claims
- No spam
- No harassment

## 5. Employee Guidelines

### 5.1 Personal Accounts

- Maintain professionalism
- Disclose affiliation clearly
- No false representation
- Company policy applies
- Personal responsibility

### 5.2 Company Accounts

- Approved posters only
- Scheduled content
- Approval process
- Brand consistency
- Quick response

## 6. Engagement

- Respond promptly
- Be professional
- Address concerns
- Escalate issues
- Maintain brand voice

- Communications: comms@lutervyn.com
