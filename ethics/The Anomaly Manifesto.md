# The Anomaly Manifesto\n\nLast Updated: May 12, 2024\n\n## Purpose\n\nThis manifesto articulates Lutervyn's core values and commitment to ethical technology.\n\n## Our Vision\n\nWe envision a future where technology serves humanity with integrity, transparency, and respect for human dignity.\n\n## Core Principles\n\n### 1. Human-Centric Design\n\nWe prioritize human well-being in every decision:\n- Technology serves people, not vice versa\n- Privacy and autonomy respected\n- Accessibility for all\n- User empowerment\n\n### 2. Transparency\n\nWe operate with openness:\n- Clear communication\n- Algorithm transparency\n- Data handling disclosed\n- No deception\n- Honest about limitations\n\n### 3. Responsibility\n\nWe take accountability:\n- For our technology's impact\n- For employee well-being\n- For societal effects\n- For environmental impact\n- For governance quality\n\n### 4. Inclusivity\n\nWe embrace diversity:\n- Diverse team\n- Inclusive culture\n- Accessibility standards\n- Community engagement\n- Global perspective\n\n### 5. Innovation\n\nWe push boundaries ethically:\n- Responsible innovation\n- Risk assessment\n- Stakeholder input\n- Continuous improvement\n- Ethical review\n\n## Commitments\n\n### To Our Users\n- Respect your privacy\n- Protect your data\n- No dark patterns\n- Clear terms\n- User control\n\n### To Our Employees\n- Fair compensation\n- Safe workplace\n- Growth opportunities\n- Voice and agency\n- Work-life balance\n\n### To Society\n- Beneficial impact\n- Environmental care\n- Community contribution\n- Ethical practices\n- Positive change\n\n## Contact\n\n- Ethics: ethics@lutervyn.com\n- Values: values@lutervyn.com
