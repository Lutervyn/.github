# Human Rights Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2024
Last Updated: May 12, 2024

## 1. Commitment

Lutervyn is committed to respecting and promoting human rights in all our operations and throughout our value chain. We believe that respect for human rights is fundamental to our business and society.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to:

- All Lutervyn employees and contractors
- Suppliers and vendors
- Business partners
- Service providers
- Operations worldwide

## 3. Principles

We respect:

- International human rights standards
- Universal Declaration of Human Rights
- International Labour Organization conventions
- Local laws and regulations
- Freedom of association
- Right to collective bargaining
- Safe working conditions
- Fair wages and benefits
- Non-discrimination
- Child labor prohibitions
- Forced labor prohibitions

## 4. Labor Rights

### 4.1 Fair Employment

- Competitive compensation
- Fair working hours
- Safe workplace
- Professional development
- Equal opportunity
- Non-exploitation

### 4.2 Freedom of Association

- Right to unionize
- Collective bargaining
- No retaliation
- Open communication
- Employee representation

### 4.3 Safe Working Conditions

- OSHA compliance
- Injury prevention
- Health services
- Equipment safety
- Emergency procedures
- Regular inspections

## 5. Non-Discrimination

We prohibit discrimination based on:

- Race
- Color
- Religion
- Sex
- National origin
- Age
- Disability
- Sexual orientation
- Gender identity
- Marital status
- Political belief
- Protected activity

## 6. Child Labor

We strictly prohibit:

- Employment of children under 15
- Child labor in any form
- Hazardous work for minors
- Exploitation of children
- Child trafficking

We comply with ILO conventions on child labor.

## 7. Forced Labor

We prohibit:

- All forms of forced labor
- Human trafficking
- Debt bondage
- Wage theft
- Mandatory overtime
- Coercion

## 8. Data Privacy and Rights

### 8.1 Privacy Rights

- Respect for personal data
- Privacy protection
- Data minimization
- Informed consent
- Access rights
- Correction rights
- Deletion rights

### 8.2 Digital Rights

- Freedom of expression
- Freedom from surveillance
- Right to access information
- Digital security
- Net neutrality support

## 9. Community Engagement

### 9.1 Stakeholder Consultation

- Community input
- Indigenous rights

- Transparent communication

### 9.2 Community Investment

- Education programs

## 10. Supply Chain

### 10.1 Supplier Standards

All suppliers must:

- Respect human rights
- Follow labor laws
- Ensure safe conditions
- Prohibit child labor
- Prohibit forced labor
- Provide fair wages
- Allow freedom of association

### 10.2 Supplier Auditing

- Regular assessments
- On-site audits
- Third-party verification
- Corrective action plans
- Remediation support
- Termination if needed

## 11. Grievance Mechanism

### 11.1 How to Report

Employees and stakeholders can report:

- **Hotline**: +1 (555) 123-4567
- **Email**: humanrights@lutervyn.com
- **Portal**: https://lutervyn.pages.dev/report-issue
- **Anonymous**: Yes, anonymous reporting available
- **Confidential**: All reports treated confidentially

### 11.2 Investigation

- Prompt investigation
- Fair process
- No retaliation
- Remediation if substantiated
- Regular follow-up
- Confidentiality maintained

## 12. Remediation

For substantiated violations:

- Immediate corrective action
- Compensation if applicable
- Restoration of dignity
- Prevention of recurrence
- Support for affected parties
- Public disclosure if appropriate
