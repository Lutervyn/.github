# Anti-Corruption Policy

Last Updated: May 12, 2024

## 1. Commitment

Lutervyn is committed to ethical business practices and zero tolerance for corruption.

## 2. Prohibited Conduct

### 2.1 Bribery

- No bribes offered
- No bribes accepted
- No facilitation payments
- No benefits for favor
- No special treatment

### 2.2 Conflicts of Interest

- Disclosed properly
- Avoided where possible
- Managed transparently
- No preferential treatment
- Arms' length transactions

### 2.3 Government Relations

- Compliant lobbying
- Legal contributions
- Transparent advocacy
- No improper influence
- Public reporting

## 3. Gifts and Entertainment

- Modest gifts only
- Proper documentation
- No cash gifts
- No entertainment bribes
- Reasonable value

## 4. Compliance

### 4.1 Training

- Annual mandatory
- All employees
- Certification required
- Testing required

### 4.2 Reporting

- Hotline available
- Anonymous reporting
- No retaliation
- Investigation procedure
- Confidentiality
