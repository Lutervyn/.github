# Stakeholder Engagement Policy

Last Updated: May 12, 2024

## 1. Purpose

Engage stakeholders in meaningful dialogue about business impact and ethics.

## 2. Stakeholder Groups

- Employees
- Customers
- Community
- Regulators
- Investors
- Partners
- General public

## 3. Engagement Methods

### 3.1 Communication

- Regular updates
- Transparent reporting
- Open dialogue
- Feedback channels
- Response process

### 3.2 Consultation

- Before major changes
- Impact assessment
- Input collection
- Feedback incorporation
- Explanation of decisions

### 3.3 Participation

- Advisory boards
- Working groups
- Community programs
- Volunteer opportunities
- Mentorship

## 4. Topics

- Business practices
- Environmental impact
- Social responsibility
- Governance
- Ethics
- Innovation
- Community needs
