# Expense Policies

Last Updated: May 12, 2026

## 1. Expense Management Overview

### 1.1 Purpose

Establish clear guidelines for employee expenses ensuring compliance, efficiency, and fairness.

### 1.2 General Principles

- Business purpose: required
- Reasonable: cost-appropriate
- Documented: receipts required
- Timely: submission deadline
- Honest: accurate reporting

## 2. Travel Expenses

### 2.1 Pre-Travel Approval

**Required Process**
- Manager: prior approval
- Business justification: documented
- Travel dates: specified
- Estimated budget: provided
- Funding: verified available

**Denial Grounds**
- Business justification: insufficient
- Budget: unavailable
- Timing: inappropriate
- Alternative: videoconference available

### 2.2 Airfare

**Standards**
- Coach: standard class
- Direct: when available
- Timing: reasonable lead time
- Economy: lowest reasonable option

**Approvals**
- First class: CEO approval required
- Premium cabin: director+ approval
- International: VP+ approval
- Exceptions: documented

**Non-Reimbursable**
- Personal entertainment: movies
- Upgrades: personal purchase
- Travel insurance: company provides
- Seat selection: premium seats

### 2.3 Lodging

**Standards**
- Hotel: reasonable quality
- Rate: market-competitive
- Location: near meeting location
- Safety: in secure areas

**Limits by City**
- Major metros (NYC, LA, SF): $250/night
- Other major cities: $200/night
- Standard cities: $150/night
- Small towns: $100/night

**Non-Reimbursable**
- Resort fees: not paid
- In-room entertainment: not paid
- Parking: if over $15/night
- Housekeeping: tips only

### 2.4 Ground Transportation

**Approved Methods**
- Rental car: if cost-effective
- Taxi/Uber: per trip
- Public transit: encouraged
- Parking: reasonable rates
- Rideshare: Uber/Lyft

**Guidelines**
- Most economical: preferred
- Rental car: standard/mid-size
- Gas: company reimburses
- Insurance: waive personal coverage
- Parking: $30/day max

**Non-Reimbursable**
- Parking violations: not paid
- Traffic tickets: employee responsible
- Valet: not reimbursed
- Premium seating: personal choice

### 2.5 Meals While Traveling

**Daily Allowance**
- Per diem: $50/day maximum
- Actual receipts: submit
- Reasonable: moderate restaurants
- Alcohol: $20 per meal limit
- Tips: 18% standard

**Meals with Clients**
- Business meal: legitimate
- Alcohol: reasonable amounts
- Atmosphere: business-appropriate
- Amount: proportionate to purpose

**Non-Reimbursable**
- Meals: if duplicated by host
- Alcohol: outside policy limits
- Entertainment: alcoholic beverages only
- Personal guests: not reimbursed

### 2.6 Incidental Expenses

**Approved**
- Baggage fees: one bag
- Visas: if required
- Vaccinations: if required
- Phone charges: business calls
- Business center: if needed

**Non-Approved**
- Laundry: unless multi-week
- Movies/entertainment: personal
- Personal care: toiletries
- Gifts: personal items

## 3. Home Office & Remote Work

### 3.1 Equipment Allowance

**One-Time Provision**
- Desk/table: up to $400
- Chair: up to $400
- Monitor: up to $300
- Accessories: up to $100
- Total: $500 per receipt requirement

**Company-Provided Equipment**
- Laptop: provided
- Monitor: provided
- Keyboard/mouse: provided
- Software: provided

### 3.2 Internet & Phone

**Reimbursement**
- Internet: not reimbursed typically
- Company phone: if provided
- Personal phone: not reimbursed
- Cellular: only business use

## 4. Professional Development

### 4.1 Conference & Training

**Approval Process**
- Manager: prior approval required
- Business case: documented
- Budget: verified
- Registration: approved vendor

**Covered Expenses**
- Conference: registration fee
- Travel: per travel policy
- Hotels: per travel policy
- Meals: per travel policy
- Materials: notebooks, handouts

**Non-Covered**
- Spouse attendance: not paid
- Companion events: not paid
- Personal development: external
- Advanced degrees: see tuition policy

### 4.2 Tuition Reimbursement

**Eligibility**
- Full-time employees
- 6+ months tenure
- Job-related: coursework
- Manager approval
- Passing grade: C or higher

**Maximum**
- Annual: $2,000
- Lifetime: $10,000
- Books/materials: included
- Testing: included

**Repayment Clause**
- If departure: within 1 year
- 100%: repayment required
- Graduated: after 1 year
- Forgiven: if 2+ years

### 4.3 Professional Memberships

**Reimbursable**
- Industry associations: relevant
- Professional licensing: required
- Annual membership: reasonable cost
- Conferences: included

**Non-Reimbursable**
- Sports clubs: not business-related
- General gyms: separate benefit
- Country clubs: personal
- Social organizations: not job-related

## 5. Meals & Entertainment

### 5.1 Business Meals

**With Employees**
- Team lunch: standard allowance
- Per person: $25 limit
- Occasional: regular basis
- Alcohol: company policy applies

**With Clients/Vendors**
- Business meal: documented
- Business purpose: recorded
- Attendees: noted
- Alcohol: proportionate

**Non-Business Meals**
- Personal lunch: not reimbursed
- Family dinners: not reimbursed
- Personal entertainment: not paid

### 5.2 Entertainment Expenses

**Approved**
- Client entertainment: business-related
- Sporting events: entertaining clients
- Golf/outings: client entertainment
- Dinner/drinks: business meals

**Documentation**
- Purpose: business reason
- Attendees: names and affiliations
- Venue: location noted
- Amount: itemized receipt

**Non-Approved**
- Personal entertainment: not paid
- Spouse entertainment: not paid
- Alcohol-only: requires meal

## 6. Expense Submission

### 6.1 Expense Report

**Timing**
- Submission: within 30 days
- Timely: encouraged
- Quarterly: accumulation maximum
- Extensions: with CFO approval

**Required Information**
- Date: of expense
- Vendor: company name
- Category: expense type
- Amount: total cost
- Business purpose: reason
- Attendees: if applicable
- Manager: initials/approval

### 6.2 Documentation

**Receipt Requirements**
- Original: submitted
- Itemized: detail required
- Credit card: statement attachment OK
- Amounts: readable
- Currency: USD or converted

**Missing Receipts**
- Under $25: written explanation
- $25-$100: manager approval
- Over $100: not reimbursed
- Exception: CFO approval

### 6.3 Submission Process

**Steps**
1. Gather receipts
2. Complete expense form
3. Categorize expenses
4. Manager approval
5. Finance processing
6. Reimbursement: within 2 weeks

**System**
- Expensify: primary tool
- Manual form: if needed
- Email: backup submission
- Digital receipts: preferred

## 7. Reimbursement & Payment

### 7.1 Reimbursement Timeline

- Processing: 5-7 business days
- Payment method: direct deposit typical
- Check: if requested
- Review: for discrepancies

### 7.2 Correction Process

- Discrepancy: notified
- Clarification: requested
- Resubmission: if needed
- Resolution: within 5 days

## 9. Policy Violations

### 9.1 Violations

**Examples**
- False receipts
- Personal expenses
- Excessive amounts
- Unauthorized travel
- Fraud: deliberate

### 9.2 Consequences

**First Offense**
- Denial: of reimbursement
- Warning: documented

**Second Offense**
- Denial: of reimbursement
- Written warning: file

**Serious/Fraud**
- Denial: all pending
- Investigation: formal
- Termination: possible
- Legal action: if warranted
