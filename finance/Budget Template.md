# Budget Template & Process

Last Updated: May 12, 2026

## 1. Budgeting Overview

### 1.1 Purpose

The budget process establishes financial targets and spending parameters for fiscal periods.

### 1.2 Budget Cycle

- Planning: August - September
- Approval: October
- Execution: January 1 - December 31
- Monthly reviews: ongoing
- Quarterly reforecasting: as needed

### 1.3 Responsible Parties

- CFO: overall budget management
- Department heads: department budgets
- Finance: tracking and reporting
- CEO: approval authority
- Board: strategic oversight

## 2. Budget Categories

### 2.1 Revenue Projections

**Sources**
- Product sales: main revenue
- Consulting services: additional
- Partnerships: revenue shares
- Licensing: recurring
- Other: miscellaneous

**Forecasting**
- Historical trends: analyzed
- Market conditions: considered
- Growth assumptions: documented
- Seasonal patterns: accounted for
- Conservative: approach used

### 2.2 Personnel Costs

**Components**
- Salaries: base compensation
- Bonuses: performance-based
- Benefits: health, 401k, etc.
- Payroll taxes: 7.65% employer
- Recruiting: acquisition costs
- Training: development

**Calculations**
- Headcount plan: by role
- Salary increases: projected
- New hires: timeline included
- Turnover: 15% typical assumed
- Burden rate: total cost factor

### 2.3 Operating Expenses

**Categories**
- Office: rent, utilities, supplies
- Technology: software, hardware
- Communications: phones, internet
- Marketing: advertising, events
- Facilities: maintenance, cleaning
- Insurance: policies and coverage
- Professional: legal, accounting
- Travel: employee travel
- Equipment: machinery, tools
- Subscriptions: software, services

### 2.4 Capital Expenditures

**Definition**
- Equipment over $5,000 useful life
- Technology infrastructure
- Office improvements
- Vehicle purchases

**Process**
- Capitalized on balance sheet
- Depreciated over useful life
- Approval required: over $10,000
- ROI analysis: required

### 2.5 Research & Development

**Components**
- R&D salaries: engineers, researchers
- Equipment: testing, development
- Facilities: lab, development space
- External research: partnerships
- Patents: application and filing

### 3.3 Assumptions Documentation

Each budget includes:
- Headcount assumptions
- Salary increase assumptions: 3% typical
- Revenue growth rate: market-based
- Customer acquisition cost: historical
- Churn rate: historical data
- Inflation assumptions: 2-3%
- Currency assumptions: if applicable
- Pricing assumptions: product rates

### 3.4 Variance Analysis

New vs. Previous Year:
- Changes documented
- Percentage variance shown
- Rationale provided
- Red flags: reviewed
- Approval: required if >15% variance

## 4. Budget Review & Approval

### 4.1 Finance Review

Finance team:
- Completeness check
- Accuracy verification
- Consistency check: across departments
- Formatting: standardization
- Preliminary total: calculated

### 4.2 Department Head Review

Heads present:
- Strategy alignment
- Key drivers and assumptions
- Risks and contingencies
- Q&A from finance and executives
- Approval or revisions

### 4.3 Executive Team Review

Executive team:
- Strategic alignment: company goals
- Cross-departmental: dependencies
- Resource prioritization

- Preliminary approval: recommended

### 4.4 Board Approval

Board review:
- Strategic presentation
- Key highlights and risks
- Financial projections: impact
- Approval vote: required
- Authority level: defined

## 5. Budget Allocation & Controls

### 5.1 Budget Vs. Actual

**Tracking Process**
- Monthly P&L: generated
- Actuals vs. Budget: compared
- Variance: analyzed
- Explanations: documented
- Trends: monitored

**Variance Thresholds**
- >5%: reviewed by manager
- >10%: finance notified
- >15%: executive review
- >25%: corrective action required

### 5.2 Spending Controls

**Approval Authority**
- Under $1,000: manager approval
- $1,000-$5,000: director approval
- $5,000-$25,000: VP approval
- $25,000+: CFO approval

**Budget Freeze**
- Can be implemented as needed
- Non-essential spending: halted
- Essential only: approved
- Exceptions: executive authority
- Communication: all departments

### 5.3 Budget Adjustments

**Mid-Year Reforecasting**
- Conducted: June (Q2)
- Based on: first half actual results
- Adjusted for: changed conditions
- If variance >10%: adjustment made
- Updated: management and board

**Budget Transfers**
- Between cost centers: allowed
- Within department: manager approval
- Cross-department: finance approval
- Documentation: reason required

- Strategic adjustments

**Schedule**
- Q1 review: April

- Q4 review: January

### 6.3 Annual Budget Review

**Components**
- Full year actual results
- Budget vs. actual: comprehensive
- Variance analysis: detailed
- Learning and insights
- Next year planning

## 7. Forecasting & Projections

**Frequency**

**Tools**

- Annual projection: high-level
- Scenario analysis: upside/downside

## 8. Zero-Based Budgeting

### 8.1 Principles

- Justify all spending: each year
- No baseline assumptions
- Prioritize from zero
- Align with strategy
- Resource allocation: optimized

### 8.2 Process

**Step 1: Identify Activities**
- Key business activities
- Goals supported
- Outcomes delivered

**Step 2: Estimate Costs**
- Required resources
- Cost drivers
- Alternative approaches
- Efficiency opportunities

**Step 3: Rank Activities**
- Strategic priority
- Business impact
- Risk assessment
- Resource requirements

**Step 4: Allocate Budget**
- Top priorities first
- Constraints applied
- Trade-offs made
- Allocation approved
