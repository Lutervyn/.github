# Accounting Policies

Last Updated: May 12, 2026

## 1. Accounting Standards

### 1.1 Accounting Framework

- Adopt: GAAP (Generally Accepted Accounting Principles)
- Private company: practical expedients used
- Consistency: year-to-year
- Transparency: financial reporting
- Audit: external annual audit

### 1.2 Fiscal Year

- Calendar year: January 1 - December 31
- Month-end close: 3 business days
- Quarter-end close: 5 business days
- Year-end close: 15 business days

### 1.3 Record Keeping

- Retain: 7 years minimum
- Digital storage: primary
- Backup systems: daily backups
- Access controls: restricted
- Audit trail: maintained

## 2. Revenue Recognition

### 2.1 Revenue Policy

**Product Sales**
- Recognized: upon delivery
- Performance obligation: transfer control
- FOB Shipping Point: customer risk
- Payment terms: Net 30 standard

**Subscription Revenue**
- Recognized: monthly/quarterly
- Ratably: over service period
- Performance obligation: continuous service
- Deferred: advance payments

**Consulting Services**
- Recognized: upon completion
- Milestone-based: if applicable
- Time and materials: monthly

**Licensing**
- Recognized: over license term
- Upfront fee: initial, if applicable
- Usage-based: monthly

### 2.2 Revenue Recognition Process

**Step 1: Identify Contract**
- Enforceable rights
- Probable consideration
- Collection likely

**Step 2: Identify Obligations**
- Distinct goods/services
- Performance obligations
- Transfer timing

**Step 3: Determine Price**
- Transaction price
- Standalone selling price
- Variable consideration

**Step 4: Allocate Price**
- Allocation: to obligations
- Based on: standalone prices

**Step 5: Recognize Revenue**
- When/as obligation satisfied
- Control transfer
- Amount determined

### 2.3 Bad Debt

- Reserve: 2-5% of receivables
- Write-off: authorized by CFO
- Collections: pursued aggressively
- Aging analysis: monthly

## 3. Expense Recognition

### 3.1 Expense Policies

**Capitalization Threshold**
- Capital: $5,000+
- Expense: below threshold
- Office supplies: always expensed
- Software: case-by-case evaluation

**Capitalized Assets**
- Depreciated: over useful life
- Office equipment: 5 years
- Computers: 3 years
- Furniture: 7 years
- Leasehold improvements: lease term

### 3.2 Accrual Accounting

**Recognition**
- Recorded: when incurred
- Not when paid
- Invoice or documented: evidence
- Matching principle: with revenue

**Cut-off**
- Year-end expenses: accrued
- Significant items: reviewed
- Manual entries: documented
- Reconciliation: monthly

### 3.3 Travel & Entertainment

**Reimbursable**
- Airfare: coach class
- Hotels: reasonable rates
- Ground: taxis, rideshare
- Meals: $50/day limit
- Client entertainment: approved

**Non-Reimbursable**
- Personal expenses
- Movies/entertainment: personal
- Alcohol: limit $20/meal
- Spouse travel: not reimbursed
- Home office: standard deduction

**Documentation**
- Receipts: for all expenses
- Business purpose: stated
- Submission: within 30 days
- Approval: manager

### 3.4 Depreciation & Amortization

**Depreciation Methods**
- Straight-line: primary
- Useful lives: by asset type
- Residual value: assumed $0
- Consistent: year-to-year

**Schedule of Lives**
- Computers: 3 years
- Office furniture: 7 years
- Equipment: 5 years
- Building improvements: lease term
- Vehicles: 5 years

**Intangible Assets**
- Software: useful life
- Trademarks: indefinite (tested)
- Patents: remaining legal life
- Customer lists: 5-10 years
- Goodwill: indefinite (tested)

## 4. Balance Sheet Items

### 4.1 Cash & Equivalents

**Classification**
- Cash: company accounts
- Equivalents: money market <90 days
- Restricted: separate presentation
- Foreign: recorded at spot rate

**Reconciliation**
- Monthly: bank reconciliation
- Timing: resolved
- Reconciling items: documented
- Variance: investigated

### 4.2 Accounts Receivable

**Valuation**
- Net: of allowance
- Reserve: 2-5% based on history
- Aging: monitored monthly
- Write-off: authorized

**Aging Analysis**
- Current: 0-30 days
- 31-60 days: review
- 61-90 days: contact
- 90+ days: collection action

### 4.3 Inventory

**Valuation Method**
- FIFO: First-In-First-Out
- Weighted average: if not feasible
- Lower of cost or market
- Physical counts: quarterly

**Obsolescence**
- Reserve: reviewed quarterly
- Aged inventory: evaluated
- Write-down: if impaired
- Disposal: authorized

### 4.4 Accounts Payable

**Recording**
- Upon receipt: goods/services
- Net amount: after discounts
- Accrual: even if not invoiced
- Terms: tracked for payment

**Discount Management**
- Early payment: 2% if available
- Tracking: system alerts
- Cash benefit: evaluated

### 4.5 Debt

**Classification**
- Current: due within 12 months
- Non-current: beyond 12 months
- Interest: accrued monthly
- Principal: reducing schedule

**Debt Covenants**
- Tracked: quarterly
- Compliance: maintained
- Breaches: reported to lender
- Remediation: if triggered

### 4.6 Equity

**Common Stock**
- Par value: $0.001
- Authorized: 10 million shares
- Issued: tracked in cap table
- Treasury: if repurchased

**Preferred Stock**
- Terms: in stock agreements
- Liquidation preference: specified
- Conversion: exercisable
- Dividends: if applicable

**Retained Earnings**
- Annual: net income added
- Dividends: if declared
- Adjustments: as needed
- Equity: rolled forward

## 5. Closing Procedures

### 5.1 Month-End Close

**Timeline: 3 Business Days**

Day 1:
- Expense coding: reviewed
- Accruals: recorded
- Reconciliation: initiated
- Trial balance: prepared

Day 2:
- Bank reconciliation: completed
- AP verification: completed
- AR aging: reviewed
- Adjustments: recorded

Day 3:
- Financial statements: prepared
- Variance analysis: completed
- Audit review: if needed
- Approval: by controller

### 5.2 Quarter-End Close

**Additional Procedures**
- Depreciation: calculated
- Allowances: evaluated
- Inventory: counted
- Receivables: aged
- Deferred items: reviewed
- Accruals: verified

### 5.3 Year-End Close

**Comprehensive Process**
- Full reconciliation: all accounts
- Goodwill: impairment testing
- Investments: fair value assessment
- Contingencies: reviewed
- Subsequent events: evaluated
- Audit: external audit conducted
- Financial statements: final
- SEC/Tax: filings prepared

## 6. Internal Controls

### 6.1 Segregation of Duties

**Key Separations**
- Authorization: approval authority
- Execution: transaction recording
- Reconciliation: independent review
- No single person: all steps

### 6.2 Approval Authority

**Spending Authority**
- Under $1,000: manager
- $1,000-$5,000: director
- $5,000-$25,000: VP
- Above $25,000: CFO/CEO
- Exception: board approval >$100k

**Check Signings**
- Two signatures: required
- Over $50,000: CFO + CEO
- Under $50,000: CFO + Mgr
- Authorized signers: listed

### 6.3 Documentation

**Supporting Documentation**
- Invoices: obtained
- Contracts: approved
- Purchase orders: matched
- Receiving reports: obtained
- Payment authorization: verified

### 6.4 System Controls

**IT Controls**
- Access: restricted by role
- Passwords: changed quarterly
- Audit logs: maintained
- Transaction limits: set
- Approval workflows: enforced
