# Invoice & Payment Procedures

Last Updated: May 12, 2026

## 1. Invoicing Overview

### 1.1 Purpose

Establish consistent invoicing procedures ensuring accurate billing and timely revenue recognition.

### 1.2 Invoicing Authority

- Sales: creates invoices
- Finance: reviews and approves
- Customer: invoice delivery
- AR: account management

## 2. Invoice Creation

### 2.1 Invoice Content

**Required Elements**
- Invoice number: sequential
- Invoice date: current date
- Customer name: official name
- Customer address: billing location
- Customer PO: if referenced
- Description: service/product detail
- Quantity: units delivered
- Unit price: cost per unit
- Total amount: extended price
- Tax: if applicable
- Payment terms: Net 30 standard
- Due date: calculated
- Invoice notes: if applicable
- Wire info: if applicable
- Contact info: payment contact

### 2.2 Invoice Number Assignment

**Numbering System**
- Format: YYYY-MM-#### (2026-01-0001)
- Sequential: no gaps
- Unique: no duplicates
- Tracked: in system
- Retention: 7 years minimum

### 2.3 Invoice Timing

**Issuance**
- Upon delivery: of goods/services
- Periodic: for subscriptions
- Milestone: for projects
- As-earned: revenue recognition basis

**Delivery Method**
- Email: primary
- Portal: customer access
- PDF: standard format
- Electronic: archive

## 3. Payment Terms

### 3.1 Standard Terms

**Net 30**
- Payment due: 30 days post-invoice
- Standard: most customers
- No discount: if paid early
- Late fee: 1.5% monthly if applicable

**Alternative Terms**
- Net 15: high-value customers
- Net 45: preferred customers
- Net 60: enterprise customers
- COD: pre-payment
- Monthly: subscription billing

### 3.2 Discounts

**Early Payment**
- 2% discount: if paid by day 10
- Calculation: discount offered
- Advertising: optional in invoice

**Volume Discounts**
- Tiered: by purchase amount
- Annual: if thresholds met
- Negotiated: per contract

### 3.3 Late Payments

**Invoice Follow-Up**
- Day 30: reminder email
- Day 45: second notice
- Day 60: phone call
- Day 90: collection action

**Late Fees**
- Applied: if terms exceeded
- Rate: 1.5% monthly (18% annual)
- Calculation: on invoice amount
- Legal: if not prohibited by law

## 4. Revenue Recognition

### 4.1 Revenue Timing

**Product Sales**
- Recognized: upon delivery
- Control: transferred to customer
- Risk: passes to customer
- Performance: obligation satisfied

**Service Revenue**
- Completion: upon project completion
- Time-based: earned over service period
- Milestone: upon milestone achievement
- Subscription: monthly/quarterly ratably

### 4.2 Deferred Revenue

**Advance Payments**
- Recorded: as deferred revenue
- Liability: recognized
- Recognition: as service provided
- Timing: systematic recognition

**Examples**
- Annual subscriptions: recognize monthly
- Prepaid services: recognize as delivered
- Retainers: recognize as earned
- Maintenance contracts: monthly recognition

## 5. Payment Processing

### 5.1 Payment Methods

**Accepted Methods**
- ACH transfer: primary
- Credit card: 2-3% processing fee
- Wire transfer: international
- Check: if required
- Direct deposit: for vendors

### 5.2 Cash Application

**Matching**
- Customer payment: matched to invoice
- Amount: verified
- Timing: recorded
- Variance: investigated

**Partial Payments**
- Allocation: to oldest invoice
- Documentation: noted
- Follow-up: remainder due

**Overpayments**
- Tracking: separate account
- Refund: if requested
- Credit: if preferred
- Documentation: clear

### 5.3 Payment Processing Timeline

**Typical Schedule**
- Check: 3-5 business days
- ACH: 1-2 business days
- Wire: same day
- Credit card: 1-2 business days

## 6. Accounts Receivable

### 6.1 AR Aging Analysis

**Categories**
- Current: 0-30 days
- 31-60 days: past due
- 61-90 days: significantly past
- 90+ days: severely past due
- Written-off: uncollectible

**Monitoring**
- Monthly: aging report
- Trend: analyzed
- Collection: prioritized
- Reserve: assessed

### 6.2 Collection Procedures

**Day 31-45**
- Email: friendly reminder
- Check: with contact
- Verify: invoice received

**Day 46-60**
- Call: contact customer
- Discussion: payment plan
- Escalate: to management

**Day 61-90**
- Manager: contact
- Negotiation: payment terms
- Interest: discussed
- Timeline: requested

**Day 90+**
- Legal: review for collection
- Collection: agency referral
- Write-off: if uncollectible
- Legal action: if warranted

### 6.3 Write-Off Procedures

**Criteria**
- Uncollectible: attempts failed
- Bankrupt: customer status
- Unreachable: contact impossible
- Legal: cost exceeds amount

**Process**
- Documentation: effort documented
- Management: approval required
- Accounting: recorded as expense
- Tax: potential deduction

## 7. Bad Debt Reserve

### 7.1 Reserve Calculation

**Method**
- Percentage: 2-5% of receivables
- Aging analysis: by age category
- Historical: based on experience
- Conservative: erring on side of caution

**Adjustment**
- Monthly: evaluated
- Specific: for known uncollectibles
- General: for portfolio
- Recorded: in income statement

### 7.2 Reserve Changes

**Increase**
- Portfolio growth: additional provision
- Deterioration: customer issues
- Economic: downturn impact
- Record: as expense

**Decrease**
- Collections: actual payment
- Portfolio: reduction
- Improvement: customer status
- Record: as reversal

## 8. Customer Credit Management

### 8.1 Credit Approval

**New Customers**
- Credit application: required
- References: verified
- Financial: statements reviewed
- Credit score: checked if available
- Limit: set accordingly

**Credit Limits**
- Determined by: creditworthiness
- Typically: 30-60 days sales
- Reviews: annually
- Adjustments: based on history

### 8.2 Credit Monitoring

**Ongoing**
- Financial: status monitored
- Payment: history tracked
- News: industry/company news
- Alerts: if issues detected

**Changes**
- Positive: increase limit
- Negative: decrease limit
- Termination: severe issues
- Discussion: with customer

## 9. Invoice Disputes

### 9.1 Dispute Process

**Initiation**
- Customer: claims issue
- Documentation: evidence provided
- Recording: noted and logged
- Investigation: initiated

**Investigation**
- Details: gathered
- Facts: documented
- Determination: made
- Communication: to customer

**Resolution**
- Correct: if error confirmed
- Dispute: if disagreement
- Partial: if partial valid
- Credit: if applicable

### 9.2 Dispute Resolution

**Options**
- Adjust invoice: if error
- Issue credit memo: if applicable
- Accept loss: if cost effective
- Legal: if warranted

**Timeline**
- Response: within 10 days
- Investigation: within 15 days
- Resolution: within 30 days
- Communication: regular updates

## 10. Billing Schedules

### 10.1 Monthly Billing

**Process**
- Service period: each month
- Meter: collected on month-end
- Invoice: issued by 5th
- Due: on Net 30 basis
- Payment: expected by 30th

### 10.2 Subscription Billing

**Timing**
- Anniversary: same day each month
- Automated: monthly billing
- Pro-rata: for mid-cycle changes
- Auto-renew: unless cancelled

**Management**
- Portal: customer self-service
- Updates: customer changes made
- Cancellation: available anytime
- Notification: pre-renewal alert
