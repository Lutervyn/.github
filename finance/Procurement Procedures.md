# Procurement Procedures

Last Updated: May 12, 2026

## 1. Procurement Overview

### 1.1 Purpose

Establish efficient vendor management and purchasing processes ensuring quality, value, and compliance.

### 1.2 Principles

- Best value: not lowest cost only
- Transparency: in process
- Compliance: regulatory and policy
- Efficiency: streamlined processes
- Relationship: vendor partnerships

## 2. Vendor Selection

### 2.1 Vendor Qualification

**Evaluation Criteria**
- Quality: capabilities and standards
- Price: competitive rates
- Reliability: on-time delivery
- Financial: stability assessment
- References: customer feedback
- Capacity: volume capability
- Location: proximity and timezone

### 2.2 Vendor Due Diligence

**Requirements**
- Business license: current
- Insurance: valid coverage
- References: at least 3
- Financial: statements reviewed
- Compliance: certification verified
- Background: if applicable

### 2.3 Vendor Agreement

**Standard Terms**
- Price: terms and pricing
- Payment: terms and method
- Delivery: timeline and location
- Quality: standards and specs
- Liability: limitations and insurance
- Termination: conditions and notice
- Confidentiality: information protection

### 2.4 Vendor List

**Maintenance**
- Updated: quarterly
- Approved vendors: listed
- Contact information: current
- Pricing: documented
- Performance: scored

## 3. Purchase Requisition Process

### 3.1 Purchase Request

**Initiation**
- Department: identifies need
- Form: completed and submitted
- Approval: manager authorization
- Budget: verified available
- Justification: provided

**Information Required**
- Description: item/service
- Quantity: units or hours
- Specifications: detailed
- Budget code: cost center
- Requested delivery: date/timeline
- Business justification: purpose

### 3.2 Approval Workflow

**Authority Levels**
- Under $1,000: manager approval
- $1,000-$5,000: director approval
- $5,000-$25,000: VP approval
- Above $25,000: CFO approval
- Over $100,000: CEO/board

**Timeline**
- Approval: within 3 business days
- Expedited: available if urgent
- Denial: reason communicated
- Revision: if needed

## 4. Vendor Selection & RFP Process

### 4.1 Request for Proposal (RFP)

**When Required**
- Over $10,000: typical threshold
- Strategic: long-term relationship
- New vendors: evaluation
- Competitive: multiple quotes

**RFP Contents**
- Business need: described
- Specifications: detailed requirements
- Scope: deliverables defined
- Timeline: project schedule
- Pricing: requested format
- References: required
- Terms: standard contract

### 4.2 Vendor Selection Process

**Step 1: Publish RFP**
- Minimum vendors: 3 quotes
- Timeline: response period stated
- Format: submission requirements
- Contact: questions process

**Step 2: Evaluate Proposals**
- Scoring rubric: applied
- Evaluation team: multiple reviewers
- Quality: 40% weighting
- Price: 30% weighting
- Reliability: 20% weighting
- Innovation: 10% weighting

**Step 3: Negotiate Terms**
- Top proposals: selected
- Discussions: conducted
- Refinements: negotiated
- Final pricing: confirmed

**Step 4: Award & Notify**
- Decision: documented
- Winner: notified
- Others: thanked
- Debriefs: available if requested

## 5. Purchase Order Process

### 5.1 PO Creation

**Required Information**
- Vendor: name and contact
- Description: item/service
- Quantity: units specified
- Unit price: per specification
- Total: calculated
- Delivery: location and date
- Terms: payment and conditions

**PO Numbering**
- Sequential: number system
- Year prefix: 2026-001
- Unique: no duplicates
- Tracked: in system

### 5.2 PO Approval

**Standard POs**
- Under $1,000: department manager
- $1,000-$5,000: director
- $5,000-$25,000: VP
- Above $25,000: CFO

**Rush Orders**
- Expedited: approval process
- Documentation: required
- Justification: business need
- Timeline: compressed

### 5.3 PO Distribution

- Vendor: official copy
- Department: purchasing copy
- Finance: for accrual
- Receiving: for matching
- File: documentation

## 6. Receiving & Inspection

### 6.1 Goods Receipt

**Process**
- Delivery: received
- Count: quantities verified
- Inspection: condition checked
- Damage: noted if present
- Signature: received acknowledged

**Documentation**
- Receiving report: completed
- PO number: referenced
- Quantities: recorded
- Dates: noted
- Issues: documented

### 6.2 Quality Inspection

**Procedures**
- Specification match: verified
- Quality: inspected
- Defects: documented
- Samples: tested if applicable
- Rejection: if unacceptable

**Non-Conforming Goods**
- Reported: to vendor immediately
- Decision: return or discount
- Documentation: of issue
- Resolution: within 48 hours

## 7. Invoicing & Payment

### 7.1 Invoice Processing

**Receipt**
- Invoice: from vendor
- PO number: referenced
- Receipt: verified
- Accuracy: checked

**Three-Way Match**
- PO details: match
- Receipt details: match
- Invoice details: match
- Discrepancies: resolved
- Variance: >2% flag

### 7.2 Invoice Review

**Verification**
- Amounts: correct
- Dates: reasonable
- Extensions: accurate
- Taxes: appropriate
- Discounts: applied

**Approval**
- Department: signs off
- Finance: verifies match
- Coding: assigned
- Payment: authorized

### 7.3 Payment Process

**Timing**
- Standard: Net 30 terms
- Early payment: 2% discount if available
- Holds: only if disputed
- Regular: bi-weekly run

**Methods**
- ACH: primary method
- Check: if required
- Credit card: convenience
- Wire: international

**Documentation**
- Payment: recorded
- Reconciliation: to invoice
- Accrual: cleared
