# Accessibility and WCAG Compliance

Last Updated: May 12, 2026

## 1. Our Commitment

Lutervyn is committed to digital inclusion. We strive to make our products accessible to everyone, regardless of their physical or cognitive abilities.

## 2. Standards

- **WCAG 2.1 Level AA**: All Lutervyn web and mobile applications must aim for compliance with the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at the AA level.

## 3. Key Requirements

### 3.1 Visual
- **Contrast**: Maintain a minimum contrast ratio of 4.5:1 for text.
- **Alt Text**: Provide descriptive alternative text for all meaningful images.
- **Color**: Do not use color as the sole method of conveying information.

### 3.2 Interaction
- **Keyboard Nav**: Ensure all functionality is accessible via keyboard alone.
- **Focus States**: Maintain clear, visible focus indicators.
- **Screen Readers**: Use semantic HTML and ARIA labels to ensure compatibility with screen readers.

### 3.3 Cognitive
- **Clarity**: Use clear, simple language.
- **Predictability**: Maintain consistent navigation and interaction patterns.

## 4. Testing and Verification

- **Automated Testing**: Integrate accessibility checks (e.g., Axe-core) into the CI/CD pipeline.
- **Manual Review**: Conduct periodic manual audits using screen readers (NVDA, VoiceOver).
- **User Testing**: Include individuals with disabilities in our user research and beta testing groups.

## 5. Training

- All frontend engineers and product designers must undergo annual training on accessibility best practices.

## 6. Feedback

- Users can report accessibility issues via accessibility@lutervyn.com. We aim to acknowledge reports within 48 hours.
