# Feature Request and Prioritization Process

Last Updated: May 12, 2026

## 1. Submission Process

### 1.1 Who Can Submit?
- **Customers**: Via the Support Portal or feedback widget.
- **Sales/CS**: Via the CRM or feedback management tool.
- **Employees**: Via the internal #product-feedback Slack channel.

### 1.2 Required Information
- **Title**: Clear and concise.
- **Problem Statement**: What problem is the user trying to solve? (Avoid specifying the solution).
- **Impact**: How many users are affected? How often?
- **Urgency**: Is this a blocker or a nice-to-have?

## 2. Review and Triage

### 2.1 Frequency
- Feedback is reviewed weekly by the Product Management team.

### 2.2 Statuses
- **Awaiting Review**: Newly submitted.
- **Under Consideration**: Aligns with strategy, being researched.
- **Planned**: Added to the roadmap.
- **Won't Do (For Now)**: Does not align with current strategy or too complex.
- **Completed**: Feature is live.

## 3. Prioritization Framework (RICE)

To ensure objective decision-making, we use the RICE framework:

### 3.1 Reach
- How many users will this affect in a given timeframe (e.g., per quarter)?

### 3.2 Impact
- How much will this increase the specific goal? 
  - 3 = Massive impact
  - 2 = High impact
  - 1 = Medium impact
  - 0.5 = Low impact

### 3.3 Confidence
- How sure are we about our estimates for Reach and Impact?
  - 100% = High confidence
  - 80% = Medium confidence
  - 50% = Low confidence (needs more research)

### 3.4 Effort
- How many "person-months" will this take? (Estimated by Engineering).

### 3.5 RICE Score Calculation
`(Reach * Impact * Confidence) / Effort = RICE Score`

Higher scores indicate higher priority.

## 4. Decision Making

### 4.1 The Roadmap Committee
- Consists of Head of Product, CTO, and VP of Sales/CS.
- Meets monthly to finalize priorities based on RICE scores and strategic alignment.

### 4.2 Handling "Urgent" Requests
- Critical security issues or massive data loss bugs bypass this process and go straight to Engineering.
- "Large Customer" requests are evaluated for strategic value vs. custom work.

## 5. Feedback Loop

### 5.1 Closing the Loop
- Submitters are notified when a feature they requested:
  - Is planned for the roadmap.
  - Is released.
- If a request is declined, a brief explanation is provided where possible.
