# Product Roadmap and Strategy Process

Last Updated: May 12, 2026

## 1. Product Vision & Strategy

### 1.1 Product Vision
To build the most intuitive and powerful [Insert Core Value Prop] for our users.

### 1.2 Strategic Pillars
- **Innovation**: Leading with cutting-edge AI and tech.
- **Reliability**: Building trust through 99.99% uptime.
- **User-Centricity**: Design driven by actual user needs.
- **Scalability**: Supporting growth from startup to enterprise.

## 2. Roadmap Development

### 2.1 Planning Horizon
- **Now (0-3 Months)**: High certainty, committed development.
- **Next (3-6 Months)**: Planned features, subject to refinement.
- **Later (6-12 Months)**: Strategic direction, discovery stage.

### 2.2 Quarterly Planning Cycle
1. **Month 1 (Review)**: Analyze performance of previous quarter.
2. **Month 2 (Discovery)**: Gather feedback, research new opportunities.
3. **Month 3 (Commitment)**: Define and approve the next quarter's roadmap.

## 3. Roadmap Inputs

### 3.1 Feedback Channels
- **Sales & CS**: Direct feedback from prospects and customers.
- **Support**: Common pain points and bug trends.
- **Data Analytics**: Usage patterns and drop-off points.
- **Market Research**: Competitor moves and industry trends.

### 3.2 Strategic Alignment
- Every roadmap item must link back to at least one Strategic Pillar.
- Resource allocation:
  - 60% Core Features / Improvements.
  - 20% Innovation / New Bets.
  - 20% Technical Debt / Infrastructure.

## 4. Roadmapping Tools

### 4.1 Single Source of Truth
- **Productboard/Linear**: For roadmap visualization and tracking.
- **Confluence/Notion**: For detailed PRDs (Product Requirement Documents).

## 5. Communication

### 5.1 Internal Updates
- Monthly "Product Pulse" meeting for the whole company.
- Weekly syncs between Product, Engineering, and Design.

### 5.2 External Roadmap
- A public-facing version of the roadmap (simplified) is shared with customers to manage expectations and gather early interest.
