# Sales Processes and Playbook

Last Updated: May 12, 2026

## 1. Sales Philosophy

### 1.1 Customer-Centric Approach
- Focus on solving customer problems rather than just selling features.
- Build long-term relationships based on trust and value.
- Maintain transparency in all interactions.

### 1.2 Solution Selling
- Understand the customer's business environment.
- Identify pain points and quantify the impact.
- Present Lutervyn solutions as a strategic investment.

## 2. Lead Generation & Prospecting

### 2.1 Lead Sources
- Inbound: Marketing-generated leads, website inquiries.
- Outbound: Targeted outreach, social selling, networking.
- Referrals: Existing customer recommendations.
- Partners: Collaborative lead sharing.

### 2.2 Prospecting Standards
- Research: Understand the prospect's company and role before contact.
- Personalization: Tailor messages to specific needs.
- Consistency: Maintain a regular outreach cadence.
- CRM Hygiene: Log all prospecting activities in the CRM.

## 3. The Sales Process

### 3.1 Qualification (Discovery)
- **Goal**: Determine if the prospect is a good fit.
- **Criteria (BANT)**: 
  - Budget: Do they have the funds?
  - Authority: Who is the decision-maker?
  - Need: What is the core problem?
  - Timeline: When do they need a solution?
- **Outcome**: Qualified Lead or Disqualified.

### 3.2 Discovery & Needs Analysis
- **Goal**: Deep dive into the customer's requirements.
- **Activities**: 
  - Conduct discovery calls/meetings.
  - Map technical and business requirements.
  - Identify key stakeholders and influencers.
- **Outcome**: Clear understanding of the solution required.

### 3.3 Solution Presentation & Demo
- **Goal**: Show how Lutervyn meets their needs.
- **Activities**: 
  - Tailored product demonstrations.
  - Case study presentations.
  - Technical deep dives (if necessary).
- **Outcome**: Prospect understands the value proposition.

### 3.4 Proposal & Business Case
- **Goal**: Formalize the offer.
- **Activities**: 
  - Prepare detailed proposals.
  - Build a ROI/Business case document.
  - Review pricing and terms.
- **Outcome**: Proposal submitted for review.

### 3.5 Negotiation & Closing
- **Goal**: Finalize the agreement.
- **Activities**: 
  - Address objections and concerns.
  - Negotiate final terms and pricing.
  - Legal and security review coordination.
- **Outcome**: Signed contract (Closed-Won).

### 3.6 Handover to Customer Success
- **Goal**: Ensure a smooth transition.
- **Activities**: 
  - Internal briefing with Customer Success Manager (CSM).
  - Introduction of the CSM to the customer.
  - Review of promised outcomes and timelines.
- **Outcome**: Customer successfully onboarded.

## 4. Sales Methodologies

### 4.1 SPIN Selling
- **S**ituation: Establish context.
- **P**roblem: Identify pain points.
- **I**mplication: Explore the consequences of the problem.
- **N**eed-Payoff: Highlight the value of the solution.

### 4.2 Challenger Sale
- Teach: Provide unique perspectives.
- Tailor: Resonate with specific customer needs.
- Take Control: Manage the tension of the sale.

## 5. CRM & Reporting

### 5.1 Pipeline Management
- Keep deal stages updated daily.
- Provide accurate close dates and probability.
- Note "Next Steps" for every active deal.

### 5.2 Key Metrics (KPIs)
- Conversion Rates: Lead to Opportunity, Opportunity to Won.
- Average Deal Size: Total value / number of deals.
- Sales Cycle Length: Average time from lead to close.
- Pipeline Velocity: Speed at which deals move through stages.

## 6. Ethics & Compliance

### 6.1 Representation
- Never over-promise features or timelines.
- Do not disparage competitors; focus on our strengths.
- Adhere to the Lutervyn Code of Conduct.

### 6.2 Data Privacy
- Handle prospect data according to GDPR/CCPA.
- Ensure all outreach is compliant with anti-spam laws.
