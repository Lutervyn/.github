# Sales Compensation Plan

Last Updated: May 12, 2026

## 1. Plan Overview

### 1.1 Purpose
This plan outlines the structure of compensation for the sales organization to align individual performance with company revenue goals.

### 1.2 Eligibility
- Full-time Sales roles: Account Executives (AE), Sales Development Representatives (SDR), Account Managers (AM).
- Requires a signed individual quota agreement.

## 2. Compensation Structure

### 2.1 Base Salary
- Fixed monthly payment based on role and experience.
- Paid twice monthly.

### 2.2 Variable Compensation (Commission)
- Performance-based pay tied to revenue generation or meeting targets.
- Paid monthly, one month in arrears.

### 2.3 On-Target Earnings (OTE)
- Total potential compensation (Base + Variable) if 100% of quota is achieved.

## 3. Account Executive (AE) Plan

### 3.1 Quota Types
- New Business ARR (Annual Recurring Revenue).
- Expansion ARR (Upsells to existing customers).

### 3.2 Commission Rates
- Base Rate: X% of contract value (Standard is 10%).
- Accelerators:
  - 101% - 120% of Quota: 1.5x Base Rate.
  - 121%+ of Quota: 2x Base Rate.
- De-accelerators: If less than 50% of quota is met, commission rates may be reduced.

### 3.3 Clawbacks
- If a customer cancels within the first 3 months (90 days), the commission paid for that deal will be deducted from future commissions.

## 4. Sales Development Representative (SDR) Plan

### 4.1 Performance Metrics
- Qualified Meetings Held (SQLs).
- Pipeline Contribution ($ Value of opportunities created).

### 4.2 Payout Structure
- Flat rate per SQL meeting.
- Bonus for opportunities that reach "Proposal" stage.

## 5. Payment Terms

### 5.1 Timing
- Commissions are calculated at the end of each calendar month.
- Payments are disbursed on the 15th of the following month.

### 5.2 Documentation
- Commissions are paid based on signed contracts and received payments (or "Signed Contract" depending on specific role terms).

## 6. Bonuses and Incentives (SPIFFs)

### 6.1 Discretionary SPIFFs
- Short-term incentives for specific goals (e.g., "Most deals closed in Q3", "New industry vertical penetration").
- Announced by sales leadership as needed.

## 7. Administrative Provisions

### 7.1 Plan Changes
- Lutervyn reserves the right to modify the compensation plan with 30 days' notice.
- Changes apply to future sales, not retroactive.

### 7.2 Disputes
- Any disputes regarding commission calculations must be submitted to Sales Ops within 5 business days of the commission statement.
