# Customer Success Playbook

Last Updated: May 12, 2026

## 1. Customer Success Mission

### 1.1 Our Goal
To ensure every Lutervyn customer achieves their desired business outcomes and realizes maximum value from our products.

### 1.2 Key Objectives
- Maximize product adoption and engagement.
- Reduce churn and increase retention.
- Drive expansion and upsell opportunities.
- Cultivate customer advocates.

## 2. Customer Journey Stages

### 2.1 Onboarding (Days 0-90)
- **Goal**: Initial value realization ("First Value").
- **Activities**:
  - Kickoff meeting.
  - Setup and configuration support.
  - User training.
  - Success criteria definition.

### 2.2 Adoption (Days 90-180)
- **Goal**: Deep integration into customer workflows.
- **Activities**:
  - Regular check-ins (Monthly/Bi-monthly).
  - Health checks.
  - Feature highlighting based on use cases.

### 2.3 Optimization (Ongoing)
- **Goal**: Continuous value and efficiency gains.
- **Activities**:
  - Strategic Business Reviews (SBR).
  - Best practice sharing.
  - Advanced feature training.

### 2.4 Renewal & Expansion
- **Goal**: Secure long-term partnership and grow account value.
- **Activities**:
  - Renewal negotiation (90 days prior).
  - Identifying upsell/cross-sell opportunities.

## 3. Engagement Model

### 3.1 High Touch (Strategic Accounts)
- Dedicated CSM.
- Weekly or bi-weekly syncs.
- Quarterly Executive Business Reviews (EBR).
- Custom success plans.

### 3.2 Low Touch (Mid-Market)
- Shared CSM resources.
- Monthly automated health reports.
- Bi-annual business reviews.
- Priority access to webinars and training.

### 3.3 Tech Touch (SMB)
- Automated onboarding sequences.
- Self-service help center and community.
- Trigger-based email outreach.

## 4. Health Scoring

### 4.1 Metrics
- **Product Usage**: Frequency, depth, and breadth of feature use.
- **Engagement**: Attendance at meetings, response to emails.
- **Support Volume**: Number and severity of support tickets.
- **NPS/CSAT**: Direct customer feedback scores.

### 4.2 Health Status
- **Green (Healthy)**: High engagement, positive feedback, regular usage.
- **Yellow (At-Risk)**: Declining usage, slow response times, multiple support issues.
- **Red (Critical)**: Negative sentiment, zero usage, executive turnover.

## 5. Risk Mitigation & Escalation

### 5.1 Red Account Process
- Immediate outreach to primary contact.
- Executive sponsor involvement.
- Creation of a Get-Well Plan with clear milestones.
- Weekly internal status updates.

### 5.2 Churn Analysis
- Conduct "Post-Mortem" for all churned accounts.
- Identify root causes (Product, Service, Price, Competitor).
- Share findings with Product and Sales teams.

## 6. Success Metrics (CS KPIs)

- **Net Revenue Retention (NRR)**: Revenue from existing customers including expansion.
- **Gross Revenue Retention (GRR)**: Revenue retained before expansion.
- **Churn Rate**: Percentage of customers/revenue lost.
- **Time to First Value (TTFV)**: Days from signup to first success milestone.
