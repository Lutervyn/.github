# Risk Registry

Last Updated: May 12, 2024

## 1. Purpose

Centralized repository for all identified risks to business operations.

## 2. Risk Categories

### 2.1 Cyber Security
- Data breach
- Ransomware
- APT attacks
- Insider threats
- Supply chain

### 2.2 Operational
- System failure
- Disaster/recovery
- Key person dependency
- Vendor failure
- Process failure

### 2.3 Compliance
- Regulatory change
- Legal violation
- Audit failure
- Policy breach
- Data protection

### 2.4 Business
- Competitive threat
- Market change
- Customer loss
- Revenue impact
- Reputation damage

## 3. Risk Assessment

### 3.1 Probability

- Very High (>50%)
- High (21-50%)
- Medium (6-20%)
- Low (1-5%)
- Very Low (<1%)

### 3.2 Impact

- Critical (>$1M loss)
- High ($500K-$1M)
- Medium ($100K-$500K)
- Low ($10K-$100K)
- Minimal (<$10K)

### 3.3 Risk Score

- Score = Probability × Impact
- 1-5: Minimal
- 6-25: Low
- 26-100: Medium
- 101-500: High
- >500: Critical

## 4. Mitigation Strategies

### 4.1 Avoidance
- Eliminate risk
- Stop activity
- Avoid business line
- Change strategy

### 4.2 Reduction
- Control measures
- Preventive actions
- Detection systems
- Response planning

### 4.3 Sharing
- Transfer to insurer
- Outsource activity
- Partner arrangement
- Insurance coverage

### 4.4 Acceptance
- Monitor closely
- Accept consequence
- Contingency planning
- Reserve funding

## 5. Monitoring

- Risk trends tracked
- New risks identified
- Mitigation effectiveness

- Executive reporting
