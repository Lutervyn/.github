# Mentorship Program

Last Updated: May 12, 2026

## 1. Overview

### 1.1 Purpose
To foster a culture of continuous learning and growth by connecting experienced employees (Mentors) with those seeking to develop specific skills or knowledge (Mentees).

## 2. Program Structure

### 2.1 Voluntary Participation
- Both mentors and mentees participate on a voluntary basis.
- Open to all full-time employees after 3 months of tenure.

### 2.2 Matching Process
- **Bi-Annual Matching**: Cycles start in January and July.
- **Criteria**: Based on skill interests, career goals, and personality fit.
- **Cross-Functional**: We encourage matching people from different departments to broaden perspectives.

## 3. Expectations

### 3.1 Time Commitment
- A minimum of one 45-minute meeting per month.
- Initial commitment of 6 months.

### 3.2 For the Mentor
- Listen and provide constructive feedback.
- Share experiences and lessons learned.
- Help the mentee navigate the company culture.
- **Note**: A mentor is NOT a manager; discussions are confidential.

### 3.3 For the Mentee
- Drive the agenda; come prepared with specific questions or goals.
- Be open to feedback and new ideas.
- Follow up on agreed-upon action items.

## 4. Program Support

- **Kickoff Workshop**: A 1-hour session for new pairs on how to have effective mentor meetings.
- **Resource Library**: Curated list of books, articles, and templates for mentorship.
- **Budget**: $50/month per pair for coffee or lunch (reimbursable).

## 5. Feedback and Evaluation

- At the end of each 6-month cycle, both parties complete a brief survey to help us improve the program.
