# Skills Training and Certification

Last Updated: May 12, 2026

## 1. Overview

To maintain our competitive edge, Lutervyn encourages and supports employees in continuously upgrading their skills through formal training and certifications.

## 2. Learning & Development (L&D) Budget

- Every full-time employee has an annual L&D budget of **$1,000**.
- **Approved Uses**:
  - Professional conferences and workshops.
  - Online courses (Udemy, Coursera, Pluralsight).
  - Professional certification exams.
  - Job-related books and subscriptions.

## 3. Approval Process

1. Discuss the training or certification with your manager during your 1-on-1.
2. If it aligns with your career goals and business needs, submit a request via the Finance system.
3. Once approved, you can purchase the training and submit the receipt for reimbursement.

## 4. Professional Certifications

- For certain roles, specific certifications may be required or highly encouraged (e.g., AWS Architect, PMP, CPA).
- Lutervyn will pay for the first two attempts at a certification exam.
- Time spent studying for critical certifications can be blocked out as "Focus Time" on your calendar.

## 5. Knowledge Sharing

- Employees who attend a conference or complete a major certification are encouraged to host a "Lunch & Learn" or write a brief summary in the [Internal Wiki](../knowledge-management/Internal%20Wiki%20and%20Knowledge%20Base%20Management.md) to share knowledge with the wider team.

## 6. Subscriptions

- Lutervyn provides company-wide access to several learning platforms. Check the [Software Asset Register](../it-ops/Software%20License%20Management.md) for the current list and how to request access.
