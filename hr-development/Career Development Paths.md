# Career Development Paths

Last Updated: May 12, 2026

## 1. Overview

Lutervyn is committed to providing clear and equitable paths for career growth. We recognize two primary tracks for advancement: the **Individual Contributor (IC)** track and the **Management (M)** track.

## 2. The Dual Track System

We believe that great engineers or salespeople should not be forced into management to advance their careers. Both tracks are valued equally in terms of compensation and status.

### 2.1 Individual Contributor (IC) Track
Focuses on deep technical expertise, craftsmanship, and increasing individual/project impact.
- **Example Levels**: Junior → Mid → Senior → Staff → Principal.

### 2.2 Management (M) Track
Focuses on people development, team building, strategy, and operational excellence.
- **Example Levels**: Team Lead → Manager → Director → VP → C-Level.

## 3. Leveling Framework

Each role at Lutervyn has a detailed "Leveling Guide" that outlines the expectations for:
- **Scope**: Size and complexity of projects/teams.
- **Complexity**: Difficulty of problems solved.
- **Impact**: Measurable effect on the business or product.
- **Leadership**: Ability to influence others and drive initiatives.

## 4. Internal Mobility

- **Role Changes**: Employees can apply for open positions in other departments after 6 months in their current role.
- **Track Switching**: It is possible to move between IC and Management tracks (e.g., a Staff Engineer becoming an Engineering Manager).

## 5. Promotion Process

- **Frequency**: Promotion cycles occur twice a year (linked to performance reviews).
- **Criteria**:
  - Consistent performance at the *next* level for at least 3-6 months.
  - Business need for the higher-level role.
  - Manager and peer recommendation.
- **Approval**: Promotions require approval from the Department VP and HR.

## 6. Individual Development Plans (IDP)

Every employee is encouraged to create an IDP with their manager to identify the specific skills and experiences needed to reach their next career milestone.
