# Software License Management

Last Updated: May 12, 2026

## 1. Overview

### 1.1 Purpose
To ensure that all software used at Lutervyn is legally licensed, properly tracked, and cost-effective.

### 1.2 Scope
Applies to all SaaS subscriptions, desktop software, mobile apps, and developer libraries used for business purposes.

## 2. Procurement & Approval

### 2.1 Requesting New Software
- All software requests must be submitted via the IT Portal.
- Requests are reviewed for:
  - **Security**: Compatibility with Lutervyn security standards.
  - **Legal**: Review of terms and conditions.
  - **Redundancy**: Checking if a similar tool already exists in the company.
  - **Budget**: Approval from the department head.

### 2.2 Centralized Purchasing
- To maximize volume discounts and maintain control, all software must be purchased through the IT/Procurement department.
- Personal credit card purchases for software are not reimbursable without prior IT approval.

## 3. License Tracking

### 3.1 License Inventory
- IT maintains a central **Software Asset Register** (SAR) detailing:
  - Software name and version.
  - Number of seats/licenses purchased.
  - Renewal dates.
  - Cost per license.
  - Assigned users.

### 3.2 Audit & Optimization
- IT conducts semi-annual audits of software usage.
- Unused licenses will be reclaimed and reassigned or cancelled to optimize costs.

## 4. Compliance & Usage

### 4.1 Prohibited Software
- Installation of unlicensed (pirated) software is strictly prohibited and is grounds for disciplinary action.
- Open-source software must be reviewed by the Engineering team for license compatibility (e.g., GPL vs. MIT).

### 4.2 License Transfer
- Licenses are assigned to roles or individuals and may not be shared (unless the license specifically allows for it, e.g., team-based accounts).
- When an employee leaves, their licenses must be revoked and returned to the pool immediately.

## 5. Renewals

- IT is responsible for tracking renewal dates and initiating the renewal process 60 days in advance with the relevant department head.
