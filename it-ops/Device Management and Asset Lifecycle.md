# Device Management and Asset Lifecycle

Last Updated: May 12, 2026

## 1. Overview

### 1.1 Purpose
To define the end-to-end management of hardware assets, from procurement to secure disposal, ensuring security and operational efficiency.

### 1.2 Scope
Includes laptops, mobile devices, servers, networking equipment, and peripheral hardware.

## 2. Procurement & Provisioning

### 2.1 Standard Hardware Specs
- IT maintains a list of standard hardware configurations to ensure compatibility and ease of support.
- Requests for non-standard hardware require CTO approval.

### 2.2 Provisioning (The "Golden Image")
- All company laptops are provisioned with a standard security stack:
  - Endpoint Protection (EDR).
  - Full Disk Encryption (FileVault/BitLocker).
  - Mobile Device Management (MDM) profile (e.g., Jamf/InTune).
  - Pre-installed productivity and security software.

## 3. Deployment & Tracking

### 3.1 Inventory Management
- Every device is assigned a unique asset tag.
- Device details (Serial Number, Specs, User, Location) are recorded in the **Asset Management Database**.

### 3.2 User Responsibility
- Employees are responsible for the physical security of their devices.
- Devices should never be left unattended in public spaces.

## 4. Maintenance & Support

### 4.1 Operating System Updates
- MDM policies enforce critical OS and security updates.
- Employees must restart their machines when prompted to apply updates.

### 4.2 Hardware Refresh Cycle
- Laptops: Every 3 years.
- Mobile Devices: Every 2 years.
- High-performance workstations: As needed based on project requirements.

## 5. Decommissioning & Disposal

### 5.1 Return of Equipment
- Upon termination of employment or hardware refresh, all equipment must be returned to IT within 48 hours.

### 5.2 Data Sanitization
- All storage media must be securely wiped using industry-standard tools (e.g., NIST 800-88 compliant).
- IT verifies and logs the successful wiping of every retired device.

### 5.3 Secure Disposal/Recycling
- Hardware that cannot be repurposed or sold is sent to a certified e-waste recycler.
- A "Certificate of Destruction" is required for all scrapped storage media.
