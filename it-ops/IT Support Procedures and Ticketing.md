# IT Support Procedures and Ticketing

Last Updated: May 12, 2026

## 1. Overview

### 1.1 Purpose
To provide a clear and efficient process for employees to request technical assistance and for the IT team to resolve issues systematically.

### 1.2 Scope
Applies to all Lutervyn employees and contractors requesting support for company-provided hardware, software, and network services.

## 2. Requesting Support

### 2.1 Standard Support Request
- **Method**: Submit a ticket via the **Lutervyn IT Portal** or email support@lutervyn.com.
- **Information Required**:
  - Clear description of the issue.
  - Urgency/Impact (e.g., "Cannot work" vs. "Minor annoyance").
  - Hardware/Software details (e.g., Laptop ID, App version).
  - Screenshots or error codes if applicable.

### 2.2 Urgent / Emergency Support
- **Method**: Post in the #it-emergency Slack channel or call the IT Hotline.
- **Definition of Urgent**:
  - System-wide outages.
  - Security breaches.
  - Executive-level blockers.
  - New hire setup issues on Day 1.

## 3. Ticketing Workflow

### 3.1 Triage & Priority
The IT team reviews new tickets within 2 hours of submission and assigns a priority:
- **P1 (Critical)**: Resolution goal: 4 hours. (e.g., Major system down).
- **P2 (High)**: Resolution goal: 1 business day. (e.g., Individual cannot work).
- **P3 (Medium)**: Resolution goal: 3 business days. (e.g., Functional glitch with workaround).
- **P4 (Low)**: Resolution goal: 5+ business days. (e.g., Feature requests, non-critical updates).

### 3.2 Resolution & Closure
1. **In Progress**: IT technician works on the issue and provides updates via the ticket.
2. **Resolved**: IT provides the solution. The employee is asked to verify the fix.
3. **Closed**: Ticket is closed automatically 48 hours after being marked "Resolved" if no further comments are made.

## 4. Hardware Support

### 4.1 On-Site Support
- Available at the main office during business hours (9:00 AM - 5:00 PM).
- Walk-ins are accepted for urgent issues, but tickets are preferred.

### 4.2 Remote Support
- IT may request permission to access your machine via **remote desktop software** (e.g., TeamViewer, ScreenConnect).
- Always ensure sensitive personal information is hidden before granting access.

## 5. Escalation Path

If a ticket is not being addressed within the target resolution time:
1. Contact the assigned technician via the ticket.
2. Escalate to the IT Manager.
3. Escalate to the CTO for critical unresolved P1 issues.
