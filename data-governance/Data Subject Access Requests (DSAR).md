# Data Subject Access Requests (DSAR)

Last Updated: May 12, 2026

## 1. Overview

### 1.1 Purpose
To define the process for handling requests from individuals who wish to exercise their rights over their personal data, as required by GDPR, CCPA, and other privacy laws.

### 1.2 Scope
Applies to all personal data held by Lutervyn regarding customers, employees, and third parties.

## 2. Types of Requests

Individuals (Data Subjects) have the right to request:
- **Access**: A copy of the personal data held about them.
- **Correction**: Rectification of inaccurate data.
- **Deletion**: Removal of their personal data.
- **Portability**: Data provided in a structured, machine-readable format.
- **Restriction/Objection**: Stopping the processing of their data.

## 3. The DSAR Process

### 3.1 Submission
- Requests should be sent to privacy@lutervyn.com.
- If a request is received via other channels (Social Media, Support ticket), it must be forwarded to the Privacy team within 24 hours.

### 3.2 Identity Verification
- Before processing, Lutervyn must verify the identity of the requester.
- For customers: Verification via logged-in account or email verification.
- For others: Requesting a copy of government ID (stored only for verification and deleted immediately after).

### 3.3 Gathering Data
- The Privacy team coordinates with Department Heads (Engineering, HR, Sales) to identify all locations where the requester's data is stored.
- Data is extracted and reviewed to ensure it does not include information about other individuals (Redaction).

### 3.4 Responding
- The final response and/or data must be sent to the requester within **30 days** of the initial request.
- If the request is complex, the period may be extended by an additional 60 days, with notice provided to the requester within the first 30 days.

## 4. Grounds for Refusal

Lutervyn may refuse a DSAR if:
- The request is "manifestly unfounded or excessive" (e.g., repetitive requests).
- Providing the data would infringe on the rights and freedoms of others.
- The data is protected by legal privilege.

## 5. Fees

- Requests are processed **free of charge**.
- A reasonable fee may be charged for administrative costs if the requester asks for further copies of the same information.
