- Referrals: 10% of leads

**Lead Qualification**
- Fit: company fit assessment
- Need: identified need
- Budget: budget confirmed
- Timeline: decision timeline
- Authority: decision authority

### 6.2 Sales Process

**Pipeline Stages**
1. Lead: initial inquiry
2. Qualified: sales-qualified lead
3. Engaged: evaluation phase
4. Proposal: proposal provided
5. Negotiation: contract negotiation
6. Closed: customer signed

**Sales Cycle**
- Length: 60 days average
- Touchpoints: 5-7 touches
- Decision makers: 2-3 stakeholders
- Evaluation: 30 day trial

### 6.3 Customer Onboarding

**Onboarding Program**
- Day 1: welcome call + training
- Week 1: system setup + basic training
- Week 2-4: hands-on training
- Month 2: advanced training
- Month 3: optimization review

**Success Metrics**
- Time to value: 30 days
- Early adoption: 80% of features
- Satisfaction: 90% satisfaction
- Retention: 90%+ year 1 retention
