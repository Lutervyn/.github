# Brand Guidelines

Last Updated: May 12, 2026

## 1. Brand Overview

### 1.1 Brand Vision

Lutervyn is a forward-thinking technology company committed to creating transformative solutions that empower organizations to achieve their goals through ethical innovation and human-centered design.

### 1.2 Brand Mission

To build technology that matters—creating products and services that solve real problems, drive meaningful change, and improve how organizations work.

### 1.3 Brand Values

- **Innovation**: Pioneering new solutions
- **Integrity**: Honest and ethical
- **Excellence**: High-quality execution
- **Inclusivity**: Diverse perspectives
- **Impact**: Creating positive change

## 2. Visual Identity

### 2.1 Logo

**Primary Logo**
- Format: horizontal lockup
- File: EPS, PNG, SVG
- Versions: color, black, white
- Minimum size: 1 inch width
- Spacing: clear space around logo
- Never: never modify or distort

**Logo Variations**
- Full: company name + symbol
- Icon only: symbol alone
- Wordmark: name only
- Vertical: stacked version

**Don'ts**
- No rotation: keep level
- No skewing: maintain proportions
- No effects: no shadows or gradients
- No colors: stick to approved colors
- No fonts: don't change typography

### 2.2 Color Palette

**Primary Colors**
- Lutervyn Blue: #1E40AF (Hex)
  - RGB: 30, 64, 175
  - CMYK: 100, 64, 0, 31
  - Used: primary brand color

- White: #FFFFFF
  - Used: backgrounds and text
  - Contrast: ensure readability

- Dark Gray: #1F2937
  - RGB: 31, 41, 55
  - Used: text and headers

**Secondary Colors**
- Accent Green: #10B981
  - Used: highlights and CTAs
  - Purpose: positive action

- Accent Orange: #F97316
  - Used: warnings and important
  - Purpose: draw attention

**Color Usage**
- Primary: 70% of design
- Secondary: 20% of design
- Accent: 10% of design
- Contrast: WCAG AA minimum

### 2.3 Typography

**Primary Font: Inter**
- Usage: headings and body
- License: open source
- Weights: Regular (400), Bold (700)
- Download: Google Fonts

**Heading Sizes**
- H1: 48px, bold, letter-spacing -1px
- H2: 36px, bold
- H3: 24px, bold
- H4: 20px, semi-bold

**Body Text**
- Size: 16px regular
- Line-height: 1.5
- Color: #1F2937 (dark gray)
- Letter-spacing: normal

**Font Don'ts**
- No sans serif: stick to approved fonts
- No decorative: stay professional
- No small caps: use standard case
- No less than 12px: readability

### 2.4 Imagery

**Photography Style**
- Professional: high-quality photos
- People: diverse representation
- Settings: office and outdoor
- Tone: professional but approachable
- Editing: consistent color grading

**Photo Guidelines**
- People: authentic, diverse
- Diversity: gender, race, age
- Authentic: avoid cheesy stock photos
- Color: consistent color treatment
- Consent: proper consent and releases

**Illustration Style**
- Modern: contemporary illustrations
- Consistent: consistent illustration style
- Color: use brand color palette
- Accessibility: include alt text
- Purpose: serve a clear purpose

### 2.5 Iconography

**Icon Style**
- Weight: 2px stroke weight
- Roundedness: 2px corner radius
- Size: 24px base size
- Color: use brand colors
- Consistency: consistent style

**Icon Usage**
- Metaphor: clear meaning
- Labeling: always labeled
- Color: high contrast for readability
- Stroke: consistent stroke weight

## 3. Voice & Messaging

### 3.1 Brand Voice

**Tone Characteristics**
- Professional: credible and authoritative
- Approachable: friendly and accessible
- Clear: simple and direct
- Confident: sure of our position
- Honest: transparent and truthful

### 3.2 Writing Principles

**Be Clear**
- Simple: use simple language
- Direct: get to the point
- Active: use active voice
- Short: keep sentences short

**Be Helpful**
- Focus: on customer benefit
- Jargon: minimize technical jargon
- Examples: provide concrete examples
- Action: clear next steps

**Be Honest**
- Truth: truthful claims
- Accuracy: factually accurate
- Limitations: acknowledge limitations
- Mistakes: own and fix errors

### 3.3 Key Messages

**Company**
- Message: "Transforming how organizations work"
- Support: through innovative technology
- Benefit: efficiency, collaboration, impact

**Products**
- Message: "Purpose-built for your success"
- Support: integrated, intuitive, powerful
- Benefit: saves time, reduces errors, improves outcomes

**Customers**
- Message: "Trusted by leading organizations"
- Support: enterprise customers, proven ROI
- Benefit: confidence, results, growth

## 4. Logo Usage

### 4.1 Logo Placement

**Clear Space**
- Minimum: equal to lowercase 'x' height
- Breathing: adequate white space
- Distance: from other elements
- Placement: use clear space guidelines

**Background**
- Solid: solid color backgrounds
- Image: no placement on complex backgrounds
- Color: adequate contrast required
- Watermark: lighter when used as watermark

### 4.2 Logo Sizing

**Minimum Size**
- Web: 100px width
- Print: 0.5 inch width
- Favicon: 32x32 pixels
- Smaller: may become illegible

**Maximum Size**
- No maximum: scale as needed
- Quality: maintain resolution
- Proportion: maintain aspect ratio

## 5. Business Card & Stationery

### 5.1 Business Card

**Dimensions**
- Size: 3.5" x 2"
- Orientation: horizontal
- Paper: 2-sided printing
- Finish: matte or gloss

**Layout**
- Logo: top left corner
- Name: bold, 11pt
- Title: 9pt, gray
- Contact: left-aligned
- Reverse: clean back side

### 5.2 Letterhead

**Specifications**
- Size: 8.5" x 11"
- Paper: white, 20lb
- Logo: top left, 1" width
- Address: footer, 8pt
- Color: single color or full color

### 5.3 Email Signature

**Format**
```
Name
Title
Company
Email: email@lutervyn.com
Phone: (555) 555-5555
Website: www.lutervyn.com
```

**Style**
- Font: Inter, 11pt
- Color: dark gray text
- Logo: small company logo
- Links: blue underlined
