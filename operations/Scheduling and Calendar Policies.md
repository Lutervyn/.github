# Scheduling and Calendar Policies

Last Updated: May 12, 2026

## 1. Calendar Transparency

### 1.1 Open Access
- All employee calendars should be visible (free/busy status) to everyone within the company.
- Details of meetings may be marked "Private" for sensitive topics (e.g., performance reviews, health appointments).

### 1.2 Working Hours
- Set your "Working Hours" in your calendar (e.g., Google Calendar/Outlook) to prevent meetings from being scheduled during your personal time.

## 2. Meeting Etiquette

### 2.1 Scheduling
- Check the attendee's calendar before sending an invite.
- Provide at least 24 hours' notice for most meetings.
- Include a clear agenda and any pre-reading materials in the invite.

### 2.2 Duration
- Default meeting duration should be 25 or 50 minutes (to allow for "speedy meetings" and transitions).
- Avoid hour-long meetings if the topic can be covered in less time.

### 2.3 Responses
- Respond to meeting invites (Yes, No, Maybe) within 4 hours of receipt.
- If declining, provide a brief reason or suggest an alternative time.

## 3. "No Meeting" Times

### 3.1 Focus Blocks
- Employees are encouraged to block out 2-hour "Focus Time" slots on their calendars daily.
- Respect "Focus Time" and avoid scheduling meetings over these blocks unless urgent.

### 3.2 No-Meeting Wednesdays
- To support deep work, Wednesdays are designated as "No-Meeting Days" across the company.
- Internal meetings are discouraged. Client-facing meetings are exempt but should be minimized.

## 4. Time Zone Awareness

- When scheduling with global team members, use a time zone converter.
- Aim for "Golden Hours" (times that overlap reasonably for both parties).
- Alternate meeting times if one party is always working late/early.

## 5. Out of Office (OOO)

### 5.1 OOO Calendar Entry
- Create an "Out of Office" entry for any absence longer than 4 hours.
- Set your Slack status to "OOO" and specify your return date.

### 5.2 Coverage
- For absences longer than 2 days, include the name of your backup/coverage person in your OOO message and calendar entry.
