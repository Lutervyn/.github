# Purchasing and Ordering Procedures

Last Updated: May 12, 2026

## 1. Overview

### 1.1 Purpose
To ensure that all company purchases are authorized, cost-effective, and properly documented.

### 1.2 Scope
Applies to all software, hardware, office supplies, and professional services.

## 2. Procurement Thresholds

- **Level 1 (Up to $500)**: Department Head approval required. Can be purchased via corporate credit card or personal card for reimbursement.
- **Level 2 ($501 - $5,000)**: Requires at least 2 quotes (where possible) and VP/C-Level approval.
- **Level 3 (Over $5,000)**: Requires at least 3 formal quotes, a signed contract (see [Contract Management](../legal/Contract%20Management.md)), and CFO/CEO approval.

## 3. The Ordering Process

### 3.1 Requisition
1. Identify the need.
2. Complete a Purchase Request (PR) form in the finance system.
3. Attach quotes and business justification.

### 3.2 Approval
- The finance system automatically routes the PR to the appropriate approvers based on the thresholds in Section 2.

### 3.3 Purchase Order (PO)
- Once approved, Finance issues a formal PO to the vendor.
- **Note**: No commitments should be made to vendors until a PO or approved contract is in place.

## 4. Software & Subscriptions (SaaS)

### 4.1 Security & IT Review
- All new software must be reviewed by IT and Security for compatibility and data privacy before purchase.

### 4.2 Centralization
- Whenever possible, software should be purchased through the centralized IT procurement channel to take advantage of volume discounts and ensure proper license management.

## 5. Receiving and Invoicing

### 5.1 Verification
- Upon receipt of goods or services, the requestor must verify that the order is complete and satisfactory.

### 5.2 Invoice Processing
- Invoices should be sent directly to accounting@lutervyn.com.
- Requestors must "match" the invoice to the PO in the finance system to trigger payment.

## 6. Corporate Credit Cards

- Cards are issued to select employees based on business need.
- Receipts must be uploaded to the expense system within 30 days.
- Personal use is strictly prohibited.
