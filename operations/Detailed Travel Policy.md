# Detailed Travel Policy

Last Updated: May 12, 2026

## 1. Introduction

### 1.1 Purpose
This policy provides detailed guidelines for business travel to ensure employee safety, cost-efficiency, and consistency across Lutervyn.

### 1.2 Scope
Applies to all employees traveling on behalf of the company.

## 2. Travel Planning & Booking

### 2.1 Pre-Approval
- All travel must be approved by the Department Head at least 14 days in advance (domestic) or 30 days (international).
- Approval is obtained via the Travel Authorization Form (TAF) in the HR portal.

### 2.2 Booking Method
- All flights, hotels, and car rentals must be booked through the **Lutervyn Travel Portal (TripActions/Navan)**.
- Booking outside the portal is only allowed with prior written approval from Finance.

## 3. Air Travel

### 3.1 Class of Service
- **Domestic (Under 6 hours)**: Economy / Coach.
- **International (Over 6 hours)**: Premium Economy (subject to budget).
- **Executive (VP+)**: Business Class allowed for flights over 8 hours.

### 3.2 Upgrades
- Employees may use personal miles or funds to upgrade their class of service.
- Company will only reimburse the cost of the lowest available economy fare at the time of booking.

## 4. Lodging

### 4.1 Preferred Hotels
- Use Lutervyn's preferred hotel partners listed in the travel portal.
- If a preferred hotel is unavailable, choose a mid-range hotel (3-4 stars).

### 4.2 Room Sharing
- Employees are not required to share rooms.

## 5. Ground Transportation

### 5.1 Car Rental
- Rental cars should only be used when public transit or rideshare is not cost-effective.
- Standard or Mid-size cars only.
- Refuel the car before returning; "Pre-paid fuel" options are not reimbursable.

### 5.2 Personal Vehicle Use
- Reimbursable at the standard IRS mileage rate (or local equivalent).
- Tolls and parking are reimbursable; traffic violations and fuel for personal vehicles are not.

## 6. Safety and Security

### 6.1 Travel Insurance
- Lutervyn provides comprehensive business travel insurance for all employees.
- Coverage includes medical emergencies, trip cancellation, and emergency evacuation.

### 6.2 Duty of Care
- Ensure your contact information and emergency contact details are updated in the HR portal before traveling.
- Use the "Check-In" feature in the travel app upon arrival at your destination.

## 7. International Travel

### 7.1 Visas and Passports
- Company reimburses the cost of business visas and passport renewals if required for a specific trip.
- It is the employee's responsibility to ensure they have valid travel documents.

### 7.2 Health and Vaccinations
- Required vaccinations for business travel are fully reimbursable.
