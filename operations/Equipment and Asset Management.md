# Equipment and Asset Management

Last Updated: May 12, 2026

## 1. Asset Ownership

### 1.1 Company Property
- All equipment provided to employees (laptops, phones, monitors, etc.) remains the property of Lutervyn.
- Assets are for business use and must be returned upon termination of employment.

## 2. Issuance and Tracking

### 2.1 Asset Tagging
- All hardware assets over $100 value will be tagged with a unique Lutervyn asset ID.
- Assets are logged in the central Asset Management Database (AMD).

### 2.2 Standard Issue
- **Software Engineers**: High-performance laptop, two 27" monitors, ergonomic keyboard/mouse.
- **Sales/Admin**: Standard laptop, one 27" monitor, standard peripherals.

## 3. Care and Maintenance

### 3.1 Responsible Use
- Employees must take reasonable care of company equipment.
- Software installations must comply with the [Acceptable Use Policy](../legal/Acceptable%20Use%20Policy.md).
- Laptops must be encrypted and password-protected.

### 3.2 Repairs and Replacements
- Report faulty or damaged equipment to IT Support immediately.
- Standard laptop refresh cycle is every 3 years.

## 4. Lost or Stolen Equipment

### 4.1 Reporting
- If equipment is lost or stolen, report it to IT and Security within 4 hours.
- A police report is required for all stolen items to facilitate insurance claims.

### 4.2 Liability
- Employees may be held liable for loss or damage caused by gross negligence or intentional misuse.

## 5. Disposal and Recycling

### 5.1 Secure Wiping
- All storage media must be securely wiped according to NIST standards before disposal.
- Hard drives are physically destroyed if they cannot be wiped.

### 5.2 Environmental Responsibility
- Retired equipment is recycled through certified e-waste partners or donated to local schools/non-profits after sanitization.
