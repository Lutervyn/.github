# Office and Facilities Management

Last Updated: May 12, 2026

## 1. Office Access & Security

### 1.1 Access Control
- Entry is via electronic keycard/badge.
- Sharing of badges is strictly prohibited.
- Lost badges must be reported to the Operations team immediately.

### 1.2 Visitor Policy
- All visitors must sign in at the front desk.
- Visitors must be escorted by a Lutervyn employee at all times.
- Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDA) are required for all visitors entering R&D or secure areas.

## 2. Workplace Guidelines

### 2.1 Workspace Cleanliness
- Employees are responsible for keeping their desks tidy.
- Communal areas (kitchen, breakroom) must be cleaned after use.
- Professional cleaning services are provided nightly.

### 2.2 Shared Spaces
- Conference rooms must be booked via the internal calendar.
- Meetings should not exceed the allotted time.
- Whiteboards must be erased after meetings unless marked "SAVE."

## 3. Health and Safety

### 3.1 Emergency Procedures
- Familiarize yourself with emergency exits and assembly points.
- Fire drills are conducted semi-annually.
- First aid kits are located in the kitchen and near the reception.

### 3.2 Ergonomics
- Ergonomic assessments are available upon request.
- Adjustable desks and chairs are provided to all employees.

## 4. Facilities Requests

### 4.1 Reporting Issues
- Report maintenance issues (leaks, lightbulbs, HVAC) via the #facilities Slack channel.
- Emergency issues (e.g., fire, flood) should be reported immediately to the Operations Manager.

### 4.2 Climate Control
- Office temperature is maintained at 72°F (22°C).
- Use of personal space heaters is prohibited for fire safety.

## 5. Mail and Deliveries

### 5.1 Personal Mail
- Employees are encouraged to have personal packages delivered to their homes.
- Lutervyn is not responsible for lost or damaged personal mail.

### 5.2 Business Shipments
- All outgoing business mail should be placed in the "Outbound" bin by 4:00 PM.
- Large shipments must be coordinated with the Operations team.
