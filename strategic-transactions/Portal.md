## Key Processes

### M&A Process Overview
1. **Opportunity Identification**: Deal source and initial screening
2. **Preliminary Review**: Strategic fit and valuation assessment
3. **Due Diligence**: Comprehensive financial, legal, technical review
4. **Negotiation**: Terms, pricing, and deal structure
5. **Integration Planning**: Post-close operations and timeline
6. **Closing**: Final agreements and transaction completion

### Partnership Process Overview
1. **Opportunity Evaluation**: Strategic alignment and value fit
2. **Partner Screening**: Financial stability and capabilities
3. **Agreement Development**: Contract terms and governance
4. **Launch Coordination**: Go-to-market and communication
5. **Ongoing Management**: Monthly reviews and annual evaluation
6. **Renewal Decision**: Continue, modify, or exit

## Transaction Authority

**Board Authority Required For**:
- Acquisitions (any size)
- Major partnerships (>$500K or >1 year term)
- Fundraising rounds
- Strategic collaborations
- M&A transactions

**CEO Authority**:
- Smaller vendor partnerships (<$100K)
- Strategic partnership discussions and negotiation
- Due diligence process oversight

**CFO Authority**:
- Financial terms and valuations
- Payment structure and timing
- Financial due diligence review

## Timeline Expectations

**M&A Transaction**: 3-6 months
- Week 1-2: Initial review and LOI
- Week 3-8: Due diligence (4-6 weeks)
- Week 9-16: Negotiation and deal structuring
- Week 17-24: Closing and integration planning

**Strategic Partnership**: 4-12 weeks
- Week 1-2: Partner evaluation
- Week 3-4: Negotiation
- Week 5-8: Contract finalization
- Week 9-12: Launch and onboarding

## Risk Assessment

**Deal Blockers** (usually fatal):
- Significant IP infringement risk
- Major revenue concentration (>50% from few customers)
- Significant undisclosed liabilities
- Key person departure required or planned
- Regulatory concerns or compliance gaps

**Red Flags** (require resolution):
- Accounting irregularities or questions
- Significant related party transactions
- Pending litigation or investigations
- Technology or architecture concerns
- Market or competitive headwinds

**Yellow Flags** (manageable risks):
- Customer concentration (>30%)
- Key person dependencies
- Integration complexity
- Market transition risks
- Team or culture differences
