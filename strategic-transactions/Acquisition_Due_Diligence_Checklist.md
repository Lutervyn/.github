# Acquisition Due Diligence Checklist

Last Updated: May 12, 2026

## 1. Financial Due Diligence

### 1.1 Historical Financials

- [ ] Last 3 years audited financial statements
- [ ] Last 3 years tax returns (corporate and personal if applicable)
- [ ] Monthly financial statements for last 12 months
- [ ] Year-to-date financial statements and accruals
- [ ] Trial balance and supporting schedules
- [ ] Fixed asset schedule and depreciation policies
- [ ] Accounts receivable aging and collectability analysis
- [ ] Inventory valuation and aging (if applicable)
- [ ] Accounts payable aging analysis
- [ ] Debt schedule and loan agreements

### 1.2 Revenue and Customer Analysis

- [ ] Revenue by customer (top 20 customers >80% of revenue?)
- [ ] Customer concentration risk analysis
- [ ] Customer contract terms and renewal rates
- [ ] Churn analysis (monthly/annual)
- [ ] Sales pipeline and backlog
- [ ] Revenue recognition policies
- [ ] Customer acquisition cost (CAC) analysis
- [ ] Customer lifetime value (LTV) analysis
- [ ] Recurring revenue vs. one-time revenue
- [ ] Refund and returns history

### 1.3 Expense Analysis

- [ ] Headcount and compensation by role
- [ ] Sales commissions and bonus structure
- [ ] Cost of goods sold (COGS) breakdown
- [ ] Operating expense breakdown
- [ ] Sales and marketing spend and ROI
- [ ] R&D spend and allocation
- [ ] General and administrative expenses
- [ ] Capital expenditures and schedule
- [ ] Unusual or non-recurring expenses

### 1.4 Financial Projections

- [ ] Management's 3-5 year financial projections
- [ ] Assumptions underlying projections
- [ ] Sensitivity analysis (revenue ±10-20%, expenses ±5-10%)
- [ ] Comparison of projections to actual performance
- [ ] Key metrics projected (EBITDA margin, burn rate, etc.)

### 1.5 Cash Flow Analysis

- [ ] Operating cash flow analysis
- [ ] Free cash flow calculation
- [ ] Days cash on hand (runway)
- [ ] Cash burn rate and runway months
- [ ] Planned uses of remaining cash
- [ ] Bank balances and cash available
- [ ] Outstanding loans and debt schedule

### 1.6 Financial Audit Items

- [ ] Auditor name and contact
- [ ] Any audit qualifications or concerns raised
- [ ] Related party transactions or related party debts
- [ ] Material off-balance sheet liabilities
- [ ] Contingent liabilities
- [ ] Warranty and return provisions
- [ ] Accounting policy differences vs. GAAP

## 2. Legal Due Diligence

### 2.1 Corporate Structure

- [ ] Certificate/Articles of Incorporation
- [ ] Current Bylaws
- [ ] Capitalization table and cap table history
- [ ] Equity ledger and option grants
- [ ] Outstanding warrants or conversion rights
- [ ] Equity vesting schedules and acceleration provisions
- [ ] Shareholder agreements
- [ ] Voting agreements or drag-along/tag-along rights

### 2.2 Material Contracts

- [ ] Customer contracts (especially >5% of revenue)
- [ ] Vendor agreements and supplier contracts
- [ ] Employment agreements (especially C-level)
- [ ] Contractor agreements
- [ ] Lease agreements (office, equipment, etc.)
- [ ] Loan agreements and credit facilities
- [ ] Insurance policies and coverage
- [ ] Strategic partnership or reseller agreements
- [ ] Non-competes and non-solicits with employees
- [ ] Change of control or consent requirements in contracts

### 2.3 Intellectual Property

- [ ] List of all patents and patent applications
- [ ] Patent attorneys and prosecution status
- [ ] List of trademarks and trademark applications
- [ ] Copyright registrations and claims
- [ ] Trade secrets and protection measures
- [ ] Source code escrow arrangements
- [ ] License agreements (inbound and outbound)
- [ ] Third-party IP licensing and royalty agreements
- [ ] IP assignment agreements from founders/employees
- [ ] Third-party IP infringement claims or notices

### 2.4 Compliance and Regulatory

- [ ] Licenses and permits required and current status
- [ ] Regulatory compliance certifications (ISO, SOC 2, etc.)
- [ ] Environmental compliance
- [ ] Health and safety compliance
- [ ] Privacy and data protection compliance (GDPR, CCPA, etc.)
- [ ] Export control and sanctions compliance
- [ ] Anti-corruption and FCPA compliance
- [ ] Financial services licensing (if applicable)
- [ ] Healthcare or industry-specific regulations (if applicable)

### 2.5 Litigation and Disputes

- [ ] Any pending or threatened litigation
- [ ] Settlement agreements and releases
- [ ] Warranty claims or product liability claims
- [ ] Employment disputes or claims
- [ ] Regulatory investigations or audits
- [ ] Insurance claims history

### 2.6 Environmental and Health/Safety

- [ ] Environmental reports or assessments
- [ ] Hazardous materials or waste
- [ ] OSHA inspections and citations
- [ ] Environmental compliance certifications
- [ ] Climate change or sustainability risks

---

## 3. Technical Due Diligence

### 3.1 Product and Technology

- [ ] Product architecture and technical documentation
- [ ] Technology stack and software dependencies
- [ ] Source code repository access and review (sample)
- [ ] Code quality and maintainability assessment
- [ ] Security architecture and controls
- [ ] Scalability limitations and plans
- [ ] Performance benchmarks and load testing
- [ ] Disaster recovery and backup procedures
- [ ] Data redundancy and availability measures

### 3.2 Infrastructure

- [ ] Cloud infrastructure (AWS, Azure, GCP, etc.)
- [ ] On-premises infrastructure (if any)
- [ ] Infrastructure cost and scaling strategy
- [ ] Infrastructure security and access controls
- [ ] Monitoring and alerting systems
- [ ] Infrastructure roadmap and capacity planning

### 3.3 Development Process

- [ ] Development methodology (Agile, Waterfall, etc.)
- [ ] Release cycle and deployment frequency
- [ ] Development tool stack (GitHub, Jira, etc.)
- [ ] Testing procedures (unit, integration, end-to-end)
- [ ] Code review and quality control processes
- [ ] Bug tracking and resolution procedures
- [ ] Development team skills and key person dependencies

### 3.4 Security and Compliance

- [ ] Security audits or penetration testing results
- [ ] SOC 2 or other security compliance certifications
- [ ] Data privacy and protection measures
- [ ] Encryption and key management
- [ ] Identity and access management
- [ ] Incident response plan
- [ ] Security vulnerabilities identified and remediation

### 3.5 Data and Analytics

- [ ] Data architecture and data warehouse
- [ ] Analytics and reporting capabilities
- [ ] Data quality and data lineage
- [ ] Data retention policies
- [ ] Compliance with data residency requirements

---

## 4. Operational Due Diligence

### 4.1 Management and Team

- [ ] Organizational chart
- [ ] Key person dependencies and risks
- [ ] Management team background and experience
- [ ] Employee turnover and retention rates
- [ ] Planned management changes or departures
- [ ] Advisory board and board composition
- [ ] Employee satisfaction and engagement surveys

### 4.2 Facilities and Operations

- [ ] Lease agreements and rent obligations
- [ ] Facility conditions and requirements
- [ ] Remote work policies and arrangement
- [ ] Office equipment and furnishings
- [ ] Manufacturing or operational facilities (if applicable)

### 4.3 Process and Procedures

- [ ] Standard operating procedures (SOPs)
- [ ] Quality assurance processes
- [ ] Customer onboarding and success processes
- [ ] Customer support procedures
- [ ] Supply chain and vendor dependencies
- [ ] Business continuity and disaster recovery plans

---

## 5. Commercial and Market Due Diligence

### 5.1 Market Position

- [ ] Target market definition and size
- [ ] Market growth trends
- [ ] Competitive landscape
- [ ] Key competitors and competitive advantages
- [ ] Market share estimate
- [ ] Barriers to entry and exit
- [ ] Pricing strategy vs. competitors
- [ ] Brand recognition and market positioning

### 5.2 Customer Base

- [ ] Customer types and segments
- [ ] Customer satisfaction scores (NPS, CSAT)
- [ ] Top customers and concentration risk
- [ ] Customer references available
- [ ] Customer advisory board or user group
- [ ] Win/loss analysis and reasons

### 5.3 Sales and Marketing

- [ ] Sales process and sales cycle
- [ ] Sales compensation and incentives
- [ ] Marketing strategy and spend
- [ ] Lead generation and conversion metrics
- [ ] Sales pipeline and forecast accuracy
- [ ] Customer acquisition strategy and costs
- [ ] Partnerships and channel strategy

### 5.4 Product Roadmap

- [ ] Product vision and strategy
- [ ] Product roadmap for next 12-24 months
- [ ] Planned feature releases
- [ ] Product-market fit evidence
- [ ] User adoption and engagement metrics
- [ ] Product differentiation and unique features

---

## 6. Organizational and HR Due Diligence

### 6.1 Employment Matters

- [ ] Employee roster with compensation
- [ ] Employment agreements and offer letters
- [ ] Stock option or equity agreements
- [ ] Vesting schedules (especially any acceleration on sale)
- [ ] Classified employees (exempt vs. non-exempt)
- [ ] Contractor agreements and 1099s
- [ ] Key person insurance policies

### 6.2 Benefits and Compensation

- [ ] Health insurance plans and carriers
- [ ] 401(k) or retirement plans
- [ ] Paid time off and benefits policies
- [ ] Severance or change of control benefits
- [ ] Bonuses and incentive plans
- [ ] Equity compensation plans

### 6.3 Compliance and Documentation

- [ ] I-9 verification for all employees
- [ ] Wage and hour compliance review
- [ ] Non-compete and non-solicitation agreements
- [ ] Confidentiality and IP assignment agreements
- [ ] Employee handbook and policies
- [ ] No layoffs or unionization threats
- [ ] No WARN Act obligations if applicable

---

## 7. Environmental, Social & Governance (ESG)

### 7.1 Environmental

- [ ] Carbon footprint or sustainability reporting
- [ ] Environmental policies and initiatives
- [ ] Compliance with environmental regulations
- [ ] Supply chain sustainability practices

### 7.2 Social

- [ ] Diversity and inclusion initiatives
- [ ] Employee satisfaction and retention
- [ ] Customer satisfaction and NPS
- [ ] Community engagement and social responsibility

### 7.3 Governance

- [ ] Board composition and independence
- [ ] Executive compensation governance
- [ ] Risk management framework
- [ ] Ethics and compliance programs
- [ ] Whistleblower policies

## 8. Integration Planning

### 8.1 Integration Preparation

- [ ] Identified integration priorities
- [ ] Key integration risks identified
- [ ] 100-day integration plan outline
- [ ] Planned organizational structure post-acquisition
- [ ] Customer communication plan
- [ ] Employee communication and retention plan
- [ ] Technology integration strategy

### 8.2 Transition Services

- [ ] Any transition services agreements needed
- [ ] Key person stay bonuses or retention agreements
- [ ] Contract assumptions or requirements
- [ ] Data migration and integration needs

---

## 9. Deal Structure and Terms

### 9.1 Valuation

- [ ] Valuation methodology and assumptions
- [ ] Comparable transactions (comps)
- [ ] Earnout structure (if any)
- [ ] Seller financing (if any)
- [ ] Representations and warranties insurance
- [ ] Escrow or holdback arrangements

### 9.2 Closing Conditions

- [ ] Regulatory approvals required
- [ ] Third-party consents needed
- [ ] Material adverse change (MAC) clause terms
- [ ] Condition precedents to closing
- [ ] Closing date and timeline

---

## 10. Red Flags and Concerns

### 10.1 Potential Issues to Investigate

- [ ] Significant customer concentration (>30% from one customer)
- [ ] Revenue growth plateau or decline
- [ ] High customer churn (>5% monthly)
- [ ] Key person dependencies
- [ ] Significant related party transactions
- [ ] Pending litigation or regulatory investigations
- [ ] Intellectual property challenges or infringement claims
- [ ] Technology obsolescence or architecture concerns
- [ ] Significant debt or financial obligations
- [ ] Accounting irregularities or unusual transactions
- [ ] Management or team turnover
- [ ] Supplier or vendor concentration risks
- [ ] Incomplete or missing documentation
