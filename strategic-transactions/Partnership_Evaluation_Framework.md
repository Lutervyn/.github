# Partner Relationship Management Framework

Last Updated: May 12, 2026

## 1. Partnership Strategy and Selection

### 1.1 Partnership Types

**Strategic Partnerships**
- Complementary technology or services
- Co-marketing and go-to-market partnerships
- Joint venture or co-development
- Long-term strategic alignment

**Channel Partnerships**
- Reseller or distributor relationships
- White-label or private-label arrangements
- Referral partnerships
- Affiliate marketing

**Vendor Partnerships**
- Critical infrastructure or service providers
- Technology enablers
- Integration partners
- Support or professional services

**Customer Success Partnerships**
- Implementation partners
- Consulting or advisory partnerships
- Integration specialist partners

### 1.2 Partnership Evaluation Framework

**Strategic Fit Score (30%)**
- Alignment with business strategy (10%)
- Complementary capabilities (10%)
- Shared target market (5%)
- Values and culture alignment (5%)

**Business Potential Score (40%)**
- Revenue opportunity (15%)
- Market reach expansion (10%)
- Customer acquisition benefit (10%)
- Margin and unit economics (5%)

**Capability and Reliability Score (20%)**
- Financial stability (8%)
- Team expertise and capability (8%)
- References and track record (4%)

**Relationship Quality Score (10%)**
- Communication style and responsiveness (5%)
- Willingness to invest in relationship (3%)
- Partnership approach and mindset (2%)

## 2. Partnership Development Process

### 2.1 Opportunity Identification

**Source**: 
- Inbound inquiries from potential partners
- Outbound business development efforts
- Industry conferences and events
- Customer requests or recommendations
- Strategic planning initiatives

**Initial Assessment**:
- Does partner fit strategic priorities?
- Is there mutual value creation opportunity?
- Are there any conflicts of interest?
- Preliminary financial viability check

### 2.2 Scoping and Negotiation

**Discovery Calls**:
- Business model and target market alignment
- Value proposition for both parties
- Specific offerings and services
- Potential revenue models
- Expectations and success metrics

**Letter of Intent (LOI)**:
- High-level agreement on partnership terms
- Mutual commitment to move forward
- Exclusivity period (if applicable)
- Timeline to final agreement

**Detailed Negotiation**:
- Contract terms and conditions
- Pricing and revenue sharing
- Support and service levels
- Marketing and go-to-market strategy
- Dispute resolution procedures

### 2.3 Legal and Compliance Review

- [ ] Partner contract reviewed by legal
- [ ] Insurance requirements verified
- [ ] Compliance certifications confirmed
- [ ] Data privacy and security reviewed
- [ ] Conflict of interest check
- [ ] Regulatory compliance verified

## 3. Partnership Agreement Framework

### 3.1 Partnership Agreement Components

**Parties and Definitions**
- Clear identification of all parties
- Definition of partnership scope and term
- Definition of key terms (services, products, etc.)

**Scope of Services**
- Detailed description of each party's responsibilities
- Deliverables and service levels
- Performance metrics and SLAs
- Escalation procedures

**Financial Terms**
- Revenue sharing model (if applicable)
- Commission or fee structure
- Payment terms and conditions
- Price adjustments or escalation
- Audit rights

**Intellectual Property**
- Ownership of partner-developed IP
- License grants and restrictions
- Use of logos and branding
- White-label or co-branding arrangements

**Confidentiality and Data Privacy**
- Confidential information definition
- Permitted uses and restrictions
- Data handling and privacy practices
- Security requirements
- Duration of confidentiality obligations

**Term and Termination**
- Partnership term (length and renewal)
- Termination for cause provisions
- Termination for convenience notice period
- Effect of termination (ongoing obligations, data, etc.)
- Transition assistance requirements

**Liability and Indemnification**
- Limitation of liability
- Indemnification for IP infringement
- Indemnification for negligence or misconduct
- Insurance requirements

**Governance and Communication**
- Regular meeting cadence
- Governance structure (if applicable)
- Primary contacts and escalation path
- Conflict resolution procedures

## 4. Partnership Launch and Onboarding

### 4.1 Launch Planning

**Timeline**: 30-60 days pre-launch
- Finalize all internal processes
- Complete staff training
- Establish communication protocols
- Prepare marketing materials
- Set up technical integrations
- Conduct joint testing (if applicable)

**Launch Activities**:
- Kick-off meeting with partner
- Announce partnership publicly (if applicable)
- Joint marketing push
- Customer/channel communications
- Internal communications and training
- Monitor initial performance

- [ ] All legal/compliance items completed

- [ ] Support and escalation procedures established

## 5. Ongoing Partnership Management

### 5.1 Regular Communication

**Weekly/Bi-Weekly Operational Calls**
- Project or initiative status updates
- Issues and concerns
- Upcoming activities and timeline
- Quick wins and celebrations

**Monthly Business Reviews**
- Performance vs. targets and metrics
- Revenue or growth tracking
- Pipeline or forecast discussion
- Customer or market feedback
- Action items and next steps

**Quarterly Strategic Reviews**
- Performance analysis vs. targets
- Strategic alignment and progress
- Market changes or competitive updates
- Partnership investment or expansion
- Risk assessment and mitigation
- Relationship health check

**Annual Executive Review**
- Annual performance summary
- ROI and business impact assessment
- Renewal decision (if applicable)
- Strategic direction for next year
- Executive relationship building

### 5.2 Performance Tracking

**Monthly Metrics Dashboard**:
| Metric | Target | Actual | Status |
|--------|--------|--------|--------|
| Revenue/Referrals | $_____ | $_____ | ___ |
| Leads Generated | [Count] | [Count] | ___ |
| Conversion Rate | __% | __% | ___ |
| Customer Satisfaction | 85+ | ___ | ___ |
| Response Time (hrs) | [X] | [X] | ___ |
| Issues or Escalations | 0 | [Count] | ___ |

**Success Metrics**:
- Revenue generated or influenced
- Number of customers referred or acquired
- Customer satisfaction and retention
- Market reach or brand lift
- Cost efficiency vs. alternatives
- Partnership relationship quality

### 5.3 Issue Management

**Issue Resolution Process**:
1. Issues escalated at appropriate level
2. Root cause analysis conducted
3. Resolution plan developed
4. Timeline established
5. Regular follow-up on resolution
6. Closure and lesson learned capture

**Common Issues**:
- Performance gaps vs. targets
- Service level misses
- Customer disputes or complaints
- Pricing or financial disagreements
- Miscommunication or misalignment
- Technical integration issues

## 8. Conflict Resolution

### 8.1 Escalation Process

**Level 1: Operational** (Owner: Primary contacts)
- Attempt to resolve directly
- Timeline: 5 business days

**Level 2: Management** (Owner: Department heads)
- Escalation to management level
- Mediated discussion
- Timeline: 10 business days

**Level 3: Executive** (Owner: C-level leaders)
- Executive level discussion
- Third-party mediation if needed
- Timeline: 15 business days

**Level 4: Legal**
- Legal review if business resolution not possible
- Formal dispute resolution procedures per contract
- Arbitration or litigation if necessary

### 8.2 Common Conflict Scenarios

**Revenue/Commission Disputes**:
- Detailed accounting of transactions
- Contract review and interpretation
- Reconciliation of records
- Resolution and payment adjustment

**Performance Issues**:
- Clear documentation of performance gap
- Root cause analysis
- Improvement plan with timeline
- Monitoring and verification

**Competitive/Exclusivity Issues**:
- Review of exclusivity terms
- Assessment of competitive threat
- Negotiated resolution or termination

## 9. Partnership Review and Renewal

### 9.1 Annual Review Process

**Review Timing**: 90 days before contract expiration

**Review Criteria**:
- Revenue and business impact (40%)
- Performance vs. SLAs (25%)
- Customer satisfaction (15%)
- Strategic alignment (15%)
- Relationship quality (5%)

**Renewal Decision**:
- Continue partnership on existing terms
- Continue with modifications or improvements
- Continue with changed financial terms
- Non-renewal and transition plan

### 9.2 Renewal Negotiation

**If Renewing**:
- Terms remain same or renegotiated
- Updated performance targets
- Pricing adjustments (if applicable)
- Enhanced benefits or support (if earned)
- Updated contract for upcoming period

**If Non-Renewing**:
- 90-180 day transition period
- Knowledge transfer and documentation
- Customer transition plan
- Final accounting and settlement
- Professional and respectful exit

## 10. Relationship Health Assessment

**Quarterly Health Check**:

| Factor | Excellent | Good | At-Risk | Critical |
|--------|-----------|------|---------|----------|
| Communication | Proactive | Regular | Reactive | Poor |
| Performance | Exceeding | Meeting | Below | Failed |
| Responsiveness | <4 hrs | <24 hrs | <48 hrs | No response |
| Professionalism | Excellent | Good | Concerns | Major issues |
| Collaboration | Strong | Good | Limited | Adversarial |
| Trust Level | High | Good | Declining | Broken |

**Health Score Calculation**:
- Green (Excellent): 90-100 → Continue and expand
- Yellow (Good): 70-89 → Continue with monitoring
- Orange (At-Risk): 50-69 → Improvement plan needed
- Red (Critical): <50 → Escalation or termination
