# Internal Wiki and Knowledge Base Management

Last Updated: May 12, 2026

## 1. Overview

### 1.1 Purpose
The Internal Wiki is Lutervyn's "Single Source of Truth" for non-versioned information, enabling employees to find answers quickly and self-serve.

### 1.2 Tool
Lutervyn uses **Notion** (or Confluence) as the primary platform for the Internal Wiki.

## 2. Wiki Structure

The Wiki is organized into the following workspaces:
- **🏠 Home**: General company info, mission, and news.
- **👥 People & Culture**: HR policies, benefits, onboarding.
- **💻 Engineering**: Tech stacks, dev guides, infrastructure.
- **🚀 Product**: Roadmap, PRDs, design system.
- **💰 Finance & Legal**: Expense policies, contract templates.
- **🤝 Sales & CS**: Playbooks, customer health data.

## 3. Content Guidelines

### 3.1 Creating Content
- Anyone is encouraged to create a page if they find a gap in our knowledge.
- Use templates where available (e.g., "Meeting Notes Template," "How-to Guide Template").
- Cross-link to other relevant pages to improve discoverability.

### 3.2 Updating Content
- If you find a mistake or outdated information, **fix it immediately**.
- For major changes to shared processes, notify the relevant team via Slack.

## 4. Search and Discoverability

- **Keywords**: Use clear, searchable titles.
- **Tags**: Use standardized tags (e.g., `#onboarding`, `#security`, `#tutorial`).
- **Organization**: Don't let the sidebar get cluttered; use sub-pages to group related topics.

## 5. Governance

- **Wiki Gardeners**: The Operations team acts as "gardeners," regularly reviewing the structure, cleaning up duplicate pages, and archiving old content.
- **Archiving**: Old pages should be moved to the "Archive" folder rather than deleted, in case the information is needed later.

## 6. Training

- All new hires receive a "Wiki Tour" as part of their onboarding.
- Monthly "Wiki Tips" are shared in the #announcements channel.
