# Internal Communication Guidelines

Last Updated: May 12, 2026

## 1. Overview

### 1.1 Purpose
To ensure that communication within Lutervyn is clear, inclusive, and efficient, preventing information overload and silos.

### 1.2 Our Philosophy
- **Default to Open**: Share information publicly in Slack channels rather than DM/email unless it is sensitive.
- **Async First**: Respect people's time by using asynchronous communication for status updates and non-urgent questions.
- **Clarity > Cleverness**: Use simple, direct language.

## 2. Choosing the Right Channel

| Channel | Best For... | Response Expectation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Slack (Public)** | General questions, team updates, social. | Within 4-8 business hours. |
| **Slack (DM)** | Private, 1-on-1, or urgent questions. | Within 2-4 business hours. |
| **Email** | Formal announcements, external parties. | Within 24 business hours. |
| **Meetings** | Complex problem solving, brainstorming. | Real-time. |
| **Linear/GitHub** | Task-specific technical discussions. | Within 1 business day. |

## 3. Slack Best Practices

- **Use Threads**: Keep discussions organized by replying in threads.
- **Channel Naming**:
  - `#team-[name]`: For department-specific work.
  - `#proj-[name]`: For specific projects.
  - `#social-[name]`: For non-work topics.
  - `#announcements`: For company-wide updates (read-only for most).
- **Notification Management**: Use "Do Not Disturb" (DND) during focus time and outside of working hours.

## 4. Email Etiquette

- **Subject Lines**: Make them descriptive (e.g., "ACTION REQUIRED: Q3 Planning").
- **Conciseness**: Get to the point in the first paragraph.
- **CC/BCC**: Only include people who actually need to see the information.

## 5. Meeting Guidelines

- **No Agenda, No Meeting**: Every invite must include an agenda.
- **Recordings**: Record major meetings for those who cannot attend.
- **Summary**: Send a brief "Action Items" summary to all attendees within 24 hours.

## 6. Personal Boundaries

- We respect the "Right to Disconnect." No one is expected to respond to non-emergency communication during weekends or holidays.
