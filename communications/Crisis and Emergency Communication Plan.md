# Crisis and Emergency Communication Plan

Last Updated: May 12, 2026

## 1. Overview

### 1.1 Purpose
To define the protocols for communicating quickly and effectively during a major crisis to ensure personnel safety and protect Lutervyn's reputation.

### 1.2 Scope
Includes natural disasters, major security breaches, legal crises, and workplace emergencies.

## 2. Crisis Communication Team (CCT)

- **Team Lead**: CEO
- **Primary Spokesperson**: Head of Communications
- **Internal Lead**: Head of HR
- **Technical/Legal Liaison**: CTO / General Counsel

## 3. Communication Protocols

### 3.1 Level 1: Life/Safety Emergency (Immediate)
- **Channel**: SMS/Phone call via emergency alert system, Slack @channel in #announcements.
- **Message**: Clear instructions on safety (e.g., "Evacuate," "Shelter in place").
- **Frequency**: Continuous updates until the danger has passed.

### 3.2 Level 2: Business Critical Crisis (Within 1-2 Hours)
- **Channel**: Internal #announcements Slack, Follow-up Email.
- **Message**: Acknowledgment of the issue, current status, and what is being done.
- **Spokesperson**: CEO or relevant Department Head.

### 3.3 External Communication (PR)
- **Rule**: NO employee is authorized to speak to the media or post about the crisis on social media without CCT approval.
- **Official Statement**: Issued via the company website and official social media accounts.

## 4. Designated Channels

- **Internal**: #announcements (Primary), Email (Backup).
- **External**: Official Lutervyn Twitter/X account, Press Release via BusinessWire.
- **Status Page**: status.lutervyn.com (for technical outages).

## 5. Post-Crisis Communication

- Within 48 hours of resolution, a "Summary Memo" will be sent to all employees.
- Within 7 days, a public "Post-Mortem" or update will be shared if the crisis affected customers.

## 6. Training & Drills

- The CCT conducts a mock crisis simulation once per year.
- All employees must ensure their emergency contact information is updated in the HR portal.
