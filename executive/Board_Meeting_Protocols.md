## 2. Board Meeting Schedule

### 2.1 Regular Meetings

**Quarterly Board Meetings**
- Scheduled: 2nd Tuesday of month following quarter end
- Q1: May 12, Q2: August 12, Q3: November 12, Q4: February 12
- Duration: 2-3 hours
- Format: In-person (annual meeting) or video conference

**Annual Meeting**
- Date: June 30 (end of FY)
- Location: Company headquarters or specified venue
- Duration: 3-4 hours
- Attendees: All board members required

**Committee Meetings**
- Audit: Monthly (1st Friday)
- Compensation: Quarterly (before board meeting)
- Nominating: Semi-annually or as needed

**Special/Emergency Meetings**
- Called by: CEO, Board chair, or any 2 directors
- Notice: 24-48 hours (except emergencies)
- Purpose: Material decisions or issues requiring immediate action

### 2.2 Meeting Cancellation

- If quorum cannot be met: Meeting postponed
- Board chair can cancel with unanimous consent
- Rescheduled within 2 weeks

## 3. Meeting Preparation

### 3.1 Meeting Materials

**Distributed By**: CFO/Board secretary  
**Timing**: Minimum 5 business days before meeting  
**Format**: PDF via secure email or board portal

**Materials Package Includes**:
1. Agenda (with time allocations)
2. Prior meeting minutes
3. Financial statements and analysis
4. KPI dashboard and performance metrics
5. Management presentations
6. Investor updates and communications
7. Pending board approvals/resolutions
8. Company announcements or press releases
9. Risk management updates
10. Competitive intelligence summary

### 3.2 Agenda Development

**Created By**: CEO + Board chair  
**Timeline**: Distributed with materials (5 days advance)

**Standing Agenda Items**:
1. Call to order and quorum confirmation (5 min)
2. Approval of prior minutes (2 min)
3. Financial overview and performance (20 min)
4. CEO update on strategic initiatives (15 min)
5. Sales and customer metrics (10 min)
6. Product roadmap update (10 min)
7. Team/people update (5 min)
8. Risk management and compliance (10 min)
9. Key decisions requiring board vote (30 min)
10. Investor update and communications (10 min)
11. Committee reports (if applicable) (15 min)
12. Executive session (without CEO) (15 min)
13. Next meeting date and adjournment (5 min)

**Special Agenda Items**: Added by CEO or board chair with notice

### 3.3 Board Portal/Document Management

**Access**: All board materials via secure portal  
**Retention**: All materials archived for 7+ years  
**Access Controls**: Board members only  
**Confidentiality**: All materials confidential

## 4. Meeting Conduct

### 4.1 Quorum and Voting

**Quorum Required**: Majority of directors present (in-person or via video)

**Voting**:
- Majority vote determines outcome (except reserved matters)
- Each director: 1 vote
- Ties: Decided by board chair
- Abstentions: Permitted, do not count as votes

**Reserved Matters** (require supermajority or unanimous):
- Amendments to bylaws/charter
- Sale or merger of company
- Liquidation or dissolution
- Related party transactions (if director conflicted)

### 4.2 Attendance Expectations

- Directors expected to attend all meetings
- Attendance via video acceptable
- Absence: Acceptable only with notice to board chair
- Proxies: Not permitted
- Minimum attendance: 75% of annual meetings

**Director Removal**: Failure to attend 2+ consecutive meetings without valid reason may result in removal recommendation

### 4.3 Conflict of Interest

**Disclosure Required**:
- At start of each meeting
- Prior to any vote
- When conflict suspected by others

**Recusal Procedures**:
- Director discloses conflict
- Director exits discussion
- Director does not vote
- Documented in minutes

**Related Party Transactions**:
- All disclosed to board
- Disinterested directors vote
- Board determines if fair and in company interest

## 5. Presentation Standards

### 5.1 Presentation Format

**Time Limits**:
- CEO update: 15 minutes + 5 min Q&A
- Department updates: 10 minutes each + Q&A
- Key decisions: 10 min presentation + discussion

**Presentation Contents**:
- Clear objective/topic
- Executive summary (slides 1-3)
- Data and analysis (supporting slides)
- Recommendation or decision requested
- Timeline for action

### 5.2 Financial Reporting

**Minimum Financial Information**:
- Income statement (actual vs. budget vs. forecast)
- Balance sheet summary
- Cash flow statement
- KPI dashboard (revenue, churn, CAC, LTV, etc.)
- Variance analysis and explanations
- Forward-looking forecast (next 3-4 quarters)
- Commentary on trends

## 6. Minutes and Documentation

### 6.1 Minutes Recording

**Prepared By**: Board secretary (CFO or designated)  
**Timing**: Approved at next meeting  
**Contents**:
- Date, time, location of meeting
- Attendees and absent directors
- Topics discussed and decisions made
- Votes taken and results
- Action items with owners and deadlines
- Conflicts of interest disclosed
- Executive session summary (if held)

### 6.2 Minutes Distribution

- Draft minutes provided within 1 week
- Discussed and approved at next board meeting
- Final approved minutes within 2 weeks
- Stored in secure repository
- Provided to audit/governance committee

### 6.3 Secretary's Responsibilities

- Maintain board calendar
- Coordinate meeting logistics
- Prepare materials and agendas
- Record and file minutes
- Maintain board records
- Track action items and follow-up

## 7. Executive Session

### 7.1 Executive Session Format

**Timing**: End of board meeting (30 min typical)  
**Attendees**: Directors only (without CEO/management)  
**Purpose**: Candid discussion on:
- CEO performance and compensation
- Executive compensation decisions
- Strategic direction feedback
- Board dynamics and effectiveness
- Confidential matters

**Frequency**: Every board meeting

### 7.2 Executive Session Notes

- Brief notes on key topics discussed
- No detailed minutes of discussion
- Summary provided to CEO after meeting
- Stored separately with high confidentiality

## 8. Board Resolutions and Approvals

### 8.1 Consent Resolutions

**Used For**: Non-controversial or emergency approvals  
**Process**:
- Proposal sent to all directors
- Directors vote via email or portal
- Unanimous approval required
- Board notified at next meeting

**Examples**:
- Hiring key executives
- Annual budget approval
- Routine contract approvals
- Banking authority resolutions

### 8.2 Standard Resolutions

**Used For**: Strategic decisions requiring discussion

**Resolution Template**:
```
RESOLVED: [Description of action]

The Company will [specific action/commitment]

Term: [Duration or until further action]

Approved: [Date]
Approved by: [Director names]
```

## 10. Board Member Evaluation

### 10.1 Annual Evaluation Process

**Timing**: June/July (fiscal year-end)  
**Process**:
1. Self-evaluation by each director
2. Peer evaluation (360-degree)
3. Board chair summarizes feedback
4. Individual meetings to discuss results
5. Recommendations for improvement

**Evaluation Criteria**:
- Meeting attendance and participation
- Committee effectiveness
- Strategic input quality
- Governance knowledge
- Interpersonal effectiveness
- Independence and objectivity
- Areas for development

### 10.2 Director Removal or Non-Reelection

**Grounds for Removal**:
- Continued poor performance
- Failure to attend majority of meetings
- Conflicts of interest not properly managed
- Violation of confidentiality
- Unethical conduct

**Process**:
- Board discussion and vote
- Supermajority required (2/3)
- Respectful notification by Board chair
- Transition period for orderly succession
