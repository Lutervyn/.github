Last Updated: May 12, 2026

## 2. Executive Decision Framework

### 2.1 Decision Authority Levels

**Level 1: CEO Authority Only**
- Board member appointments/removals
- Major M&A transactions (>$1M)
- Fundraising rounds and investor agreements
- Strategic pivots or business model changes
- Executive team hiring/firing (C-level)
- Budget >$100K not previously approved

**Level 2: CEO + One Other Executive**
- Capital expenditures $50-100K
- New product lines or major features
- Major partnerships (>$500K or >1 year)
- Real estate leases >$50K annually
- Marketing campaigns >$50K
- Requires written approval

**Level 3: Relevant Department VP + Approval**
- Department budget allocations
- Headcount additions within approved budget
- Vendor contracts <$50K
- Customer deals within approved terms
- Standard operational decisions

### 2.2 Decision Process

**Step 1: Proposal**
- Department lead submits business case
- Include: Rationale, financials, alternatives, risks
- Timeline: Decision urgency identified

**Step 2: Review**
- Relevant executives evaluate
- Financial impact assessed
- Strategic alignment verified
- Risk assessment completed

**Step 3: Decision**
- Decision maker(s) approve or reject
- Rejection includes feedback
- Documentation in decision log

**Step 4: Communication**
- All affected parties notified
- Implementation timeline established
- Accountability assigned

## 3. Executive Meeting Cadence

### 3.1 Weekly Executive Team Meeting

**Frequency**: Monday 9:00-10:30 AM  
**Attendees**: CEO, CTO, CFO, CPO, COO  
**Duration**: 90 minutes maximum

**Agenda**:
1. Week objectives recap (10 min)
2. Strategic initiatives status (20 min)
3. Financial snapshot (10 min)
4. People/culture updates (10 min)
5. Cross-functional issues (20 min)
6. Decision items requiring exec alignment (15 min)
7. Next week priorities (5 min)

### 3.2 Monthly All-Hands Meeting

**Frequency**: Last Friday of month, 3:00 PM  
**Attendees**: All staff  
**Duration**: 60 minutes

**Format**:
- CEO: Company vision and monthly highlights (10 min)
- Department heads: Team updates (20 min)
- Q&A: Employee questions (15 min)
- Recognition and celebrations (10 min)
- Announcements (5 min)

### 3.3 Monthly Finance Review

**Frequency**: 1st Friday of month, 10:00 AM  
**Attendees**: CFO, CEO, Department heads  
**Duration**: 45 minutes

**Topics**:
- Monthly P&L review
- Variance analysis
- Cash position and runway
- Budget vs. actuals by department
- Forward-looking forecasts

### 3.4 Quarterly Board Meeting

**Frequency**: 2nd Monday of quarter-end month  
**Attendees**: Board members, CEO, CFO  
**Duration**: 2 hours

**Format**:
- CEO update: Strategic progress (15 min)
- Financial update: Results and forecast (20 min)
- Investor updates: Communications (10 min)
- Metrics and KPIs: Performance (15 min)
- Board decisions: Votes and approvals (20 min)
- Executive session: Without CEO (15 min)

### 3.5 Bi-Weekly 1-on-1s

**CEO 1-on-1s**:
- Every other Tuesday with each direct report
- 30 minutes each
- Topics: Performance, goals, obstacles, feedback

## 7. Escalation and Conflict Resolution

### 7.1 Executive Conflict Process

**Step 1: Direct Discussion**
- Concerned executives meet 1-on-1
- Attempt to resolve directly
- Timeline: Within 3 business days

**Step 2: CEO Mediation**
- If unresolved, CEO facilitates discussion
- All perspectives heard
- Timeline: Within 1 week

**Step 3: Board Escalation**
- If still unresolved, CEO reports to board
- Board provides guidance or decision
- Reserved for significant strategic conflicts

### 7.2 Executive Performance Issues

**Performance Management**:
- CEO provides feedback in regular 1-on-1s
- Clear expectations and improvement plans
- 30-day assessment periods if needed
- Board notification if performance critical

**Termination Process**:
- Documented performance issues required
- 30-day notice period typical (or paid in lieu)
- Board approval for C-level termination
- Transition plan with knowledge transfer

## 8. Executive Code of Conduct

### 8.1 Standards

All executives will:
- Maintain high ethical standards
- Comply with all laws and regulations
- Avoid conflicts of interest
- Protect confidential information
- Treat employees and partners with respect
- Model company values

### 8.2 Conflict of Interest

**Disclosure Required For**:
- Personal investments or businesses
- Family relationships with employees
- Outside board positions
- Customer or vendor relationships
- Any transactions with company

**Resolution**:
- CEO notified of conflict
- Independent assessment if needed
- Recusal from decision-making if required

## 9. Succession Planning

### 9.1 Succession Strategy

**CEO Succession**:
- Board identifies 3-5 potential successors annually
- Development plan for each potential successor
- External recruitment option prepared

**Executive Succession**:
- Each executive identifies 2 potential replacements
- Development plans created for high-potential employees
- Emergency succession plan maintained

### 9.2 Emergency Procedures

**If CEO Unavailable**:
- COO assumes temporary leadership
- Board called into emergency session
- Decision authority temporarily delegated

**If Any Executive Unavailable**:
- Deputy or next senior leader assumes role
- Department continues operations
- Board notified within 24 hours

### 10.2 Quarterly Performance Reviews

**Review Process**:
- Self-assessment by executive

- CEO assessment and feedback
- Board review (C-level only)
