## Key Policies at a Glance

**Executive Decision Authority**:
- CEO: Strategic decisions and >$100K investments
- CFO: Financial decisions and budget management
- CTO: Technology and engineering decisions
- CPO: Product and roadmap decisions
- COO: Operations and vendor management

**Meeting Cadence**:
- Weekly Executive Team Meeting (90 min)
- Monthly All-Hands Meeting (60 min)
- Monthly Finance Review (45 min)
- Quarterly Board Meetings (2 hours)
- Bi-weekly CEO 1-on-1s (30 min each)

**Decision Process**:
- Problem definition → Analysis & options → Stakeholder input → Recommendation → Decision & communication
- All decisions >$10K must be documented
- SLAs: <$10K (3 days), $10K-$50K (5 days), $50K-$100K (10 days), >$100K (board cycle)

**Board Requirements**:
- Quarterly board meetings (mandatory)
- 5 days advance materials distribution
- Minimum 75% attendance
- Director conflicts of interest disclosed
- Executive session at every board meeting

## Process Overview

### Executive Decision Process
1. Problem definition and decision request
2. Analysis of 3+ options with pros/cons
3. Stakeholder and legal input
4. Recommendation to decision maker
5. Decision documentation and communication

### Board Meeting Process
1. Agenda and materials prepared (5 days in advance)
2. Pre-meeting coordination calls
3. Board meeting (2-3 hours)
4. Executive session (without CEO)
5. Minutes approved at next meeting
6. Action item tracking

### Strategic Planning Cycle
- August: Board input and market analysis
- September: Executive planning and goal setting
- October: Board approval and company-wide communication
- Quarterly reviews of progress against plan
