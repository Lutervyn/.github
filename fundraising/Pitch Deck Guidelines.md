# Pitch Deck Guidelines

Last Updated: May 12, 2026

## 1. Pitch Deck Overview

### 1.1 Purpose

The pitch deck is a visual tool to tell Lutervyn's story to potential investors in 10-15 minutes.

### 1.2 Audience

- Venture capital firms
- Angel investors
- Strategic investors
- Potential acquirers
- Board members

### 1.3 Goal

- Secure meeting: follow-up investor meeting
- Interest: investor interest in deeper due diligence
- Investment: ultimate goal is funding commitment
- Relationship: build investor relationship

## 2. Deck Structure

### 2.1 Standard Slide Order

1. Title Slide
2. Problem Statement
3. Solution
4. Market Opportunity
5. Business Model
6. Traction
7. Product Demo
8. Go-to-Market Strategy
9. Competition
10. Team
11. Financial Projections
12. Use of Funds
13. Funding Ask
14. Call to Action

### 2.2 Slide Count

- Minimum: 10 slides
- Standard: 12-15 slides
- Maximum: 20 slides
- Appendix: additional slides as reference

## 3. Individual Slide Guidelines

### 3.1 Title Slide

**Content**
- Company name: prominent display
- Tagline: concise description
- Date: pitch date
- Contact: founder contact info
- Logo: company logo

**Design**
- Professional: high quality
- Branded: consistent with brand
- Simple: clear and clean
- Impactful: memorable opening

### 3.2 Problem Slide

**Content**
- Problem: clearly stated
- Pain: customer pain points
- Market: market size context
- Status quo: current approach
- Gap: why current solutions don't work

**Approach**
- Relatable: investors understand problem
- Validated: evidence of problem
- Quantified: size of problem
- Customer: customer perspective

### 3.3 Solution Slide

**Content**
- Solution: how Lutervyn solves
- Differentiation: what makes it unique
- Benefits: key benefits
- Features: key features
- Technology: technology advantage

**Approach**
- Clear: easy to understand
- Compelling: why it's better
- Simple: not overly technical
- Focused: on customer benefit

### 3.4 Market Opportunity Slide

**Content**
- TAM: total addressable market size
- SAM: serviceable addressable market
- SOM: serviceable obtainable market
- Growth: market growth rate
- Timing: why now

**Metrics**
- Quantified: specific numbers
- Sourced: credible sources cited
- Conservative: conservative estimates
- Trends: market trends supporting

### 3.5 Business Model Slide

**Content**
- Revenue: primary revenue source
- Pricing: pricing strategy
- Customers: target customer
- Channels: distribution channels
- Unit: economics (LTV/CAC)

**Format**
- Visual: diagram or graphic
- Clear: revenue streams obvious
- Scalable: business model scales
- Sustainable: economics work

### 3.6 Traction Slide

**Content**
- Metrics: key metrics
- Growth: growth rate
- Users: user growth
- Revenue: revenue growth
- Partnerships: key partnerships
- Press: media coverage
- Validation: customer validation

**Approach**
- Specific: actual numbers not percentages
- Trend: showing growth trajectory
- Honest: realistic metrics
- Compelling: impressive but believable

### 3.7 Product Demo Slide or Video

**Content**
- Walkthrough: product demonstration
- Features: key features shown
- Value: customer value shown
- Integration: how it's used
- Experience: user experience

**Approach**
- Concise: 1-2 minute maximum
- Engaging: visually appealing
- Relevant: shows customer value
- Prepared: smooth presentation
- Backup: screenshot slides if tech fails

### 3.8 Go-to-Market Strategy

**Content**
- Target: target customer segment
- Acquisition: customer acquisition strategy
- Channels: primary channels
- Messaging: key messaging
- Timeline: go-to-market timeline
- Team: go-to-market team

**Approach**
- Realistic: achievable plan
- Detailed: specific tactics
- Proven: leveraging existing channels
- Capital efficient: appropriate for funding

### 3.9 Competition Slide

**Content**
- Competitors: direct and indirect
- Comparison: feature comparison
- Positioning: Lutervyn's position
- Advantage: competitive advantage
- Barriers: defensibility

**Approach**
- Honest: acknowledge competitors
- Differentiated: clear differentiation
- Compelling: why Lutervyn wins
- Barriers: defensibility emphasized

### 3.10 Team Slide

**Content**
- Founders: founder bios
- Experience: relevant experience
- Expertise: key expertise
- Roles: current roles
- Gaps: planned hires

**Approach**
- Credible: team members' credentials
- Complementary: team has complementary skills
- Experienced: relevant experience
- Committed: team fully committed
- Complete: key gaps identified

### 3.11 Financial Projections

**Content**
- Revenue: revenue projections
- Timeline: 3-5 year outlook
- Profitability: path to profitability
- Margins: gross margin trajectory
- Growth: assumed growth rates
- Assumptions: key assumptions

**Approach**
- Conservative: realistic assumptions
- Detailed: bottom-up build if possible
- Comparison: benchmarked to industry
- Sensitivity: sensitivity analysis
- Track record: management track record

### 3.12 Use of Funds

**Content**
- Total raised: total funding requested
- Allocation: pie chart breakdown
- Personnel: team hiring
- Product: product development
- Marketing: marketing and sales
- Operations: operating expenses
- Runway: expected runway

**Approach**
- Specific: detailed allocation
- Purposeful: clear purpose for each dollar
- Realistic: realistic allocations
- Timeline: funding runway explained

### 3.13 Ask Slide

**Content**
- Amount: specific funding amount
- Terms: proposed valuation or terms
- Use: primary use of funds
- Timeline: funding timeline
- Next steps: next steps

**Approach**
- Clear: specific amount
- Reasonable: reasonable for stage
- Terms: flexible terms discussed separately
- Timeline: realistic funding timeline

### 3.14 Call to Action

**Content**
- Contact: founder contact info
- Email: investor email
- Phone: phone number
- Website: company website
- Follow-up: next meeting timeline
- Thank you: appreciation

**Approach**
- Simple: clear call to action
- Memorable: easy to remember
- Responsive: quick response time
- Appreciative: thank investors

## 4. Design Guidelines

### 4.1 Visual Design

**Colors**
- Branded: use company colors
- Professional: appropriate colors
- Contrast: readable text/background
- Consistency: consistent throughout

**Fonts**
- Readable: large, readable font
- Limited: 2-3 fonts maximum
- Professional: professional fonts
- Size: 24pt minimum for body

**Layout**
- Clean: uncluttered layout
- Consistent: consistent formatting
- Aligned: properly aligned elements
- Breathing: white space

### 4.2 Graphics & Charts

**Data Visualization**
- Clear: easy to understand
- Labeled: clearly labeled
- Sourced: sources cited
- Proportional: accurate proportions
- Simple: not overly complex

**Images**
- Professional: high quality
- Relevant: relevant to content
- Consistent: consistent style
- Branded: brand consistent

### 4.3 Text & Content

**Slide Text**
- Minimal: bullets not paragraphs
- Large: readable text size
- Headline: clear headline per slide
- Consistent: consistent formatting

**Bullet Points**
- Concise: 2-3 lines maximum
- Key: key points only
- Parallel: parallel structure
- Impact: impactful language

## 5. Presentation Tips

### 5.1 Delivery

**Preparation**
- Practice: multiple run-throughs
- Timing: 10-15 minute target
- Stories: compelling story
- Transitions: smooth transitions
- Notes: minimal notes

**Presentation**
- Engagement: engage audience
- Eye contact: look at audience
- Enthusiasm: show passion
- Pace: appropriate pace
- Tone: professional tone

### 5.2 Q&A Preparation

**Likely Questions**
- Market: how big is market
- Competition: how do you compete
- Team: why should we trust you
- Revenue: how do you make money
- Growth: how do you scale
- Risk: what are the risks

**Answer Preparation**
- Think-through: potential questions
- Data: have data ready
- Honest: honest answers
- Concise: 1-2 minute answers
- Follow-up: offer follow-up data

### 5.3 Technical Setup

**Equipment**
- Laptop: reliable laptop
- Projector: test projector
- Remote: wireless presenter remote
- Backup: backup copy on USB
- Phone: backup on phone

**Contingency**
- Printouts: printed copies
- Screen: screenshots as backup
- Video: if product demo fails
- Connection: backup WiFi/hotspot

## 6. Common Mistakes to Avoid

**Mistakes**
- Too dense: too much text per slide
- Too long: presentation too lengthy
- No differentiation: unclear competitive advantage
- Weak team: unqualified team members
- Unrealistic: unrealistic projections
- Unclear: unclear business model
- No traction: no customer validation
- Poor design: unprofessional design
- No story: lacks compelling narrative
- Overly technical: too technical for audience

## 7. Appendix Slides

**Suggested Appendix**
- Market research: supporting research
- Customer testimonials: customer quotes
- Partnerships: key partnerships
- Technology: technology deep-dive
- Regulatory: regulatory landscape
- Financial detail: detailed financial analysis
- Team: detailed team bios
- Roadmap: product roadmap
- References: customer references

## 8. Digital Delivery

### 8.1 File Format

- PDF: primary format (not editable)
- Slides: PowerPoint backup copy
- Video: screen recording with narration
- Web: hosted on website
- Portal: in investor portal

### 8.2 Sharing Deck

**Process**
- Request: investor requests deck
- NDA: get NDA signed if needed
- Provide: send deck in formats
- Watermark: include watermark
- Track: track distribution

**Caution**
- Confidential: mark as confidential
- Control: limit distribution
- Track: can it be tracked
- Expiration: deck expiration date
