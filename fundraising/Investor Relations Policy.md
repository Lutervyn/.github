# Investor Relations Policy

Last Updated: May 12, 2026

## 1. Investor Relations Overview

### 1.1 Purpose

Establish professional, transparent communication with current and prospective investors aligned with securities laws.

### 1.2 Objectives

- Transparency: clear financial reporting
- Communication: regular updates
- Compliance: regulatory adherence
- Relationships: build long-term partnership
- Trust: demonstrate good stewardship

### 1.3 IR Team

**Responsible Parties**
- CEO: overall strategy
- CFO: financial information
- IR Director: day-to-day IR
- Legal: compliance review
- Board: oversight

## 2. Investor Communication

### 2.1 Regular Updates

**Frequency**
- Quarterly: financial results
- Annual: comprehensive review
- Ad-hoc: material events
- Requested: investor inquiries

**Timing**
- Financial: within 30 days quarter-end
- Annual: before year-end filings
- Material events: prompt disclosure
- Embargo: until announcement

### 2.2 Communication Methods

**Approved Channels**
- Email: formal communications
- Investor portal: secure access
- Conference calls: quarterly earnings
- Board meetings: investor presentations
- Website: public information

**Investor Portal**
- Secure: authentication required
- Documents: financial statements
- Updates: real-time notifications
- Filings: SEC documents if applicable
- Contact: investor questions

### 2.3 Communication Content

**Financial Information**
- Balance sheet: assets and liabilities
- Income statement: revenue and expenses
- Cash flow: operations and investing
- Key metrics: KPIs and ratios
- Variance: explanation of changes

**Non-Financial Information**
- Strategy: business direction
- Markets: market opportunity
- Competition: competitive landscape
- Personnel: key management changes
- Governance: board and committee changes

### 2.4 Restrictions on Communication

**What Cannot Be Shared**
- Material non-public information (MNPI)
- Until: official public announcement
- Insider trading: prevention
- Confidential: trade secrets
- Competitive: sensitive information

**Forward-Looking Statements**
- Disclaimer: cautionary language
- Risks: material risks disclosed
- Assumptions: underlying assumptions
- Caveats: limitations of projections

## 3. Investor Relations Policies

### 3.1 Disclosure Policy

**Principle**
- Timely: prompt disclosure
- Accurate: factually correct
- Complete: all material information
- Fair: equal access
- Consistent: with prior disclosures

### 3.2 Selective Disclosure

**Prohibited**
- MNPI: cannot be selectively disclosed
- Fairness: all investors treated equally
- Leaking: of material non-public information prohibited
- Timing: no advance notice to select investors

### 3.3 Investor Meetings

**Preparation**
- Agenda: prepared in advance
- Materials: provided to participants
- Scripts: approved by legal/finance
- Participants: authorized spokespersons only
- Documentation: notes taken

**During Meeting**
- Stick to: prepared remarks
- Avoid: speculation
- Clarify: uncertain information
- Record: if authorized
- Confirm: follow-up in writing

**After Meeting**
- Thank you: to investors
- Recap: of key points discussed
- Corrections: if inaccuracies noted
- Files: maintained for records

### 3.4 Earnings Calls

**Quarterly Calls**
- Timing: within 30 days of quarter-end
- Participants: CEO, CFO, IR
- Format: prepared remarks + Q&A
- Recording: available to all investors
- Transcript: published afterward

**Call Conduct**
- Operators: introduce participants
- Disclaimer: forward-looking statement
- Remarks: 15-20 minutes
- Q&A: investor questions
- Closing: thank participants
- Archive: recording available

### 3.5 Annual Meeting

**Investor Presentation**
- Comprehensive: full business overview
- Results: prior year performance
- Strategy: future direction
- Questions: Q&A session
- Documents: investor package provided

## 4. Financial Information & Reporting

### 4.1 Financial Statements

**Provided to Investors**
- Income statement: revenue, expenses, net income
- Balance sheet: assets, liabilities, equity
- Cash flow: operating, investing, financing
- Key metrics: ratios and KPIs
- Segment: if applicable by division

### 4.2 Financial Metrics

**Key Performance Indicators**
- Revenue: total and by segment
- EBITDA: earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation, amortization
- Gross margin: percentage of revenue
- Operating margin: percentage of revenue
- Net income: bottom line
- Cash position: available liquidity

### 4.3 Forecasts & Projections

**Guidance Provided**
- When: if management guidance given
- Quarterly: or annual guidance
- Updates: revised as appropriate
- Caveats: risks and assumptions
- Ranges: high and low estimates

**Guidance Management**
- Realistic: achievable targets
- Conservative: underpromise, overdeliver
- Revisions: if circumstances change
- Transparency: explain changes
- Consistency: track versus actual

### 4.4 Management Discussion & Analysis (MD&A)

**Components**
- Overview: business summary
- Results: period performance
- Trends: year-to-year comparison
- Analysis: explanations of variances
- Forward: outlook and guidance

## 5. Investor Types & Relationships

### 5.1 Equity Investors

**Venture Capital**
- Partnership: ongoing relationship
- Board seats: typically represented
- Updates: board meetings quarterly
- Engagement: strategic discussions
- Follow-on: investment in future rounds

**Angel Investors**
- Individual: high-net-worth individuals
- Communication: regular updates
- Meeting: less frequent than VC
- Notes: detailed investor updates
- Network: may assist with introductions

**Institutional Investors**
- Funds: mutual funds, hedge funds
- Holdings: investment thesis
- Analyst: dedicated coverage
- Reports: SEC filings public
- Liquidity: potential exit timeline

### 5.2 Debt Investors

**Bank Lenders**
- Covenants: compliance reporting
- Frequency: quarterly or monthly
- Metrics: financial covenants
- Violations: prompt reporting
- Compliance: maintain loan in good standing

**Bond Holders**
- Rating agencies: periodic updates
- Covenants: maintenance requirements
- Payment: timely interest payment
- Events: notice of material events

## 6. Confidentiality & Non-Disclosure

### 6.1 Confidentiality Agreements

**Investors**
- NDA: required for material information
- Timing: before detailed discussions
- Scope: applies to MNPI
- Term: perpetual for trade secrets
- Enforcement: company rights

### 6.2 Information Security

**Documents**
- Stored: secure location
- Access: restricted to authorized
- Encryption: for digital documents
- Destruction: when no longer needed
- Third parties: NDAs required

## 7. Investor Updates

### 7.1 Investor Newsletter

**Frequency**
- Quarterly: after earnings
- Content: summary of performance
- Format: email or portal
- Recipients: all investors
- Archives: available on portal

### 7.2 Investor Q&A

**FAQ Document**
- Common: frequently asked questions
- Answered: clear responses
- Updated: quarterly
- Distributed: to all investors
- Portal: available online

### 7.3 Material Events

**Announcement Process**
- Event: material development
- Determination: is it material?
- Announcement: simultaneous to all
- Details: full disclosure
- Timing: as soon as practicable

**Material Events**
- Financing: new funding round
- M&A: acquisition or merger
- Leadership: key executive changes
- Losses: significant losses
- Litigation: material litigation
- Disasters: force majeure events
- Products: major product launch
- Partnerships: major partnerships

## 8. Compliance & Legal

### 8.1 Securities Law Compliance

**Federal**
- SEC Rules: Regulation FD (Fair Disclosure)
- Insider trading: prohibited
- MNPI: careful handling
- Form 4: executive transactions
- Proxy statements: if applicable

**State**
- State laws: applicable state regulations
- Filings: as required
- Notices: to state authorities

### 8.2 Insider Trading Policy

**Blackout Periods**
- During: financial close (month-end)
- Until: 24 hours after disclosure
- Exception: pre-planned trades
- Officer: more restrictive windows

### 8.3 Legal Review

- Draft: all materials reviewed
- Disclaimer: forward-looking statements
- Accuracy: factual verification
- MNPI: identification and control
- Approval: legal sign-off required

## 9. Crisis Communication

### 9.1 Crisis Events

**Types of Crises**
- Financial: significant losses
- Regulatory: investigation or violation
- Personnel: executive departure
- Product: safety or quality issue
- Legal: major litigation

### 9.2 Crisis Response

**Immediate**
- Assess: situation and impact
- Containment: stop the bleeding
- Communication: to board/leadership
- Team: assemble IR team

**Within 24 Hours**
- Prepare: response statement
- Legal: review statement
- Announcement: make disclosure
- Investors: notify key investors
- Media: respond to inquiries

**Follow-Up**
- Updates: as situation develops
- Transparency: honest communication
- Recovery: outline remediation
- Learning: address future prevention
