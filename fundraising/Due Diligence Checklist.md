# Due Diligence Checklist

Last Updated: May 12, 2026

## 1. Due Diligence Overview

### 1.1 Purpose

This checklist ensures thorough investor due diligence review during capital raises.

### 1.2 Timing

- Pre-term sheet: initial screening
- Pre-commitment: full due diligence
- Post-commitment: final verification

## 2. Business & Operations

### 2.1 Business Model

- [ ] Business description: clearly articulated
- [ ] Revenue model: explained
- [ ] Customer acquisition: described
- [ ] Market: market size documented
- [ ] Traction: revenue/metrics
- [ ] Differentiation: competitive advantage
- [ ] Unit economics: LTV vs CAC

### 2.2 Market Analysis

- [ ] TAM: total addressable market estimated
- [ ] SAM: serviceable available market
- [ ] Market growth: trend documented
- [ ] Competition: competitive landscape
- [ ] Barriers: entry barriers identified
- [ ] Trends: market trends analyzed
- [ ] Regulatory: regulatory environment

### 2.3 Operations

- [ ] Facilities: location and condition
- [ ] Equipment: operational equipment
- [ ] Supplier: supplier agreements
- [ ] Customers: customer concentration
- [ ] Contracts: material contracts reviewed
- [ ] Insurance: insurance coverage verified
- [ ] Compliance: regulatory compliance

### 2.4 Products & Services

- [ ] Development: product development status
- [ ] Roadmap: future product plans
- [ ] Capabilities: current capabilities
- [ ] Performance: performance metrics
- [ ] Quality: quality assurance
- [ ] Support: customer support structure
- [ ] Pricing: pricing strategy

## 3. Financial Review

### 3.1 Financial Statements

- [ ] Income statement: last 3 years
- [ ] Balance sheet: last 3 years
- [ ] Cash flow: last 3 years
- [ ] Prepared by: audited or reviewed
- [ ] Notes: significant items explained
- [ ] Audit: clean opinions or caveats
- [ ] Variances: significant variances explained

### 3.2 Financial Performance

- [ ] Revenue: growth rates analyzed
- [ ] Profitability: margins calculated
- [ ] Burn rate: monthly burn if not profitable
- [ ] Runway: months of cash remaining
- [ ] Unit: economics verified
- [ ] Forecasts: management forecasts reviewed
- [ ] Assumptions: forecast assumptions validated

### 3.3 Accounting & Controls

- [ ] Auditor: external auditor information
- [ ] Policies: accounting policies sound
- [ ] Controls: internal controls documented
- [ ] Systems: accounting systems adequate
- [ ] Records: financial records organized
- [ ] Tax: tax positions documented
- [ ] Compliance: tax compliance verified

### 3.4 Capitalization Table

- [ ] Cap table: current cap table obtained
- [ ] Shareholders: all shareholders identified
- [ ] Shares: share ownership documented
- [ ] Options: outstanding options listed
- [ ] Restrictions: transfer restrictions noted
- [ ] Preferences: liquidation preferences
- [ ] Dilution: fully diluted ownership calculated

### 3.5 Projections

- [ ] Three-year: 3-year projections
- [ ] Assumptions: underlying assumptions
- [ ] Reasonableness: assumptions reasonable
- [ ] Methodology: projection methodology sound
- [ ] Conservative: appropriately conservative
- [ ] Sensitivities: scenarios modeled
- [ ] Track record: management forecast track record
