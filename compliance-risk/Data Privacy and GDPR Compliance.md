# Data Privacy and GDPR Compliance Documentation

Last Updated: May 12, 2026

## 1. Overview

### 1.1 Purpose
This document outlines Lutervyn's commitment to data privacy and our specific procedures for ensuring compliance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and other applicable privacy laws.

### 1.2 Scope
Applies to all personal data processed by Lutervyn, whether of employees, customers, or partners.

## 2. Core Principles

- **Lawfulness, Fairness, and Transparency**: Data is processed legally and users are informed.
- **Purpose Limitation**: Data is only collected for specified, explicit, and legitimate purposes.
- **Data Minimization**: Only the data necessary for the purpose is collected.
- **Accuracy**: Data must be kept up to date.
- **Storage Limitation**: Data is deleted when no longer needed.
- **Integrity and Confidentiality**: Data is kept secure.

## 3. Data Subject Rights (DSR)

Lutervyn supports the following rights for data subjects:
- **Right to Access**: Users can request a copy of their data.
- **Right to Rectification**: Users can correct inaccurate data.
- **Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten")**: Users can request data deletion.
- **Right to Portability**: Users can request their data in a machine-readable format.
- **Right to Object**: Users can object to certain types of processing (e.g., marketing).

### 3.1 DSR Request Process
1. Request received via privacy@lutervyn.com.
2. Identity of the requester verified.
3. Request logged and assigned to the Data Privacy Officer (DPO).
4. Data gathered/action taken within 30 days.
5. Confirmation sent to the requester.

## 4. Data Protection Impact Assessments (DPIA)

- A DPIA must be conducted for any new processing activity that is likely to result in a high risk to the rights and freedoms of individuals.
- Includes:
  - Description of the processing.
  - Assessment of necessity and proportionality.
  - Risk assessment.
  - Measures to mitigate risks.

## 5. Data Processing Agreements (DPA)

- All third-party vendors (Processors) who handle Lutervyn data must sign a DPA.
- The DPA must include standard contractual clauses (SCCs) where applicable for international transfers.

## 6. Training and Awareness

- All employees must undergo annual privacy and GDPR training.
- Specialized training provided for teams handling sensitive data (HR, Sales, Support).

## 7. Breach Notification Protocol

- In the event of a personal data breach:
  - The DPO must be notified immediately.
  - If the breach is likely to result in a risk to individuals, the relevant Supervisory Authority must be notified within 72 hours.
  - Affected individuals must be notified without undue delay if the breach is high-risk.
