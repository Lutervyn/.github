# Risk Management Framework

Last Updated: May 12, 2026

## 1. Introduction

### 1.1 Purpose
To establish a consistent process for identifying, assessing, and managing risks across all Lutervyn business operations.

### 1.2 Definition of Risk
Any event or circumstance that could negatively impact Lutervyn's ability to achieve its strategic objectives, protect its assets, or maintain its reputation.

## 2. Risk Categories

- **Strategic**: Competition, market shifts, failed partnerships.
- **Operational**: System failures, human error, supply chain disruptions.
- **Financial**: Currency fluctuations, credit risk, budget overruns.
- **Legal & Compliance**: Regulatory changes, lawsuits, fines.
- **Reputational**: Negative publicity, loss of customer trust.

## 3. The Risk Management Process

### 3.1 Identification
- Annual risk assessment workshops with department heads.
- Ongoing identification via "Risk Alerts" from any employee.
- Review of industry trends and external threats.

### 3.2 Assessment
Each risk is evaluated based on:
- **Likelihood**: Probability of the risk occurring (1-5).
- **Impact**: Severity of the consequence if it occurs (1-5).

**Risk Score = Likelihood * Impact**

### 3.3 Risk Matrix
- **Low (1-5)**: Monitor and manage through routine procedures.
- **Medium (6-12)**: Specific mitigation plans required; quarterly review.
- **High (15-25)**: Immediate action required; monthly board-level review.

## 4. Risk Treatment Options

- **Avoid**: Change plans to eliminate the risk entirely.
- **Mitigate**: Implement controls to reduce likelihood or impact.
- **Transfer**: Use insurance or contracts to shift the risk to a third party.
- **Accept**: Acknowledge the risk and decide not to take action (only for low-scoring risks).

## 5. Monitoring and Reporting

### 5.1 Risk Register
- A central document (Risk Registry) maintained by the Compliance team.
- Records all identified risks, their scores, owners, and mitigation status.

### 5.2 Reporting
- Monthly summary report to the Executive Team.
- Quarterly deep-dive report to the Board of Directors.

## 6. Roles and Responsibilities

- **Board of Directors**: Oversight of the overall risk appetite.
- **CEO/Executives**: Ultimate responsibility for managing key risks.
- **Department Heads**: Identification and mitigation of risks within their areas.
- **Compliance Team**: Maintaining the framework and register.
