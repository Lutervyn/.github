# Business Incident Response Plan

Last Updated: May 12, 2026

## 1. Overview

### 1.1 Purpose
To provide a structured approach for responding to non-technical incidents that threaten Lutervyn's operations, reputation, or personnel.

### 1.2 Scope
Includes, but is not limited to:
- Physical security breaches.
- Natural disasters affecting offices.
- Legal/Regulatory investigations.
- Public relations (PR) crises.
- Workplace safety incidents.
- Significant financial fraud.

## 2. Incident Response Team (IRT)

The Business IRT consists of:
- **CEO/COO**: Executive decision-maker.
- **Head of Legal**: Legal and regulatory guidance.
- **Head of Communications**: PR and internal messaging.
- **Head of HR**: Personnel and safety issues.
- **Head of Finance**: Financial impact and fraud investigation.

## 3. Incident Lifecycle

### 3.1 Detection & Reporting
- Any employee observing a significant incident must report it to their manager or via the #emergency-business Slack channel.
- Managers must escalate to the IRT within 30 minutes of detection.

### 3.2 Triage & Assessment
- The IRT meets (virtually or in-person) to determine the scope and severity.
- Initial assessment of impact on:
  - Life/Safety.
  - Legal/Compliance.
  - Brand/Reputation.
  - Financial Stability.

### 3.3 Containment & Mitigation
- Immediate steps to stop the situation from worsening.
- Examples:
  - Closing an office during a physical threat.
  - Issuing a "Holding Statement" to the press.
  - Suspending specific financial accounts.

### 3.4 Resolution & Recovery
- Implementing long-term solutions.
- Returning to "Business as Usual" (BAU).
- Communicating resolution to stakeholders.

### 3.5 Post-Incident Review
- Conducted within 5 business days of resolution.
- Document:
  - What happened and why.
  - Effectiveness of the response.
  - Lessons learned and required changes to policies/procedures.

## 4. Communication Protocols

### 4.1 Internal Communication
- Keep employees informed through official channels (email/Slack).
- Provide clear instructions on what to do (or not do).

### 4.2 External Communication
- **Only** authorized spokespeople (CEO/Head of Comms) may speak to the media or public.
- All inquiries from press or regulators must be forwarded to Legal/Comms immediately.

## 5. Contact Information

- **Emergency Services**: 911 (or local equivalent).
- **Lutervyn Security Desk**: security@lutervyn.com
- **Legal Hotline**: legal-emergency@lutervyn.com
