# Performance Review Procedures

Last Updated: May 12, 2026

## 1. Overview

Performance reviews at Lutervyn are designed to provide fair, transparent, and actionable feedback to support every employee's growth and success.

## 2. Review Cycle

### 2.1 Formal Reviews
- Conducted twice per year (H1 Review in July, H2 Review in January).

### 2.2 Continuous Feedback
- Managers are expected to hold weekly or bi-weekly 1-on-1 meetings with their direct reports to provide ongoing feedback and address issues in real-time.

## 3. The Review Process

### 3.1 Step 1: Self-Assessment
- Employees reflect on their accomplishments, challenges, and progress against their OKRs.

### 3.2 Step 2: 360-Degree Peer Feedback
- Feedback is gathered from 3-5 peers or collaborators to provide a well-rounded view of the employee's impact and teamwork.

### 3.3 Step 3: Manager Assessment
- Managers review self-assessments, peer feedback, and OKR data to write the final review.

### 3.4 Step 4: The Review Meeting
- A dedicated 1-hour meeting to discuss the feedback, celebrate successes, and identify areas for growth in the next cycle.

## 4. Evaluation Criteria

Employees are evaluated on:
- **Impact**: Achievement of OKRs and overall contribution to business goals.
- **Competency**: Technical and soft skills defined in the [Leveling Framework](../hr-development/Career%20Development%20Paths.md).
- **Values**: Demonstrating Lutervyn's core mission and values.

## 5. Ratings

- **Exceeds Expectations**: Significant impact beyond the requirements of the role.
- **Meets Expectations**: Consistent performance and impact at the expected level.
- **Developing**: Meeting some but not all requirements; clear plan for improvement.
- **Underperforming**: Significant gaps in performance requiring immediate action.

## 6. Underperformance

- Any "Underperforming" rating will trigger a formal **Performance Improvement Plan (PIP)** to provide structured support and clear targets for improvement over a 30-90 day period.
