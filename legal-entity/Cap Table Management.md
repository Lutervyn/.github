# Cap Table Management

Last Updated: May 12, 2026

## 1. Cap Table Overview

### 1.1 Purpose

The cap (capitalization) table tracks all ownership in Lutervyn Inc., including shareholders, shares, and valuations.

### 1.2 Responsible Party

- CFO: primary responsibility
- Secretary: records maintenance
- Finance: administrative support
- Board: oversight and approval

### 1.3 Confidentiality

- Confidential: not public disclosure
- Restricted: access limited
- Stockholders: personal holdings disclosed
- NDA: signers only

## 2. Cap Table Components

### 2.1 Capitalization Table Structure

**Columns**
- Shareholder name: full legal name
- Share class: Common, Series A, Series B, etc.
- Shares held: current holdings
- Percentage: ownership percentage
- Exercise price: for options
- Strike price: option exercise price
- Vesting: schedule and status
- Liquidation preference: if applicable
- Notes: special terms or restrictions

### 2.2 Share Classes

**Common Stock**
- Shares: [Number] issued
- Holders: founders and employees
- Voting: one vote per share
- Dividends: pro-rata if declared

**Series A Preferred**
- Shares: [Number] authorized, [Number] issued
- Price: $[Price] per share
- Investors: Series A investors
- Terms: [Specific terms]
- Liquidation: 1x preference

**Series B Preferred**
- Shares: [Number] authorized, [Number] issued
- Price: $[Price] per share
- Investors: Series B investors
- Terms: [Specific terms]
- Liquidation: 1x preference

**Options & Warrants**
- Outstanding: [Number] options
- Strike: average $[Price]
- Vesting: 4-year cliff/monthly
- Fully diluted: reflected in cap table

### 2.3 Stockholder Record

**Each Shareholder Entry**
- Name: legal name (match ID)
- Address: current address
- Tax ID: if applicable
- Shares: held in each class
- Certificate: number(s)
- Transfer: restricted or free
- Notes: special terms

## 4. Cap Table Updates

### 4.1 Triggering Events

**Events Requiring Updates**
- Stock issuance: new shares issued
- Share transfer: ownership change
- Stock split: share adjustment
- Exercise: option exercised
- Merger: combination event
- Acquisitions: purchase of stock
- Conversions: preferred to common

### 4.2 Update Process

**Steps**

4. Stock certificates: issued or amended
5. Cap table: updated
6. Stock ledger: changed
7. Records: filed
8. Stockholders: notified if relevant

### 4.3 Documentation

- Stock certificates: scanned copies

## 5. Ownership Tracking

### 5.1 Fully Diluted Analysis

**Includes**
- Issued shares: currently held
- Options outstanding: unexercised
- Reserved: for future issuance
- Warrants: if any
- Convertible: securities

**Example**:
- Common stock: 1,000,000 shares
- Series A: 500,000 shares
- Series B: 500,000 shares
- Options outstanding: 400,000
- Reserved pool: 200,000
- Total fully diluted: 2,600,000

### 5.2 Ownership Percentages

**Calculation**
- Fully diluted: use full dilution scenario
- Current: existing shares only
- As-converted: assuming all conversions
- Pro-rata: per share class

**Ownership Dilution**
- New round: calculation updated
- Down round: potential negative consequences
- Anti-dilution: protection mechanisms
- Analysis: quarterly review

### 5.3 Voting Control

**Majority Shareholders**
- Control: 50%+ of votes
- Protective provisions: if preferred
- Board seats: typically control
- Major transactions: approval required

**Blocking Shareholders**
- Super-majority: 66.67%+ provisions
- Veto power: on certain actions
- Key decisions: merger, liquidation
- Amendment: bylaw changes

## 6. Investor Rights & Preferences

### 6.1 Liquidation Preferences

**Series A Preferences**
- Amount: 1x invested amount
- Participating: or non-participating
- Seniority: Series A > Series B > Common
- Waterfall: distributed per preference

**Example Waterfall**:
1. Series A: receive 1x preference
2. Series B: receive 1x preference
3. Remaining: to common stockholders

### 6.2 Anti-Dilution Provisions

**Weighted Average**
- Formula: adjusts strike price
- Dilutive event: down round
- Broad-based: all shares included
- Narrow-based: limited shares

**Application**
- Down round: if new round < prior round
- Adjustment: downward strike price
- Conversion: preferred to common
- Effect: economic protection

### 6.3 Conversion Rights

**Automatic**
- Trigger: IPO or merger
- Effect: preferred converts to common
- Ratio: 1:1 conversion
- Timing: at closing of event

**Optional**
- Holder: may convert anytime
- Reason: better outcome
- Effect: loses preferences
- Documentation: executed

## 8. Corporate Actions & Cap Table

### 8.1 Stock Splits

**Effect on Cap Table**
- Shares: multiplied by split factor
- Price: adjusted inversely
- Percentage: unchanged
- Example: 2:1 split doubles shares

**Cap Table Update**
- All shares: adjusted
- Strike prices: adjusted
- Percentages: remain same
- History: tracked

### 8.2 Stock Dividends

**Effect on Cap Table**
- Paid: in additional shares
- Distributed: pro-rata
- Recorded: new certificate
- Cap table: updated with additional shares

### 8.3 Recapitalization

**Common Events**
- Reverse split: consolidate shares
- Conversion: preferred to common
- Reorganization: restructure ownership
- Effect: cap table significantly changed

**Cap Table Updates**
- All holders: affected
- Percentages: recalculated
- Classes: may change
- Historical: prior cap table retained

## 10. Financing Events

### 10.1 Funding Round

**Cap Table Impact**
- New class: series issued
- Investors: added to cap table
- Dilution: existing holders diluted
- Post-money: valuation calculated

**Pro Forma Cap Table**
- Before round: existing ownership
- Investment: new shares issued
- After round: diluted ownership
- Effect: typically 15-25% dilution

### 10.2 Valuation

**Post-Money Valuation**
- Formula: (new investment) / (% purchased)
- Example: $5M investment for 20% = $25M post
- Cap table: effects all holders
- Implications: future rounds

### 10.3 Down Round

**Anti-Dilution Trigger**
- Down round: valuation < prior round
- Adjustment: anti-dilution protects holders
- Conversion: price adjusted lower
- Common: diluted significantly
