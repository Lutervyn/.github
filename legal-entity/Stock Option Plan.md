# Stock Option Plan

Last Updated: May 12, 2026

## 1. Plan Overview

### 1.1 Purpose

The Lutervyn Inc. 2024 Stock Option Plan (the "Plan") is established to:
- Attract qualified personnel
- Retain key employees and directors
- Align interests with stockholders
- Reward performance and achievement
- Provide tax-efficient compensation

### 1.2 Effective Date

January 1, 2024

### 1.3 Plan Duration

Perpetual until terminated by Board action or earlier resignation.

### 1.4 Administration

The Plan is administered by the Compensation Committee (or Board if no Committee).

## 2. Eligibility

### 2.1 Eligible Recipients

- Employees: full-time and part-time
- Directors: including non-employee directors
- Consultants: providing substantial services
- Advisors: with board approval

### 2.2 Participation

- Voluntary: employee's choice
- Selection: by administrator
- Timing: subject to plan availability
- Frequency: annual grants typical

### 2.3 Non-Eligible Participants

- Terminated employees: generally not eligible
- Former employees: only if agreement states
- Contractors: may be eligible by board action

## 3. Authorized Shares

### 3.1 Share Authorization

- Total Shares: 1,000,000 shares
- Class: Common Stock
- Adjustment: for stock splits, dividends
- Reservation: shares reserved throughout plan term

### 3.2 Share Pool

- Reserved: 1,000,000 shares
- Allocation: to option grants
- Availability: as options granted
- Reset: as options expire/terminate
- Accounting: treated as outstanding stock

## 4. Option Grants

### 4.1 Grant Authority

Granted by:
- Board of Directors: full authority
- Compensation Committee: delegated authority
- CEO: within parameters
- Committee Charter: specific provisions

### 4.2 Option Agreement

**Required Terms**
- Option type: Incentive or Non-Qualified
- Number of shares: specified quantity
- Exercise price: fair market value (FMV)
- Vesting schedule: specified
- Exercise period: 10 years standard
- Acceleration: change of control
- Termination: upon separation

### 4.3 Grant Timing

- Annual: typical grant date
- Regular: by board determination
- Special: for new hires
- Frequency: multiple per year permitted
- Documentation: formal agreement

## 5. Strike Price

### 5.1 Exercise Price Determination

**Methodology**
- Fair Market Value: board determination
- Valuation: independent valuation
- Date: grant date used
- IRS 409A: compliant valuation
- Documentation: board resolution

### 5.2 Minimum Price

- Cannot be: below fair market value
- Fair value: required
- Audit: by committee
- Lower bound: $0.001 minimum (par value)

### 5.3 Price Adjustments

- Anti-dilution: for stock splits
- Adjustment: automatic
- Recapitalization: permitted
- Merger: adjustment required

## 6. Vesting Schedule

### 6.1 Standard Vesting

**Vesting Formula**
- Total term: 4 years
- Cliff period: 1 year
- Monthly vesting: following cliff
- Schedule: 1/48th per month
- Acceleration: upon certain events

**Example Schedule**
- Year 1: 25% vest at one-year anniversary
- Years 2-4: 6.25% vest each quarter
- Fully vested: at four-year mark

### 6.2 Partial Vesting

**Pro-Rata Vesting**
- Termination: pro-rata through date
- Separation: acceleration does not apply
- Cliff: if not satisfied before termination

### 6.3 Accelerated Vesting

**Triggers**
- Change of control: 100% acceleration
- Death: 100% acceleration
- Disability: 100% acceleration
- Termination without cause: 25%-50% acceleration

**Definition: Change of Control**
- Sale: of 50%+ stock or assets
- Merger: combination with another entity
- Liquidation: dissolution and asset sale
- Recapitalization: material change in control

## 7. Exercise of Options

### 7.1 Exercise Process

**Steps**
1. Option vested: share has vested
2. Notice: to company of intent
3. Payment: of exercise price
4. Stock certificate: issued or electronic
5. Records: updated by secretary

### 7.2 Exercise Methods

**Payment Options**
- Cash: payment at exercise
- Stock swap: existing stock exchanged
- Cashless exercise: broker assisted
- Variable prepaid forward: planned exercise
- Net issuance: shares retained

### 7.3 Exercise Period

- Grant period: options granted
- Expiration: 10 years from grant
- Termination: limited exercise period
- Outstanding: up to expiration date

### 7.4 Exercise Upon Termination

**Termination of Employment**
- Vested options: may exercise within 90 days
- Unvested options: immediately forfeit
- Good reason: 90-day exercise window
- Cause: forfeited immediately

**Death or Disability**
- Vested: may exercise within 12 months
- Unvested: accelerate to 100% vesting
- Beneficiary: may exercise rights

## 8. Taxes & Withholding

### 8.1 Incentive Stock Options (ISOs)

**Tax Treatment**
- No tax: at grant
- No tax: at exercise (within limits)
- Tax: at sale
- Holding: 2-year grant, 1-year exercise
- Favorable: long-term capital gains rate

**Requirements**
- Employee status: required
- Exercise price: fair market value
- Vesting: not accelerated
- $100,000: annual limit (5-year aggregate)

### 8.2 Non-Qualified Options (NSOs)

**Tax Treatment**
- No tax: at grant
- Income tax: at exercise (spread)
- Capital gains: at disposition
- W-2: income reported
- FICA: payroll taxes apply

### 8.3 Withholding Obligations

**Withholding Required**
- Exercise: income tax withholding
- FICA: employer and employee portions
- Method: from cash, shares, or net issuance
- Amount: withholding calculation

**Withholding Process**
- Calculation: income at exercise
- Notification: to optionholder
- Method: selected by holder
- Company: satisfies tax obligations

### 8.4 Code Section 409A

**Compliance Required**
- Options: designed to comply
- Valuation: 409A-compliant
- Changes: limited or non-compliant
- Acceleration: must comply
- Amendment: to comply if needed

## 9. Restrictions & Limitations

### 9.1 No Rights as Stockholder

- Options only: until exercise
- Voting: not granted until stock issued
- Dividends: not received on unexercised options
- Acceleration: upon corporate event only

### 9.2 Option Transfer Restrictions

- No transfer: generally prohibited
- Family transfer: permitted for estate planning
- Pledge: not permitted
- Exercise: only by optionholder

### 9.3 Forfeiture

**Events Triggering Forfeiture**
- Termination for cause: immediate
- Termination without vesting: forfeited
- Death: accelerated then exercisable
- Voluntary departure: after 90 days
- Separation: before vesting

## 10. Amendment & Termination

### 10.1 Plan Amendments

**Authority**
- Board: may amend plan
- Stockholders: may require approval
- Material: stockholder approval required
- Examples: increased shares, benefit changes

### 10.2 No Automatic Changes

- Price adjustment: only for approved events
- Terms: not automatically adjusted
- Amendment: required for changes

### 10.3 Plan Termination

**Termination Rights**
- Board: may terminate
- No new grants: if termination effective
- Existing options: continue per terms
- Outstanding options: remain exercisable

## 11. Change of Control

### 11.1 Treatment Upon Acquisition

**Conversion/Assumption**
- Assumed: by acquirer on similar terms
- Converted: to acquirer options
- Substitute: replacement awards
- Alternatives: cash-out or acceleration

### 11.2 Double Trigger Acceleration

**Conditions**
- Change of control: first trigger
- Termination: within 12 months of control change
- Acceleration: 100% upon both triggers
- Single trigger: not automatic

### 11.3 Cash-Out Consideration

- Fair value: per option terms
- Payment: cash or acquirer stock
- Formula: exercise price to fair market value
- Timing: at change of control closing

- Adjust: for corporate events
- Determine: vesting and terms
- Records: maintain complete records

### 12.2 Committee Composition

- Delegation: permitted by committee
- Secretary: records minutes

### 12.3 Record Keeping

- Kept: by corporation
- Retention: 7 years minimum
- Accessible: to optionholders upon request
- Documentation: plan administration files
