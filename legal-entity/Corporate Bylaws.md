# Corporate Bylaws

Last Updated: May 12, 2026

## 1. Stockholders

### 1.1 Annual Meeting

**Date and Time**
- Held: in May of each year
- Time: 10:00 AM
- Location: principal office or as determined
- Date: second Tuesday of May

**Purpose**
- Elect directors
- Ratify auditors
- Consider stockholder proposals
- Other business: as appropriate

### 1.2 Special Meetings

**Authority to Call**
- President: may call
- Secretary: may call
- Stockholders: 25%+ may request
- Board: may call

**Notice**
- Time: 10 days minimum
- Medium: written notice
- Content: date, time, place, purpose
- Delivery: email or certified mail

### 1.3 Notice of Meeting

- Mailed: at least 10 days prior
- Contents: date, time, location
- Purpose: stated in notice
- Proxy: information included
- Delivery: to record holders

### 1.4 Quorum

- Required: majority of shares
- Presence: in person or by proxy
- Conduct: by stockholders present
- Postponement: for lack of quorum

### 1.5 Voting

**Entitled Shareholders**
- Record date: determined by board
- Shares: held of record
- Voting list: prepared by secretary
- Inspection: stockholder right

**Voting Methods**
- In person: voice vote
- Proxy: written authorization
- Ballot: if multiple candidates
- Cumulative: not permitted

**Majority Vote**
- Directors: plurality of votes cast
- Other matters: majority of votes cast
- Proxy: may vote for/against/abstain
- Record: stockholder votes recorded

### 1.6 Action Without Meeting

- Permitted: if unanimous written consent
- Delivery: written consent to secretary
- Effective: upon delivery
- Record: filed with minutes

### 1.7 Remote Communication

- Permitted: stockholder meetings by remote communication
- Technology: secure and accessible
- Participation: full participation required
- Record: maintained of proceedings

## 2. Board of Directors

### 2.1 General Powers

The board of directors shall have the authority:
- Manage: business and affairs
- Issue: stock and set terms
- Declare: dividends
- Purchase: company stock
- Borrow: money on credit
- Mortgage: company assets
- Adopt: bylaws
- Appoint: officers and agents

### 2.2 Number of Directors

- Minimum: 1 director
- Maximum: 11 directors
- Current: 5 directors
- Change: by board or stockholder action

### 2.3 Election of Directors

**Election Process**
- Annual: at stockholder meeting
- Plurality: of votes cast
- Classes: may be established
- Term: until successor elected

**Removal and Replacement**
- Removal: with or without cause
- Stockholder: may remove by majority
- Vacancy: board may fill
- Successor: serves until next election

### 2.4 Qualifications

- None: statutorily required
- Age: no requirement
- Citizenship: U.S. not required
- Stock: ownership not required

### 2.5 Board Meetings

**Regular Meetings**
- Scheduled: by board action
- Notice: not required if scheduled
- Location: principal office typical
- Frequency: quarterly minimum

**Special Meetings**
- Called by: president, secretary, or director
- Notice: 24 hours advance
- Medium: telephonic acceptable
- Purpose: stated in notice

**Remote Participation**
- Permitted: by telephone or videoconference
- Notice: participants identified
- Participation: counts for quorum
- Minutes: recorded

### 2.6 Quorum

- Required: majority of directors
- Presence: physical or remote
- Participation: entitled to vote
- Absence: meeting may not proceed

### 2.7 Action and Voting

**Voting**
- Each: one vote per director
- Proxy: not permitted
- Secret ballot: if requested
- Record: maintained

**Board Action**
- Majority: of quorum required
- Tie vote: motion fails
- Abstention: counted present
- Recusal: for conflicts

### 2.8 Board Committees

**Committees Permitted**
- Audit Committee: required
- Compensation Committee: typical
- Nominating Committee: typical
- Others: as established

**Committee Authority**
- Delegated: full board authority
- Except: merger, stock issuance, bylaw changes
- Chair: appointed by board
- Meetings: per committee charter

### 2.9 Directors' Fees

- Compensation: as determined by board
- Cash: annual retainer
- Equity: option grants typical
- Reimbursement: expenses incurred

### 2.10 Indemnification

- Indemnify: directors, officers, employees
- Extent: maximum permitted by law
- Insurance: company may purchase
- Limitation: prohibited conduct excluded

### 2.11 Removal of Directors

**Cause**
- For: breach of duty
- For: gross negligence
- For: malfeasance
- Process: notice and opportunity

**Without Cause**
- Stockholders: may remove by majority
- Effect: immediate upon vote
- Replacement: board may appoint

## 3. Officers

### 3.1 Officers of the Corporation

- President
- Secretary
- Treasurer
- Vice President (optional)
- Chief Financial Officer (optional)
- Chief Executive Officer (optional)

### 3.2 Chief Executive Officer

**Authority**
- General management: of company
- Execution: of board decisions
- Delegation: of duties to officers
- Representation: of company

**Appointment**
- Elected: by board of directors
- Term: indefinite, at board pleasure
- Removal: by board vote

### 3.3 President

**Authority**
- Leadership: of company operations
- Presides: over board meetings (typical)
- Sign: contracts and documents
- Reports: to board and stockholders

**Appointment**
- Selected: by board or CEO
- Term: at pleasure of board
- Removal: by board

### 3.4 Secretary

**Duties**
- Maintain: minute books
- Prepare: notices of meetings
- Keep: stock transfer records
- Authenticate: company documents
- Custodian: of corporate seal

**Appointment**
- Elected: by board
- Term: at board pleasure
- Removal: by board

### 3.5 Treasurer

**Duties**
- Custodian: of funds
- Deposited: in company banks
- Accounting: of all transactions
- Reports: financial statements
- Records: financial records

**Appointment**
- Elected: by board
- Term: at board pleasure
- Removal: by board

### 3.6 Vice Presidents

- Authority: as delegated
- Duties: as assigned
- Succession: in absence of president
- Term: at board pleasure

### 3.7 Other Officers

- Appointed: by board or CEO
- Authority: as delegated
- Duties: as specified
- Removal: at will

### 3.8 Officer Bonds

- Required: as board determines
- Coverage: embezzlement insurance
- Amount: as appropriate
- Carrier: approved company

### 3.9 Removal of Officers

- By: board of directors
- Cause: not required
- Notice: not required (typically)
- Authority: board authority

## 4. Stock

### 4.1 Stock Certificates

- Issued: upon full payment
- Signed: by president and secretary
- Seal: corporate seal affixed
- Shares: represented by certificate
- Electronic: may be dematerialized

### 4.2 Transfer Restrictions

- Notice: to secretary required
- Approval: not required (typically)
- Restrictions: noted on certificate
- Lock-ups: per stockholder agreements

### 4.3 Lost, Stolen, or Destroyed Certificates

- Replacement: issued upon request
- Indemnification: holder may be required
- Affidavit: may be required
- Fee: not charged

### 4.4 Stock Ledger

- Maintained: by secretary or transfer agent
- Information: stockholder name, shares
- Updated: upon each transfer
- Reconciled: to certificates

## 5. Dividends

### 5.1 Declaration of Dividends

- Authority: board of directors
- Conditions: when permissible
- Amount: at board discretion
- Reserves: as board deems appropriate

### 5.2 Payment of Dividends

- Paid: in cash or stock
- Record date: established by board
- Payment date: established by board
- Method: specified by board

## 6. Fiscal Year

- Year-end: December 31
- Accounting: calendar year
- Reports: annual financial statements
- Audit: external audit annual

## 7. Corporate Seal

- Adopted: by board action
- Custody: with secretary
- Use: on official documents
- Design: company logo and name

## 8. Amendments to Bylaws

### 8.1 Authority to Amend

- Board: may adopt new bylaws
- Board: may amend existing bylaws
- Board: may repeal bylaws
- Stockholders: may adopt, amend, repeal

### 8.2 Amendment Process

- Notice: stockholders or board
- Consideration: at meeting
- Vote: stockholder or board action
- Effective: upon adoption

### 8.3 Stockholder Override

- Stockholders: may repeal board amendments
- Requires: majority of shares
- Effective: upon vote
- Notice: of amendment

## 9. Conflict of Interest

### 9.1 Director Conflicts

- Disclosure: required
- Abstain: from voting
- Interested party: not present during discussion
- Record: minutes reflect disclosure

### 9.2 Contracts with Directors

- Permitted: if properly disclosed
- Fair: terms must be fair to corporation
- Approval: by disinterested directors or stockholders
- Documentation: agreement preserved
