# Acceptable Use Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2024
Last Updated: May 12, 2024

## 1. Purpose

This Acceptable Use Policy ("AUP") defines the standards of acceptable and unacceptable use of Lutervyn services. These policies protect our infrastructure, users, and the broader internet community.

## 2. Prohibited Activities

### 2.1 Illegal Content and Activities

You may not use the Services to:

- Engage in illegal activities or encourage others to do so
- Violate any local, state, national, or international law
- Commit fraud, forgery, or misrepresentation
- Engage in money laundering or financial crime
- Facilitate terrorism, violence, or dangerous activities
- Violate export control laws or sanctions
- Distribute or download illegal content
- Engage in child exploitation or abuse
- Commit trademark, copyright, or patent violations

### 2.2 Security Violations

You may not:

- Gain unauthorized access to services or systems
- Attempt to access restricted areas
- Conduct security testing without authorization
- Deploy malware, worms, viruses, or harmful code
- Engage in denial-of-service (DoS/DDoS) attacks
- Attempt to compromise system integrity
- Use known or zero-day vulnerabilities maliciously
- Engage in port scanning or network reconnaissance
- Brute force credentials or authentication systems
- Social engineer employees or users
- Engage in packet sniffing or traffic interception
- Attempt to interfere with network operations

### 2.3 Intellectual Property Violations

You may not:

- Upload, post, or distribute copyrighted content without permission
- Violate trade secrets or confidential information
- Infringe on patent rights
- Infringe on trademark rights
- Distribute pirated software or media
- Remove or alter copyright or license information
- Create derivative works without authorization
- Plagiarize others' content or code
- Pass off others' work as your own
- Violate open source license terms

### 2.4 Harassment and Abusive Conduct

You may not:

- Harass, threaten, or intimidate other users or employees
- Send unsolicited threatening or harassing messages
- Engage in cyberstalking or targeted abuse
- Dox or reveal personal information of others
- Post abusive, hateful, or violent content
- Discriminate against others based on protected characteristics
- Engage in targeted harassment campaigns
- Make unwanted sexual advances or content
- Engage in mob harassment or pile-ons
- Threaten or encourage violence against individuals or groups
- Post or share intimate images without consent

### 2.5 Spam and Abuse of Services

You may not:

- Send spam messages or unwanted communications
- Engage in bulk email campaigns without consent
- Manipulate search rankings or metrics
- Artificially inflate engagement or votes
- Engage in click fraud or impression fraud
- Phish for information or credentials
- Send unsolicited advertisements
- Abuse automated systems or bots inappropriately
- Flood systems with requests (rate limiting abuse)
- Engage in automated scraping without permission
- Create accounts for spamming purposes
- Manipulate discussions or voting systems

### 2.6 Exploitation and Abuse

You may not:

- Exploit or abuse system resources
- Run cryptocurrency mining on shared infrastructure
- Conduct OSINT or reconnaissance for malicious purposes
- Create or distribute methods to circumvent security
- Engage in extortion or blackmail
- Attempt to compromise other users' accounts
- Use Services as part of an attack infrastructure
- Engage in privacy violations or data harvesting
- Attempt to breach data security measures
- Engage in unauthorized access to data

## 3. Prohibited Content

You may not post, upload, or distribute:

- Child sexual abuse material (CSAM) or child exploitation content
- Non-consensual intimate imagery
- Graphic violence or gore
- Terrorist content or extremist propaganda
- Content promoting illegal activities
- Malware, trojans, or exploits
- Phishing content or scam materials
- Hate speech or discriminatory content
- Doxxing information
- Instructions for self-harm or suicide
- Illegal weapons or explosive information
- Stolen data or passwords
- Fake identity or impersonation content

## 4. Enforcement

### 4.1 Suspension and Termination

We may, at our sole discretion:

- Warn users of violations
- Suspend accounts immediately for serious violations
- Terminate accounts for repeated violations
- Remove violating content
- Reset passwords or lock accounts
- Restrict access to specific features
- Implement rate limiting

### 4.2 Investigation

We investigate violations thoroughly:

- We gather evidence and documentation
- We interview affected parties
- We review logs and metadata
- We consult with legal counsel
- We determine appropriate action
- We document findings

### 4.3 Escalation

Serious violations may result in:

- Permanent account termination
- IP address banning
- Referral to law enforcement
- Civil litigation
- Public disclosure of violations
- Cooperation with authorities

## 5. Resource Usage

### 5.1 Bandwidth and Storage

You agree not to:

- Exceed reasonable bandwidth quotas
- Use storage for non-affiliated purposes
- Store illegal or harmful content
- Distribute large volumes of unsolicited content
- Engage in large-scale backups without permission
- Mirror content without authorization
- Stream media excessively

### 5.2 Computational Resources

You may not:

- Engage in cryptocurrency mining
- Run resource-intensive processes without authorization
- Engage in distributed computing for unauthorized purposes
- Use shared resources for commercial purposes without license
- Perform penetration testing without authorization
- Run brute force attacks
- Engage in large-scale automated requests

### 5.3 Service Quotas

Services have usage limits:

- API rate limits as specified
- Storage limits per plan tier
- Concurrent connection limits
- Request size limitations
- Concurrent operation limits
- Monthly usage caps for free tier

## 6. Open Source Guidelines

For open source contributions:

- Respect existing licenses and copyright
- Follow project contribution guidelines
- Ensure code quality and security
- Don't introduce malicious code
- Attribute third-party code properly
- Follow coding standards
- Document changes clearly
- Don't violate contributor agreements

## 7. Monitoring and Enforcement

### 7.1 Detection Methods

We use:

- Automated threat detection
- Manual review and investigation
- User reports and flags
- Security scanning tools
- Log analysis and forensics
- Behavioral analysis
- Threat intelligence feeds

### 7.2 Response Times

- Critical security issues: immediate response
- Abuse reports: 24-48 hours initial review
- Legal requests: per legal procedures
- Appeals: 5-10 business days

## 8. Appeals Process

### 8.1 Submitting an Appeal

If your account is suspended or terminated:

1. Contact legal@lutervyn.com with "APPEAL" in subject
2. Include your account information
3. Provide detailed explanation
4. Submit supporting evidence
5. Include contact information

### 8.2 Appeal Review

- Separate reviewer evaluates appeal
- We review original decision
- We consider new evidence
- We provide written decision
- We may reinstate, maintain, or modify decision

### 8.3 Appeal Timelines

- Appeals accepted within 30 days
- Decision within 10-15 business days
- Final decision is binding
- Limited to one appeal per incident

## 9. Reporting Violations

### 9.1 How to Report

Report violations via:

- In-app abuse report button
- Email: abuse@lutervyn.com
- GitHub issue with `abuse` label
- Online form: https://lutervyn.pages.dev/report-abuse
- Phone: +1 (555) 123-4567 (emergency only)

### 9.2 What to Include

Provide:

- Description of violation
- Links or evidence
- Your contact information
- Date and time of incident
- Screenshots or documentation
- Any context needed for investigation

### 9.3 Investigation Process

- We acknowledge receipt
- We investigate thoroughly
- We gather evidence
- We take appropriate action
- We follow up with reporter
- We maintain confidentiality

### 10.2 Repeated Violations
