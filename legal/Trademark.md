## 5. Prohibited Uses

Do NOT use Lutervyn trademarks:

- As part of your own brand or company name
- To suggest endorsement not given
- In domain names (without permission)
- As product names
- To mislead about affiliation
- In meta tags or keywords for SEO
- In apps or software names
- Registered as your own
- In a way suggesting partnership

## 7. Permission Requests

### 7.1 When Permission Needed

Request permission to use Lutervyn marks for:

- Company/product names
- Merchandise or materials
- Significant commercial use
- Domain names
- Apps and software
- Events and sponsorship
- Any non-referential use

### 7.2 How to Request

**Email:** trademark@lutervyn.com

**Include:**
1. Intended use description
2. Context and purpose
3. Website/materials mockups
4. Approximate duration
5. Geographic scope
6. Your contact information
7. Legal entity details

### 7.3 Review Process

1. **Submission**: Request received
2. **Review**: 5-10 business days
3. **Decision**: Approved, conditional, or denied
4. **Agreement**: License agreement if approved
5. **Compliance**: Ongoing monitoring

### 7.4 Timeline

- Simple requests: 3-5 days
- Standard requests: 5-10 days
- Complex requests: 2-4 weeks
- Appeals: 10-15 days
