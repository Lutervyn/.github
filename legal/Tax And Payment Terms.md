# Tax and Payment Terms

Last Updated: May 12, 2024
Effective Date: January 1, 2024

## 1. Invoicing

### 1.1 Invoice Format

Invoices include:

- Invoice number and date
- Billing period
- Services rendered
- Amount due
- Due date
- Payment instructions
- Tax identification numbers
- Company information

### 1.2 Invoice Delivery

- Sent electronically
- Via email
- Available in account portal
- On monthly basis
- Within 5 business days of period end

## 2. Payment Terms

### 2.1 Payment Deadline

- Due within 30 days of invoice
- Net 30 terms
- Late payment subject to interest
- 1.5% monthly (18% annual)
- Or maximum allowed by law

### 2.2 Late Payment

- Late fees apply if not paid
- Interest calculated daily
- Compounded monthly
- Collection costs recovered
- Dunning notifications sent

## 3. Accepted Payment Methods

- Credit card (Visa, Mastercard, Amex)
- Bank transfer (ACH, wire)
- Check (if agreed)
- PayPal (if agreed)
- Other methods on agreement

## 4. Tax Obligations

### 4.1 Tax Responsibility

Customer responsible for:

- Sales tax
- Value-added tax (VAT)
- Goods and services tax (GST)
- Use tax
- Any applicable taxes
- Unless exempt

### 4.2 Tax-Exempt Status

To claim exemption:

- Provide tax exempt certificate
- Valid in applicable jurisdiction
- Updated annually
- Liability if invalid
- Backfill taxes if revoked

### 4.3 Tax Identification

Customer must provide:

- Federal tax ID (EIN)
- State tax ID if applicable
- VAT number (if international)
- Certification of accuracy
- Updated information

## 5. Invoice Disputes

### 5.1 Dispute Process

If invoice disputed:

- Notify within 30 days
- Provide detailed explanation
- Supporting documentation
- Evidence of error
- Good faith dispute

### 5.2 Resolution

- Investigation conducted
- Adjustment if warranted
- Credit issued if overpayment
- Dispute fees may apply
- Final decision binding

## 6. Deductions and Credits

### 6.1 Early Payment Discount

- 2% discount if paid within 10 days
- Applied upon payment
- Net 30 standard terms
- Automatically calculated

### 6.2 Credits and Adjustments

- Applied to next invoice
- Or refunded if requested
- Must be claimed within 90 days
- Documentation required

## 7. Promotional Pricing

### 7.1 Discounts

- Applied as specified
- For duration specified
- Subject to terms
- Not combinable
- Expires on specified date

### 7.2 Volume Discounts

- Based on usage level
- Applied automatically
- Reviewed monthly
- Calculated on invoice
- Subject to agreement

## 8. Refunds

### 8.1 Refund Policy

- Service credits available
- Used within 12 months
- Non-refundable upon expiration
- For paid plans
- Subject to terms

### 8.2 Early Termination

- No refund of paid fees
- Except as specified
- Service continues until period end
- Or immediate if breach
- Credit for unused service

## 9. Currency and Exchange

- Charges in US dollars (USD)
- Or as agreed
- Currency conversion
- At current exchange rate
- On invoice date
- Bank fees on international transfers

## 10. Price Changes

### 10.1 Notice

- 30 days notice of price change
- Via email notification
- Posted on website
- Effective on renewal
- Existing plans grandfathered 12 months

### 10.2 Acceptance

- Continued use = acceptance
- Or provide notice of cancellation
- Within 30 days
- No refund of prepaid fees

## 11. Payment Processing

### 11.1 Authorization

Customer authorizes:

- Charge for services
- Recurring charges
- As specified
- On account
- Until cancellation

### 11.2 Payment Gateway

- Processed through secure provider
- PCI DSS compliant
- Data encrypted
- Fraud detection
- Automatic retry on failure

## 12. Accounting and Records

### 12.1 Record Retention

- Records maintained 7 years
- Per tax requirements
- Available upon request
- In electronic format
- Confidentiality protected

### 12.2 Accounting Reports

- Available in account portal
- Downloadable format
- Monthly breakdown
- Year-to-date summary
- Export capability
