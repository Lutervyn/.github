# Data Sharing Agreement

Last Updated: May 12, 2024
Effective Date: January 1, 2024

## 1. Purpose

This Agreement governs sharing of data between Lutervyn and partners.

## 2. Definitions

**Shared Data** means data provided by one party to the other.

**Recipient** means party receiving Shared Data.

**Provider** means party providing Shared Data.

## 3. Data Categories

Shared Data includes:

- User information (with consent)
- Usage analytics
- Performance metrics
- Aggregated statistics
- De-identified data
- Metadata
- As agreed in Schedule A

## 4. Data Sharing Purpose

- Defined purpose only
- Listed in Schedule A
- No other use permitted
- No secondary use
- No combination with other data

## 5. Data Protection

Recipient shall:

- Protect data confidentially
- Use reasonable security measures
- Restrict access to authorized personnel
- Not combine with other datasets
- Implement technical safeguards
- Maintain audit trail

## 6. Permitted Use

Recipient may:

- Use for stated purpose
- Analyze for insights
- Create reports
- Share results (de-identified)
- Improve services
- As specified in agreement

## 7. Prohibited Use

Recipient shall not:

- Use for marketing without consent
- Sell or transfer to third parties
- Use for discrimination or targeting
- Reverse identify individuals
- Combine with other datasets
- Use beyond stated purpose

## 8. Data Quality

Provider represents:

- Data accuracy to best knowledge
- Lawful collection
- Valid consent obtained
- No violations of privacy law
- Current and maintained

## 9. Data Retention

- Retained for stated purpose only
- Deleted when no longer needed
- Upon request from Provider
- Within 30 days
- Certified deletion

## 10. Confidentiality

- Shared Data treated as confidential
- Not disclosed to third parties
- Restricted personnel access
- Secure storage required
- Encryption recommended

## 11. Security

Recipient shall implement:

- Encryption in transit (TLS)
- Encryption at rest
- Access controls
- Audit logging
- Intrusion detection
- Regular backups
- Incident response

## 12. Privacy Compliance

- GDPR compliance (if applicable)
- CCPA compliance (if applicable)
- Data subject rights supported
- Breach notification
- Privacy impact assessment
- Data Processing Agreement

## 13. Liability

- Recipient liable for unauthorized use
- Recipient liable for breaches
- Provider not liable for Recipient actions
- Indemnification for third-party claims
- Attorney's fees recoverable

## 14. Termination

- Either party may terminate
- 30 days written notice
- Immediate for material breach
- Data handling continues post-termination
- Confidentiality survives

## 15. Return or Deletion

Upon termination:

- Return all Shared Data
- Or delete at Provider request
- Certified deletion in writing
- Within 30 days
- Logs preserved if required

## 16. Dispute Resolution

- Good faith negotiation first
- Escalation to management
- Mediation if needed
- Legal action last resort
- Governing law specified

## 17. Contact

- Data Sharing: data@lutervyn.com
- Privacy: privacy@lutervyn.com
- Legal: legal@lutervyn.com
