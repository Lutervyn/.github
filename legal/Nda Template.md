# Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA) Template

Last Updated: May 12, 2024

## 1. Introduction

This Non-Disclosure Agreement ("NDA" or "Agreement") is entered into as of _____ ("Effective Date") between Lutervyn Inc. ("Disclosing Party") and _____________ ("Receiving Party").

## 2. Definition of Confidential Information

### 2.1 Confidential Information Includes

- Technical data and specifications
- Business plans and strategies
- Customer lists and information
- Pricing and financial information
- Software code and algorithms
- Trade secrets and know-how
- Project roadmaps
- Marketing strategies
- Any information marked "Confidential"
- Information disclosed verbally marked as confidential

### 2.2 Exclusions

Confidential Information does not include information that:

- Is or becomes publicly available (not through breach)
- Was known prior to disclosure
- Is independently developed
- Is rightfully received from third party
- Is required to be disclosed by law

## 3. Obligations

### 3.1 Protection

Receiving Party agrees to:

- Treat Confidential Information as secret
- Protect using reasonable care (minimum industry standard)
- Restrict access to employees with legitimate need
- Not disclose to third parties
- Not use for unauthorized purposes
- Use same care as for own confidential information

### 3.2 Use Restrictions

- Use only for stated purpose
- No commercial use without permission
- No modification without consent
- No reverse engineering
- No derivative works

## 4. Duration

- Term: _____ years from Effective Date
- Obligations survive termination
- For trade secrets: Duration of trade secret status
- For other information: _____ years after termination

## 5. Disclosure by Law

### 5.1 Required Disclosure

If disclosure is legally required:

- Notify Disclosing Party immediately
- Provide minimum advance notice
- Cooperate on protective order request
- Disclose only minimum necessary
- Request confidential treatment

### 5.2 Exception

No notice required if prohibited by law.

## 6. Return or Destruction

### 6.1 Upon Request

Within 30 days of request:

- Return all Confidential Information
- Destroy all copies
- Certify in writing
- Exception: One copy for legal compliance

### 6.2 Upon Termination

At termination of relationship:

- Return or destroy as requested
- Destroy all notes and summaries
- Certify destruction
- Retain only legally required copies

## 7. No License

- No license or rights granted
- No permission to use
- Ownership retained by Disclosing Party
- All rights reserved

## 8. Residual Knowledge

### 8.1 Exception

Employees may use residual knowledge:

- General ideas and concepts
- Without access to Confidential Information
- Not specific implementations
- Not without risk of liability

## 9. No Obligation

- No obligation to disclose
- No obligation to do business
- Evaluation is voluntary
- May not enter into transaction

## 10. Representations and Warranties

### 10.1 Authority

- Receiving Party has authority to enter agreement
- Will comply with all terms
- No conflicts with other agreements

### 10.2 Employees

- Will bind employees to confidentiality
- Will obtain written agreements
- Remains liable for breaches

## 11. No Warranty

- Information provided "AS IS"
- No accuracy warranties
- No completeness warranties
- No non-infringement warranties
- No fitness warranties

## 12. Remedies

### 12.1 Injunctive Relief

- Breach may cause irreparable harm
- Equitable relief available
- Not exclusive remedy
- Damages may not be adequate

### 12.2 Other Remedies

- Damages available
- Attorney's fees and costs
- Right to equitable relief
- Right to pursue all remedies

- No employment relationship
