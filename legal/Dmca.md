# Digital Millennium Copyright Act (DMCA) Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2024
Last Updated: May 12, 2024

## 1. Overview

Lutervyn respects intellectual property rights and complies with the Digital Millennium Copyright Act (DMCA) and similar international copyright laws. This policy outlines our procedures for handling copyright infringement claims and counter-notifications.

## 2. Copyright Infringement Claims

### 2.1 Notification Requirements

To report alleged copyright infringement, provide a written notice that includes:

**Mandatory Information:**

1. **Your Identification**: Physical or electronic signature

2. **Identification of Copyrighted Work**: Describe the work claimed to be infringed

3. **Location of Infringing Material**: Provide specific URLs or identifiers

4. **Your Contact Information**:
   - Name
   - Address
   - Telephone number
   - Email address
   - Physical or email signature

5. **Statement of Good Faith**: You have a good faith belief that use is not authorized

6. **Penalty Statement**: Acknowledge perjury penalties

7. **Authorization**: State you are authorized to act on behalf of the copyright holder

### 2.2 Where to Send Notices

Send DMCA notifications to:

**Email**: copyright@lutervyn.com

**Mailing Address**:
Lutervyn Inc.
Copyright Agent
123 Innovation Street
Tech City, TC 12345
Attn: Copyright Agent

**Include**: "DMCA Notice" in subject line

### 2.3 What Happens After We Receive Notice

1. **Verification**: We verify the notice contains required information

2. **Investigation**: We investigate the claim

3. **Access Removal**: If valid, we remove or disable access to material

4. **Notification**: We notify affected parties

5. **Documentation**: We maintain records of the DMCA process

6. **Counter-Notification**: We provide counter-notification information

## 3. Counter-Notification Procedure

### 3.1 When You Can File a Counter-Notice

If your content was removed and you believe:

- The removal was mistaken or improper
- You have authorization to use the material
- The claim is based on a misunderstanding
- The material does not infringe copyright
- You have a valid license or permission

You may file a counter-notification.

### 3.2 Counter-Notification Requirements

Your counter-notification must include:

1. **Your Identification**: Physical or electronic signature

2. **Identification of Removed Material**: Describe content and its location

3. **Statement Under Penalty of Perjury**: You have a good faith belief the removal was improper

4. **Your Contact Information**:
   - Name
   - Address
   - Telephone number
   - Email address

5. **Consent to Jurisdiction**: You consent to jurisdiction in appropriate venues

6. **Authorization Statement**: You will accept service of legal process

### 3.3 Counter-Notification Process

1. **Submission**: Send counter-notice to copyright@lutervyn.com

2. **Verification**: We verify the counter-notification

3. **Notification**: We notify the claimant

4. **Period**: Material remains removed for 10-14 business days

5. **Reinstatement**: Material is restored unless claimant files legal action

6. **Final Decision**: Legal action determines final outcome

## 4. Repeat Infringement Policy

### 4.1 Account Termination

Lutervyn will terminate accounts of repeat infringers. An account is considered a repeat infringer if:

- Multiple DMCA claims are substantiated
- The user has been notified previously
- Pattern of infringement continues
- User ignores warnings

### 4.2 Determination Process

1. We evaluate substantiated DMCA claims
2. We review user's history
3. We consider context and circumstances
4. We make termination decision
5. We provide written notice

## 5. Our Responsibilities

When we receive a valid DMCA notice, we:

- Promptly remove or disable access to content
- Send notices to content providers
- Document the process
- Preserve evidence
- Maintain records
- Follow statutory timelines
- Act expeditiously

## 6. Expedited Removal

### 6.1 Obvious Infringement

For clearly infringing content such as:

- Exact duplicates of copyrighted works
- Content clearly marked with copyright ownership
- Established copyrighted works
- Commercial piracy

We may remove content even without formal DMCA notice.

### 6.2 Abuse Prevention

We may refuse to process DMCA notices that appear to be:

- Harassment or abuse
- Frivolous or vexatious
- Made in bad faith
- Part of a pattern of abuse
- Factually insufficient

## 7. Copyright Licenses on Our Platform

### 7.1 Creative Commons

Content licensed under Creative Commons must:

- Display the appropriate CC license
- Follow license attribution requirements
- Respect license restrictions
- Not be misrepresented

### 7.2 Open Source Software

Open source projects:

- Must specify their license (in LICENSE file)
- Comply with license requirements
- Include license headers in source files
- Respect copyleft obligations
- Provide source code when required

### 7.3 Fair Use

We recognize fair use as a defense to copyright infringement for:

- Criticism and commentary
- News reporting
- Teaching, scholarship, research
- Parody
- Limited excerpts
- Transformative use

Fair use is analyzed case-by-case.

## 8. Trademark Issues

While DMCA covers copyright, we also address:

- Trademark infringement
- Brand misuse
- Confusingly similar use
- Commercial trademark violations

Report trademark issues to legal@lutervyn.com

## 9. Recording the DMCA Process

### 9.1 What We Track

- DMCA notice information
- Submitter information
- Material location
- Content description
- Actions taken
- Dates and times

- Appeals
- Outcomes

### 9.2 How Long We Keep Records

## 11. False Claims

### 11.1 Legal Liability

Under 17 U.S.C. § 512(f), submitting false DMCA notices subjects you to:

- Damages
- Costs and attorney's fees
- Enhanced damages for knowing falsity
- Potential criminal liability

### 11.2 Investigation of False Claims

We investigate apparent false claims:

- Analyzing substantiation
- Reviewing evidence
- Consulting legal counsel
- Preserving evidence
- Reporting to authorities if appropriate

## 12. Restoration After Improper Removal

### 12.1 Restoring Content

We restore content that was:

- Improperly removed
- Covered by valid counter-notification
- Subject to successful appeal
- Determined to be non-infringing

### 12.2 Compensation

For material we wrongfully remove:

- We restore as quickly as possible
- We provide written explanation
- We may offer appropriate remedies
- We review our processes

## 15. Clarifications

### 15.1 What DMCA Covers

DMCA applies to:

- Copyright infringement
- Circumvention of technological protection
- Removal of copyright management information
- Not trademark or patent (handled separately)

### 15.2 What DMCA Doesn't Cover

DMCA doesn't address:

- Contract disputes
- Trademark infringement
- Patent infringement
- Trade secret misappropriation
- Privacy violations
- Defamation claims
