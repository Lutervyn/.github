# Beta Tester Agreement

Last Updated: May 12, 2024
Effective Date: January 1, 2024

## 1. Purpose

This Agreement governs participation in Lutervyn's beta testing program.

## 2. Definitions

**Beta Features** means unreleased software and features provided under this program.

**Confidential Information** means all information about Beta Features marked confidential.

**Feedback** means comments, suggestions, reports provided by tester.

## 3. Beta Feature Access

- Access limited to authorized testers
- Non-transferable
- Non-exclusive
- Revocable at any time
- Solely for testing purposes

## 4. Confidentiality

Tester agrees:

- Keep Beta Features confidential
- Not disclose to third parties
- Use reasonable care
- Protect same as own confidential info
- Restrict access to employees

## 5. Restrictions

Tester shall not:

- Reverse engineer
- Decompile or disassemble
- Benchmark or compare
- Use for commercial purposes
- Modify or create derivatives
- Disclose specifications
- Use beyond testing scope

## 6. Feedback

- Tester provides feedback on Beta Features
- Company owns all feedback
- Company may use feedback freely
- No compensation for feedback
- Feedback not confidential

## 7. Testing Obligations

Tester shall:

- Test features thoroughly
- Report bugs and issues
- Provide detailed feedback
- Test per Company guidelines
- Participate in testing activities
- Complete surveys and assessments

## 8. Data and Privacy

- Tester may provide test data
- Company owns test data
- Company may analyze data
- Privacy policy applies
- Data handled securely

## 9. No Warranty

- Beta Features provided "AS IS"
- No warranty or support
- May be unstable or unreliable
- May cause data loss
- Beta status may never release

## 10. Liability

- Company not liable for damages
- Tester use at own risk
- Backup all important data
- No consequential damages
- Limited liability warranty

## 11. Termination

- Agreement terminates on mutual consent
- Company may terminate at any time
- Without cause or notice
- Upon termination, return all materials
- Destroy all Confidential Information

## 12. Term

- Begins on acceptance date
- Continues until termination
- Or until Beta Features release
- Confidentiality survives termination
- Obligations continue

## 13. No License or Rights

- No license granted
- No rights to Beta Features
- No right to future versions
- Company retains all rights
- All IP belongs to Company

## 14. Representations

Tester represents:

- Authority to enter agreement
- Compliance with terms
- No conflicts with other agreements
- Age 18+ or legal representative
