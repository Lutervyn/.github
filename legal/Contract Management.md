# Contract Management Policy

Last Updated: May 12, 2026

## 1. Introduction

### 1.1 Purpose
To ensure that all contracts entered into by Lutervyn are properly reviewed, approved, executed, and archived to protect the company's interests and ensure compliance.

### 1.2 Scope
Applies to all agreements, including:
- Customer Service Agreements (MSA/SOW).
- Vendor/Supplier Contracts.
- Employment/Contractor Agreements.
- Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDA).
- Partnership Agreements.

## 2. Standard Templates

### 2.1 Use of Templates
- Employees are required to use Lutervyn's approved legal templates whenever possible.
- Templates are located in the [Legal Portal](../legal/LEGAL_PORTAL.md).
- Any modification to a template (other than filling in standard fields) requires Legal review.

### 2.2 Key Templates
- **Standard NDA**: For initial business discussions.
- **Master Service Agreement (MSA)**: For customer relationships.
- **Independent Contractor Agreement**: For external consultants.
- **Vendor Agreement**: For purchasing services/products.

## 3. Review and Approval Process

### 3.1 Thresholds for Legal Review
- All contracts with a total value over $5,000.
- Any contract on a third-party (non-Lutervyn) template.
- Any contract containing non-standard indemnification, liability, or IP clauses.

### 3.2 Approval Workflow
1. **Originator**: Drafts/Gather contract.
2. **Department Head**: Approves business terms and budget.
3. **Legal**: Reviews and approves legal terms.
4. **Authorized Signatory**: Executes the contract.

## 4. Execution (Signing)

### 4.1 Authorized Signatories
- **Contracts < $10k**: Department Heads.
- **Contracts $10k - $50k**: VP or C-Level.
- **Contracts > $50k**: CEO or CFO.

### 4.2 Electronic Signature
- Lutervyn uses **DocuSign/PandaDoc** as the primary method for contract execution.
- Electronic signatures are considered legally binding and preferred for efficiency.

## 5. Storage and Retention

### 5.1 Central Repository
- All executed contracts must be uploaded to the **Contract Management System (CMS)** or the `Legal/Executed Contracts` secure folder within 48 hours of signing.

### 5.2 Retention Period
- Standard retention: 7 years after the contract expires or terminates.
- Specific tax or regulatory requirements may extend this period.

## 6. Renewals and Terminations

- Department Heads are responsible for tracking expiration dates and initiating renewals 60 days in advance.
- Termination notices must be reviewed by Legal before being sent.
