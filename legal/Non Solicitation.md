# Non-Solicitation Agreement

Last Updated: May 12, 2024
Effective Date: January 1, 2024

## 1. Purpose

This Agreement prohibits solicitation of employees and customers during and after employment.

## 2. Definitions

**Restricted Period** means during employment and _____ years after termination.

**Company Employees** means persons employed by Company as of termination.

**Company Customers** means persons with business relationship with Company.

## 3. Employee Solicitation

Employee shall not:

- Solicit Company Employees
- For employment or position
- During Restricted Period
- For competitive enterprise
- Indirect solicitation prohibited
- Exception: General advertising

## 4. Customer Solicitation

Employee shall not:

- Solicit Company Customers
- For business or services
- During Restricted Period
- For competitive purposes
- Indirect solicitation prohibited
- Using Company information

## 5. Restricted Territory

- Applies within _____________
- Geographic scope defined
- Based on Company operations
- Reasonable restriction
- Protects legitimate interests

## 6. Exceptions

Not restricted from:

- General advertising
- Public job postings
- General business solicitation
- Non-targeted solicitation
- Parties first contacted solicitor

## 7. Non-Disparagement

Employee shall not:

- Disparage Company
- Disparage Company products
- Disparage Company employees
- Publicly criticize
- During Restricted Period

## 8. Confidential Information

Employee shall not:

- Use employee lists
- Use customer lists
- Use pricing information
- Use trade secrets
- Use confidential information
- For any solicitation

## 9. Remedies

Breach allows:

- Injunctive relief
- Damages recovery
- Attorney's fees
- Specific performance
- Equitable relief

## 10. Consideration

- Employment is consideration
- Restrictive covenants supported
- Legitimate business interests
- Protects competition
- Reasonable in scope

## 11. Governing Law

- Reasonable restriction
- Enforced as written
- Severability clause applies
