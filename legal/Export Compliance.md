# Export Compliance Policy

Last Updated: May 12, 2024
Effective Date: January 1, 2024

## 1. Purpose

This policy ensures compliance with US export control laws and regulations.

## 2. Applicable Laws

### 2.1 US Laws

- Export Administration Regulations (EAR)
- International Traffic in Arms Regulations (ITAR)
- Office of Foreign Assets Control (OFAC) sanctions
- Treasury Department regulations
- Commerce Department regulations

### 2.2 International Laws

- UN sanctions
- EU export controls
- Allied nations coordinations
- Re-export limitations
- Country-specific rules

## 3. Export Items

### 3.1 Controlled Items

Lutervyn services may be:

- Subject to export controls
- Contain encryption
- Have commercial value
- Have dual-use potential
- Require authorization

### 3.2 Classification

- Assessed for control status
- Commerce Control List reviewed
- Encryption assessment
- Dual-use analysis
- Classification documented

## 4. Restricted Parties

### 4.1 Screening

All customers screened against:

- OFAC Specially Designated Nationals
- Denied Parties List
- Entity List
- Unverified List
- Proliferation List
- EU consolidates list
- UK sanctions list

### 4.2 Sanctioned Countries

Services restricted to:

- North Korea
- Iran
- Syria
- Cuba
- Crimea
- Other embargoed countries
- As updated by regulations

### 4.3 Sanctions Screening

- Automated screening tools
- Database updates
- Manual verification
- Periodic re-screening
- Exception process available
- License required for transactions

## 5. End-Use Verification

### 5.1 Red Flags

Lutervyn monitors:

- Unusual access patterns
- Large downloads
- Sensitive technology interest
- Country location mismatch
- Proliferation concerns
- Suspicious activity

### 5.2 Diligence

When red flags:

- Investigation conducted
- Customer contacted
- End-use verified
- Purpose confirmed
- Approved or declined
- Documentation retained

## 6. Customer Representations

Customers represent:

- Not on restricted party list
- Not in embargoed country
- Not for prohibited end-use
- Not for military use (per EAR)
- Not for proliferation
- Compliance with all laws
