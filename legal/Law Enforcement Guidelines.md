# Law Enforcement Guidelines

Last Updated: May 12, 2024
Effective Date: January 1, 2024

## 1. Purpose

These guidelines govern how Lutervyn responds to law enforcement requests for user data.

## 2. Principles

Lutervyn commits to:

- Comply with valid legal process
- Protect user privacy rights
- Require proper authorization
- Minimize data disclosure
- Notify users when possible
- Transparent reporting

## 3. Types of Requests

### 3.1 Subpoena

- Requires court order
- From authorized court
- Valid case number
- Specific data requested
- Signed by official
- Proper service required

### 3.2 Search Warrant

- Requires judicial authorization
- Probable cause shown
- Specific location/data identified
- 4th Amendment requirements met
- Properly executed
- Return and inventory

### 3.3 Emergency Disclosure

- Only for imminent danger
- Child safety concerns
- Serious threat of harm
- Requires written request
- Documented reasoning
- Legal review completed

### 3.4 National Security

- National Security Letters (NSLs)
- Foreign Intelligence Surveillance Act (FISA)
- Requires proper authority
- Legal review completed
- Accompanied by non-disclosure orders
- May challenge if appropriate

## 4. Request Handling

### 4.1 Submission

Requests submitted to:

- Legal Department
- Email: legal@lutervyn.com
- Address: [Legal Department Address]
- Attention: Law Enforcement
- Include all documentation

### 4.2 Verification

- Verify request legitimacy
- Confirm authority
- Validate legal basis
- Check for proper service
- Ensure completeness
- Contact jurisdiction if needed

### 4.3 Review Process

- Legal review completed
- Compliance assessment
- Data access requirements
- Privacy considerations
- Notification obligations
- Response determination

## 5. Data Disclosure

### 5.1 Minimal Disclosure

- Provide only requested data
- No additional information
- Narrowest scope
- Redact irrelevant info
- Protect third parties
- Preserve privacy

### 5.2 Available Information

Lutervyn may have:

- Account registration data
- IP addresses and logs
- Device information
- Usage patterns
- Communication metadata
- Payment information
- As permitted by law

### 5.3 Limited Information

Lutervyn may not have:

- Content of communications
- Encrypted content
- Deleted information
- Third-party information
- Real-time monitoring
- Surveillance capability

## 6. User Notification

### 6.1 Notification Policy

- Users notified when possible
- Prior to disclosure
- Unless prohibited by law
- Describing request received
- Data disclosed
- Contact information provided

### 6.2 Exceptions

Notification delayed/withheld:

- When legally prohibited
- By court order
- By law enforcement
- By national security
- When investigation at risk
- As briefly as possible

### 6.3 Timing

- Notification sent immediately
- When legally permitted
- Usually before disclosure
- Some delay if restrained
- No unreasonable delay

## 7. Costs

### 7.1 Cost Recovery

Lutervyn may charge:

- Staff time for preparation
- Data retrieval costs
- Legal review costs
- Data formatting costs
- As permitted by law
- Reasonable rates

### 7.2 Fee Waiver

- Waived in some cases
- Law enforcement discount
- Public interest matters
- Prosecutorial purpose
- At Lutervyn discretion

## 8. Challenge Process

### 8.1 Overbroad Requests

Lutervyn may challenge:

- Requests lacking basis
- Seeking too much data
- Seeking protected info
- Improper service
- Invalid legal basis
- In appropriate forum

### 8.2 Invalid Requests

- Lack of authority
- Insufficient detail
- Improper process
- Unconstitutional
- Conflict with law
- May be denied

## 9. Confidentiality

- Non-disclosure orders respected
- Cannot disclose requests
- Cannot discuss specifics
- Limited exceptions
- Transparency reports filed
- Annual aggregate reporting

## 10. Transparency Reporting

### 10.1 Annual Report

Lutervyn publishes:

- Number of requests received
- By type (subpoena, warrant, etc.)
- By jurisdiction
- Data disclosed
- Requests denied
- Ranges for privacy
- Publicly available

### 10.2 Content

Reports include:

- Request statistics
- Compliance information
- Challenges issued
- User notification
- Trends and changes
- Linked from privacy page

## 11. Policy Changes

### 11.1 Updates

- Policy reviewed annually
- Updated for legal changes
- Published on website
- Notification of changes
- Effective immediately
- Saved versions maintained
