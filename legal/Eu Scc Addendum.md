# EU Standard Contractual Clauses (SCC) Addendum

Last Updated: May 12, 2024
Effective Date: January 1, 2024

## 1. Purpose

This Addendum incorporates EU Standard Contractual Clauses for personal data transfers outside EU/EEA.

## 2. Applicability

- Applies when Lutervyn processes personal data from EU/EEA residents
- Incorporates European Commission Decision 2021/915
- Covers Modules One through Four
- Enables lawful data transfers

## 3. Definitions

- **Personal Data**: Information relating to identified/identifiable person
- **Processing**: Any operation on personal data
- **Data Exporter**: EU/EEA entity sending data
- **Data Importer**: Entity receiving data outside EU/EEA
- **Data Subject**: Person to whom data relates

## 4. SCC Clauses

### 4.1 Core Obligations

- Process only on instruction
- Ensure confidentiality
- Implement security measures
- Assist with data subject rights
- Assist with audits

### 4.2 Liability

- Liable for breach damage
- Joint liability if both breach
- Exceptions for force majeure

### 4.3 Transfers

- Further transfers restricted
- Sub-processor authorization required
- Same protections maintained

### 4.4 Data Subject Rights

- Facilitate access requests
- Support rectification
- Support erasure requests
- Support portability
- Support objections

## 5. Supplementary Measures

Implemented safeguards:

- Encryption in transit (TLS 1.2+)
- Encryption at rest (AES-256)
- Pseudonymization
- Multi-factor authentication
- Access controls and logging
- Regular audits
- Incident response

## 6. Transfer Impact Assessment

- Assessed legality of transfer
- Verified protection levels
- Supplementary measures identified
- Essential equivalence maintained
- Ongoing monitoring

## 7. Sub-processors

- Authorization required
- Named list maintained
- Changes notified
- Same protections required
- Processor liable for sub-processor

## 8. Duration

- Continues while processing
- Survives contract termination
- Until data deleted or returned
- Immediate termination possible

## 9. Data Handling

- Return or delete upon request
- Within 30 days
- Certification in writing
- Legal retention excepted

## 10. Security

**Technical:**
- AES-256 encryption at rest
- TLS 1.2+ for transit
- SHA-256 hashing minimum
- Secure key management
- Data segmentation

**Organizational:**
- Role-based access control
- Need-to-know principle
- Background checks
- Annual training
- 24-hour incident response

## 11. Audit Rights

- Annual audit permitted
- Upon request inspection
- Reasonable notice required
- Confidentiality maintained
- SOC 2 Type II annual

## 12. Breach Notification

- Without undue delay
- Within 24-48 hours preferred
- Breach description provided
- Categories of data identified
- Measures taken documented
