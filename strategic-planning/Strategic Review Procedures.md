# Strategic Review Procedures

Last Updated: May 12, 2026

## 1. Objectives

### 1.1 Purpose
To periodically step back from day-to-day operations and assess Lutervyn's competitive position, market opportunities, and long-term direction.

## 2. Review Cadence

### 2.1 Monthly Executive Review
- 2-hour session with the C-Level team.
- Focus: Short-term pivots and KPI performance.

### 2.2 Quarterly Strategic Sync
- Half-day session with the Executive Team and VP-level leaders.
- Focus: OKR progress and resource reallocation.

### 2.3 Annual Strategic Retreat
- 2-3 day offsite for senior leadership.
- Focus: Long-term (3-5 year) vision and major strategic bets.

## 3. Analysis Frameworks

Lutervyn uses the following tools during strategic reviews:

### 3.1 SWOT Analysis
- **Strengths**: Internal advantages.
- **Weaknesses**: Internal limitations.
- **Opportunities**: External possibilities for growth.
- **Threats**: External risks to the business.

### 3.2 PESTEL Analysis
- Review of Political, Economic, Social, Technological, Environmental, and Legal factors impacting the industry.

### 3.3 Competitive Matrix
- Deep dive into competitor features, pricing, and market share.

## 4. Input Requirements

The following data must be prepared 5 days before a strategic review:
- Current Financials (P&L, Cash Flow).
- Product Roadmap status.
- Customer Sentiment / NPS scores.
- Market Trend reports.
- Employee Engagement scores.

## 5. Outputs & Documentation

### 5.1 Strategic Memo
- A summary of the key decisions and direction changes.
- Distributed to the Board and senior leadership.

### 5.2 Action Log
- Specific tasks, owners, and deadlines resulting from the review.
- Tracked monthly in the Executive Sync.
