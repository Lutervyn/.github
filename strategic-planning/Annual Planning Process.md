# Annual Planning Process

Last Updated: May 12, 2026

## 1. Overview

### 1.1 Purpose
To define the objectives, key results (OKRs), and budget for the upcoming fiscal year, ensuring all departments are aligned with Lutervyn's long-term strategy.

### 1.2 Timeline
- **Q3 (July - Sept)**: Strategic Review and Top-Level Goal Setting.
- **Q4 (Oct - Nov)**: Departmental Planning and Budgeting.
- **Q4 (Dec)**: Board Approval and Finalization.
- **Q1 (Jan)**: Company-wide Kickoff.

## 2. Planning Phases

### 2.1 Phase 1: Strategic Vision (Executives)
- Review performance against current year goals.
- Identify top 3-5 strategic themes for the next year.
- Set high-level financial targets (Revenue, EBITDA, Growth).

### 2.2 Phase 2: Departmental OKRs (Department Heads)
- Departments define their Objectives and Key Results that support the Strategic Themes.
- Resource requirements (Headcount, Budget) are identified.

### 2.3 Phase 3: Budget Integration (Finance)
- Finance reviews all departmental requests.
- Iterative process to align requests with financial targets.
- Creation of the master budget for the upcoming year.

### 2.4 Phase 4: Board Review
- The annual plan and budget are presented to the Board of Directors for review and formal approval.

## 3. OKR Framework

Lutervyn uses the OKR (Objectives and Key Results) framework:
- **Objectives**: Qualitative, aspirational, and time-bound (e.g., "Become the market leader in [Category]").
- **Key Results**: Quantitative, measurable outcomes (e.g., "Achieve $50M ARR").

## 4. Communication & Alignment

### 4.1 Company Kickoff
- The finalized annual plan is shared with all employees during the January "All Hands" meeting.
- Individual performance goals are aligned with team OKRs.

### 4.2 Quarterly Reviews
- Progress against the annual plan is reviewed at the end of each quarter.
- OKRs may be adjusted (but not the overall strategy) based on market changes.
