# Shareholder Communication Framework

Last Updated: May 12, 2026

## 1. Objectives

### 1.1 Purpose
To maintain transparency, build trust, and ensure that all shareholders are accurately and timely informed about Lutervyn's performance and prospects.

## 2. Communication Channels

### 2.1 Quarterly Investor Letter
- Sent within 45 days of the end of each quarter.
- Contents:
  - CEO message.
  - Financial highlights vs. targets.
  - Major product/market milestones.
  - Discussion of risks and challenges.

### 2.2 Annual Shareholder Meeting
- Held once per fiscal year (usually within 120 days of year-end).
- Opportunity for shareholders to vote on key resolutions and hear directly from management.

### 2.3 Investor Relations (IR) Portal
- A secure area of the website containing historical letters, financial statements, and governance documents.

## 3. Transparency & Disclosure

### 3.1 Equal Access
- Material information must be disclosed to all shareholders simultaneously.
- Selective disclosure (providing "tips" to specific investors) is strictly prohibited.

### 3.2 Material Information
- Significant events (e.g., funding rounds, major lawsuits, leadership changes) require a dedicated "Material Change Notice" sent within 48 hours of the event.

## 4. Handling Inquiries

### 4.1 Designated Spokespeople
- **Only** the CEO and CFO are authorized to communicate directly with shareholders regarding financial performance or strategic direction.
- All other employee inquiries must be forwarded to IR@lutervyn.com.

### 4.2 Quiet Periods
- From the end of a quarter until the issuance of the Quarterly Investor Letter, the company enters a "Quiet Period" where communication with investors is limited to routine matters.

## 5. Compliance

- All communications must adhere to relevant securities laws and the company's Confidentiality agreements.
