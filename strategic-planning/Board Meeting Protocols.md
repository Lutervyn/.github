# Board Meeting Protocols

Last Updated: May 12, 2026

## 1. Governance Overview

### 1.1 Purpose
To ensure that meetings of the Lutervyn Board of Directors are productive, compliant with legal requirements, and provide effective oversight.

## 2. Meeting Frequency

### 2.1 Quarterly Meetings
- Primary sessions for strategic review and financial approval.
- Usually held in the second month of each quarter (Feb, May, Aug, Nov).

### 2.2 Special Meetings
- Called by the Chairman or CEO as needed for urgent matters (e.g., M&A, major legal issues).
- Minimum 48 hours' notice required.

## 3. Preparation & Materials

### 3.1 The Board Deck
- Consists of:
  - CEO Update.
  - Financial Performance (P&L, Balance Sheet, Forecast).
  - Departmental Updates (Sales, Product, Ops).
  - Strategic Discussion Topic (Deep Dive).
  - Consent Agenda (Minutes approval, routine resolutions).

### 3.2 Distribution Timeline
- Materials must be sent to Board members at least **5 business days** before the meeting to allow for thorough review.

## 4. Meeting Conduct

### 4.1 Attendance & Quorum
- A majority of the directors must be present (in person or via video) to constitute a quorum for voting.

### 4.2 Executive Session
- Every board meeting includes an "Executive Session" (Board members only, no company management) to discuss sensitive governance issues.

### 4.3 Voting
- Resolutions are passed by a simple majority vote unless the Bylaws specify otherwise.

## 5. Minutes & Documentation

### 5.1 Responsibility
- The Corporate Secretary (or legal counsel) is responsible for taking minutes.

### 5.2 Contents
- Record of attendance.
- Summary of discussions.
- Details of all resolutions passed.
- Clear record of any dissenting votes or abstentions.

### 5.3 Approval
- Draft minutes distributed within 7 days.
- Formally approved at the next Board meeting.
