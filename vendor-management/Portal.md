## Key Policies at a Glance

**Vendor Selection Process**: 4 phases from initial screening to final decision → 30-60 day timeline

**Performance Tracking**: Monthly metrics dashboard + Quarterly business reviews + Annual evaluation

**SLA Standards**:
- Uptime: 99.5-99.9%
- Response Time: 1-24 hours depending on severity
- Support: 24/7 critical issue escalation

**Service Credits**:
- 99.0%-99.5%: 5% credit
- <99.0%: 10-25% credit + management review

**Issue Escalation**: 4 levels from operational to legal (5 day, 10 day, 15 day timelines)
