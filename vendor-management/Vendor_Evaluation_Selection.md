# Vendor Evaluation & Selection Criteria

Last Updated: May 12, 2026

## 1. Vendor Evaluation Process

### 1.1 Evaluation Phases

**Phase 1: Initial Screening**
- Company background and financial stability
- Industry experience and certifications
- References and client portfolio
- Preliminary capability assessment

**Phase 2: Detailed Assessment**
- Detailed vendor capabilities
- Pricing comparison and ROI analysis
- Security and compliance review
- Risk assessment

**Phase 3: Due Diligence**
- Reference calls (minimum 3)
- On-site visits if applicable
- Financial statements review
- Contract negotiation

**Phase 4: Final Decision**
- Executive approval
- Contract execution
- Onboarding coordination

---

## 2. Evaluation Criteria

### 2.1 Capability & Fit (30%)

**Service Quality**
- Industry expertise and experience (15%)
- Client success stories and case studies (10%)
- Service breadth and depth match (5%)

**Technical Capability**
- Technology stack and integration (10%)
- Scalability for growth (7%)
- Innovation and roadmap (5%)

### 2.2 Financial (25%)

**Pricing**
- Cost competitiveness (15%)
- Value for investment (10%)
- Transparent pricing model (5%)

**Financial Health**
- Company financial stability (8%)
- Payment terms flexibility (5%)

### 2.3 Reliability & Performance (20%)

**Uptime and SLA**
- Guaranteed uptime (10%)
- Support availability (7%)
- Incident response time (5%)

**Compliance and Security**
- Data security measures (10%)
- Compliance certifications (7%)
- Data privacy practices (5%)

### 2.4 References & Reputation (15%)

**Reference Quality**
- Customer references provided (8%)
- Reference call feedback (8%)

**Reputation**
- Industry reputation and awards (5%)
- Market presence and size (5%)

### 2.5 Relationship Fit (10%)

**Cultural Alignment**
- Company values alignment (5%)
- Communication style (3%)
- Partnership approach (2%)

## 4. Reference Check Template

**Questions for References**:

1. How long have you used [Vendor] for [Service]?
2. How would you rate their service quality (1-10)?
3. Have they met agreed SLAs?
4. How responsive is their support team?
5. Any significant issues or downtime?
6. Would you recommend them to others?
7. What could they improve?
8. What are their biggest strengths?
9. Any concerns or red flags?
10. Overall satisfaction with pricing?

---

## 5. Contract Negotiation Points

**Key Terms to Negotiate**:
- Price and payment terms
- Service level agreements (SLA) and uptime guarantees
- Support response times
- Scalability and growth provisions
- Data security and compliance requirements
- Term length and renewal options
- Termination for convenience provisions
- Liability and indemnification
- Confidentiality protections
- Vendor insurance requirements

## 7. Ongoing Performance Tracking

### 7.1 Monthly Review

- Service delivery on schedule
- Quality meets specifications
- No unplanned downtime
- Support responsiveness adequate
- Pricing accurate per contract

### 7.2 Quarterly Review

- Performance metrics vs. SLA
- Cost vs. budget
- Customer satisfaction
- Any issues or complaints
- Vendor relationship quality

### 7.3 Annual Review

- Overall performance assessment
- Renewal recommendation
- Price adjustment negotiation
- Strategic alignment review
- Continued fit evaluation

## 8. Vendor Risk Assessment

**Risk Factors**:
- Financial stability concerns
- Key person dependency
- Geopolitical or regulatory risks
- Technology obsolescence
- Market consolidation risks
- Competitive pressure
- Cybersecurity vulnerabilities
- Compliance certification lapses

**Mitigation Strategies**:
- Dual-sourcing critical services
- Contractual performance guarantees
- Regular security audits
- Escrow accounts for critical data
- Contingency planning
- Diversification strategy

---

## 9. Vendor Termination Process

**Grounds for Termination**:
- Repeated SLA breaches
- Security incident or data breach
- Non-compliance with regulations
- Financial instability
- Unacceptable service degradation
- Business model changes
- Strategic realignment

**Termination Steps**:
1. Written notice of issues
2. 30-day cure period
3. Escalation to vendor leadership
4. If unresolved, termination notice
5. Transition planning and knowledge transfer
6. Data migration and retrieval
7. Final settlement and disputes resolution
