# Vendor-Partner Relationship Management

Last Updated: May 12, 2026

## 1. Partner Relationship Overview

**Partner Types**:
- Vendors and suppliers
- Technology integration partners
- Channel and reseller partners
- Strategic business partners

This guide focuses on managing ongoing relationships with vendors and partners post-selection.

---

## 2. Relationship Governance Structure

### 2.1 Governance Framework

**Account Manager Assignment**
- Designated account manager for each strategic partner
- Primary contact for vendor communications
- Owns relationship health and escalations
- Quarterly business review facilitator

**Steering Committee** (for strategic partners)
- Executive sponsor
- Department lead (customer of services)
- Procurement/Vendor management
- Finance (if budget impacts)
- Meets quarterly to assess relationship

### 2.2 Communication Cadence

| Level | Frequency | Attendees | Purpose |
|-------|-----------|-----------|---------|
| Operational | Weekly | Account manager + vendor contact | Status updates, issues |
| Management | Monthly | Manager level + vendor account manager | Performance, issues, improvements |
| Strategic | Quarterly | Executive + vendor leadership | Business review, planning |
| Executive | Annual | C-level + vendor executives | Strategic alignment, renewal |

## 3. Partner Communication Plan

### 3.1 Meeting Templates

**Weekly Operational Call** (30 min)
- Deliverables status
- Issues and concerns
- Upcoming milestones
- Quick wins

**Monthly Business Review** (60 min)
- Performance vs. targets
- Customer feedback
- Pipeline and forecast
- Action items status

**Quarterly Strategic Review** (90 min)
- Comprehensive performance analysis
- Market and competitive updates
- Strategic alignment
- Investment and expansion discussion
- Relationship health

### 3.2 Communication Standards

**Response Time**:
- Urgent (critical issues): <4 hours
- High priority: <24 hours
- Standard: <48 hours

**Escalation Path**:
- Issue first reported to vendor account manager
- If not resolved in 48 hours, escalate to vendor manager
- If not resolved in 5 days, escalate to executive level

## 4. Performance Metrics and Monitoring

### 4.1 Key Performance Indicators

**Delivery & Quality**:
- On-time delivery rate (target: 95%)
- Quality/defect rate (target: <2%)
- Customer satisfaction (target: 8+/10)
- Resolution time (target: <24 hours)

**Financial**:
- Cost per unit (trend analysis)
- Budget variance (target: ±5%)
- Payment terms compliance
- Volume discounts achievement

**Relationship**:
- Responsiveness score (target: 9+/10)
- Issue resolution rate (target: >95%)
- Proactive communication (yes/no)
- Willingness to improve (assessment)

### 4.2 Performance Dashboard

Monthly dashboard shared with partner:
- KPI performance vs. targets
- Trend analysis (month-over-month)
- Outstanding issues
- Success stories and wins
- Improvement initiatives in progress

## 5. Issue Escalation and Resolution

### 5.1 Issue Categories

**Critical (P1)**
- Service completely unavailable
- Customer facing impact
- Financial or compliance risk
- Response: Immediate (within 2 hours)
- Escalation: VP level

**High (P2)**
- Service degradation
- Multiple customers affected
- Operational impact
- Response: <4 hours
- Escalation: Manager level

**Medium (P3)**
- Isolated impact
- Limited customer effect
- Operational inefficiency
- Response: <24 hours
- Escalation: Team lead level

**Low (P4)**
- Enhancement request
- Minor improvement
- No operational impact
- Response: <72 hours
- Escalation: Account manager level

### 5.2 Resolution Process

1. **Issue Reported**: Document and prioritize
2. **Immediate Acknowledgment**: Within 1 hour
3. **Root Cause Analysis**: Within 24 hours (critical)
4. **Resolution Plan**: Develop and communicate
5. **Implementation**: Execute with timeline
6. **Verification**: Confirm resolution
7. **Prevention**: Implement measures to prevent recurrence

## 6. Service Level Agreements (SLAs)

### 6.1 SLA Components

**Availability/Uptime**
- 99.5% uptime target
- Measured: 99.0% = acceptable, <98.0% = unacceptable
- Excludes planned maintenance (communicated in advance)

**Response Times**
- Critical: 2-hour response, 4-hour resolution target
- High: 4-hour response, 24-hour resolution target
- Standard: 24-hour response, 72-hour resolution target

**Delivery**
- On-time delivery: 95% of shipments
- Late delivery: <2% of orders

### 6.2 Service Credits

| SLA Attainment | Service Credit |
|---|---|
| 99.5%-99.0% | 2.5% monthly fee |
| 99.0%-98.5% | 5% monthly fee |
| 98.5%-98.0% | 10% monthly fee |
| <98.0% | 15% monthly fee + review |

## 7. Regular Business Reviews

### 7.1 Quarterly Business Review (QBR) Agenda

**Part 1: Performance Review** (30 min)
- KPI dashboard review
- Performance vs. targets
- Trend analysis (improving/declining/stable)
- Customer feedback highlights
- Issues and resolutions

**Part 2: Customer Insights** (20 min)
- Customer satisfaction scores
- Customer feedback and complaints
- Positive customer stories
- Customer retention/churn

**Part 3: Operational Discussion** (20 min)
- Resource allocation
- Staffing and team changes
- Technology or process improvements
- Capacity for increased volume

**Part 4: Strategic Alignment** (20 min)
- Company growth plans
- Market changes
- Product or service enhancements
- Partnership expansion opportunities

**Part 5: Action Items and Next Steps** (10 min)
- Initiatives for next quarter
- Improvement commitments
- Support or resources needed
- Next QBR date and agenda

### 7.2 QBR Documentation

- Action items with owners and deadlines
- Performance commitments for next quarter
- Investment or improvement initiatives
- Attendance and approvals
- Stored for reference

## 9. Conflict Resolution

### 9.1 Escalation Path

**Step 1: Direct Resolution** (Owner: Account managers)
- Open discussion of issue
- Attempt to resolve through compromise
- Timeline: 5 business days

**Step 2: Management Review** (Owner: Department heads)
- Both sides present their perspective
- Mediated resolution attempt
- Timeline: 10 business days

**Step 3: Executive Decision** (Owner: VP/Director level)
- Executive level review
- Decision authority makes determination
- Communication to both parties
- Timeline: 15 business days

**Step 4: Contract Review/Legal**
- If still unresolved, legal review
- Formal dispute procedures per contract
- Escalation or termination consideration

### 9.2 Common Conflict Scenarios

**Performance Gaps**:
- Document specific performance misses
- Provide opportunity to improve with timeline
- Regular monitoring and reporting
- Escalate if not resolved

**Pricing Disputes**:
- Review contract terms and pricing model
- Verify calculations and charges
- Reconcile any discrepancies
- Adjust billing if warranted

**Resource Limitations**:
- Discuss capacity constraints
- Negotiate timeline adjustments
- Consider alternate solutions
- Plan for future capacity

## 10. Partner Retention and Development

### 10.1 Retention Strategy

**Investment in Relationship**:
- Regular engagement and communication
- Recognition of achievements
- Fair and timely payment
- Opportunity for business growth
- Support for partner success

**Partner Development**:
- Training and capability building
- Access to market insights
- Introduction to potential customers
- Co-marketing and visibility
- Joint innovation initiatives

### 10.2 Renewal Decision

**Renewal Evaluation Criteria** (90 days before expiration):
- Performance vs. SLAs (40%)
- Customer satisfaction (25%)
- Financial performance (20%)
- Strategic alignment (15%)

**Renewal Options**:
- Continue on existing terms
- Continue with improvements
- Renegotiate pricing or terms
- Transition to alternative partner
