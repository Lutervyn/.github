# Continuous Improvement Process

Last Updated: May 12, 2026

## 1. Philosophy: Kaizen

Lutervyn adopts the philosophy of **Kaizen** (continuous improvement). We believe that small, incremental changes made consistently result in significant long-term improvements.

## 2. The PDCA Cycle

All teams are encouraged to use the **Plan-Do-Check-Act (PDCA)** cycle:
1. **Plan**: Identify an opportunity and plan for change.
2. **Do**: Implement the change on a small scale.
3. **Check**: Use data to analyze the results of the change.
4. **Act**: If successful, implement on a wider scale and continuously assess.

## 3. Feedback Sources for Improvement

- KPI/Metric misses.
- Customer feedback and NPS comments.
- Employee suggestions (via the internal "Idea Bank").
- Post-mortem reports from incidents or project failures.
- Competitor analysis and industry benchmarks.

## 4. Structured Improvement Initiatives

### 4.1 Post-Mortems (Blame-Free)
- Required for all S1/S2 incidents or major project delays.
- Focus: Systemic causes rather than individual errors.
- Output: 3-5 specific "Action Items" to prevent recurrence.

### 4.2 Innovation Sprints
- Once per quarter, teams can spend 3 days working on internal process improvements or "Quality of Life" features.

### 4.3 Internal Audits
- Periodic reviews of departmental processes by the Compliance or Operations team to identify inefficiencies or non-compliance.

## 5. Recognizing and Rewarding Improvement

- The **"Impact Award"** is given monthly to an employee or team that implemented a process improvement with significant measurable results (e.g., cost savings, time reduction).

## 6. Documentation

- Successful improvements must be documented in the [Internal Wiki](../knowledge-management/Internal%20Wiki%20and%20Knowledge%20Base%20Management.md) and relevant policy documents must be updated.
