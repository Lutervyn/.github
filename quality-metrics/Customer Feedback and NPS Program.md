# Customer Feedback and NPS Program

Last Updated: May 12, 2026

## 1. Overview

### 1.1 Purpose
To systematically gather, analyze, and act upon customer feedback to improve our products and services.

### 1.2 Key Metric: Net Promoter Score (NPS)
NPS measures customer loyalty by asking: *"On a scale of 0-10, how likely are you to recommend Lutervyn to a friend or colleague?"*
- **Promoters (9-10)**: Loyal enthusiasts.
- **Passives (7-8)**: Satisfied but unenthusiastic.
- **Detractors (0-6)**: Unhappy customers.

## 2. Feedback Channels

### 2.1 Transactional Surveys (CSAT)
- Sent immediately after a support ticket is closed or a major feature is used.
- Focus: Immediate satisfaction with a specific event.

### 2.2 Relationship Surveys (NPS)
- Sent semi-annually to all active customers.
- Focus: Overall brand health and loyalty.

### 2.3 Qualitative Feedback
- Customer advisory boards.
- User interviews.
- Review site monitoring (G2, Capterra).
- Social media mentions.

## 3. The "Close the Loop" Process

### 3.1 Detractor Management
- Any NPS score of 0-6 triggers an automatic alert to the Customer Success Manager (CSM).
- The CSM must contact the customer within 48 hours to understand the issue and attempt resolution.

### 3.2 Promoter Activation
- Customers scoring 9-10 are invited to join our referral program or provide a case study.

### 3.3 Feature Request Integration
- Feedback related to product functionality is tagged and sent to the [Feature Request Process](../product/Feature%20Request%20and%20Prioritization%20Process.md).

## 4. Analysis and Reporting

- **Monthly Feedback Summary**: Distributed to Product, Engineering, and Sales teams.
- **Sentiment Analysis**: Using AI tools to identify trends in open-ended comments.
- **Executive Review**: Quarterly presentation of NPS trends and top 3 feedback-driven initiatives.

## 5. Incentives

- We do not offer direct financial incentives for positive scores to maintain data integrity.
- We may offer small tokens of appreciation (e.g., company swag) for completing detailed interviews.
