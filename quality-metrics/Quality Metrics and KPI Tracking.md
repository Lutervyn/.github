# Quality Metrics and KPI Tracking

Last Updated: May 12, 2026

## 1. Overview

### 1.1 Purpose
To define the key performance indicators (KPIs) and metrics used to measure the quality of Lutervyn products, services, and operations.

### 1.2 Principles
- **Measurability**: Metrics must be quantifiable.
- **Actionability**: Data should drive specific improvements.
- **Transparency**: Metrics are shared openly with the relevant teams.

## 2. Product & Engineering Metrics

| Metric | Definition | Target |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Uptime/Availability** | % of time services are operational | 99.9% |
| **Bugs by Severity** | Number of open S1/S2 bugs | < 5 |
| **Mean Time to Resolution (MTTR)** | Average time to fix a reported bug | < 48 hours |
| **Deployment Frequency** | Number of production deployments per week | > 5 |
| **Change Failure Rate** | % of deployments resulting in service degradation | < 5% |

## 3. Customer & Service Metrics

| Metric | Definition | Target |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Net Promoter Score (NPS)** | Customer likelihood to recommend | > 50 |
| **Customer Satisfaction (CSAT)** | Satisfaction with specific interactions | > 4.5/5 |
| **Support Response Time** | Time to first response on a ticket | < 2 hours |
| **Customer Retention Rate** | % of customers retained over 12 months | > 90% |

## 4. Operational & Financial Metrics

| Metric | Definition | Target |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Employee Net Promoter Score (eNPS)** | Employee likelihood to recommend Lutervyn | > 40 |
| **Budget Variance** | Difference between actual and planned spend | +/- 5% |
| **Compliance Audit Score** | Score on internal/external audits | > 95% |

## 5. Tracking & Reporting

### 5.1 Real-Time Dashboards
- Engineering uses **Grafana/Datadog** for system performance.
- Sales/CS uses **Salesforce/HubSpot** for customer metrics.

### 5.2 Monthly Performance Review
- Department heads present their metrics to the Executive Team.
- Analysis of "Misses" and creation of remediation plans.

## 6. Data Integrity

- Data for KPIs must be pulled directly from systems of record whenever possible to avoid manual entry errors or bias.
