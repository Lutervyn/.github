## How to Use These Templates

### Step 1: Customize
- Replace [BRACKETED PLACEHOLDERS] with specific information
- Adjust terms to match deal structure
- Update pricing, timing, and deliverables
- Modify terms per negotiation

### Step 2: Legal Review
- Have legal counsel review before execution
- Verify compliance with applicable laws
- Confirm all changes are captured

- Obtain required internal approvals per authority levels

- Document approval signatures

- Store signed agreement in document repository
- Create reminders for renewal/termination dates
- Communicate key terms to relevant parties
- Monitor for compliance with obligations

## Key Contract Terms to Understand

**Term**: How long the agreement lasts (e.g., 1 year, 2 years)
**Auto-Renewal**: Whether agreement automatically renews unless terminated
**Termination for Cause**: Grounds for immediate termination (breaches, etc.)
**Termination for Convenience**: Ability to end agreement without cause (with notice)
**Confidentiality**: Obligation to keep information secret
**IP Ownership**: Who owns work product and intellectual property
**Liability Limits**: Maximum exposure for breaches or damages
**Indemnification**: Who covers legal costs for certain types of claims
**Severability**: If one clause is invalid, agreement continues

## Legal Compliance

**Before Using Templates**:
- Verify compliance with applicable laws
- Confirm no conflicts with existing agreements
- Check regulatory requirements for your industry
- Ensure tax and employment law compliance

**Specific Areas**:
- Employment agreements: Wage and hour laws, non-compete enforceability
- NDAs: Reasonable scope and duration
- Vendor agreements: Insurance requirements, indemnification limits
- Customer agreements: Consumer protection laws, warranty disclaimers
