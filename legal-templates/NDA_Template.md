---

## 1. Purpose

The Parties wish to disclose certain confidential information to each other to evaluate a potential business opportunity ("Purpose") and agree to keep such information confidential under the terms of this Agreement.

---

## 2. Definition of Confidential Information

"Confidential Information" means any non-public information disclosed by one Party ("Disclosing Party") to the other Party ("Receiving Party"), whether in written, oral, electronic, or visual form, that:

(a) Is marked as "Confidential" or identified as confidential at time of disclosure;

(b) Is disclosed orally and confirmed in writing within 10 business days as confidential;

(c) Would reasonably be understood as confidential given its nature or circumstances;

(d) Includes but is not limited to: business plans, financial information, customer lists, technical data, software, specifications, source code, algorithms, processes, trade secrets, strategies, pricing, and business methods.

---

## 3. Exclusions from Confidential Information

Confidential Information does not include information that:

(a) Is or becomes publicly available through no breach of this Agreement;

(b) Was rightfully possessed by the Receiving Party prior to disclosure, as evidenced by contemporaneous written records;

(c) Is independently developed by the Receiving Party without reference to Confidential Information, as evidenced by written records;

(d) Is rightfully received by the Receiving Party from a third party without confidentiality obligations;

(e) Is required to be disclosed by law, court order, or regulatory requirement, provided the Receiving Party:
   - Promptly notifies the Disclosing Party in writing (unless legally prohibited)
   - Cooperates with Disclosing Party's efforts to obtain protective order
   - Discloses only the minimum information legally required

---

## 4. Obligations of Receiving Party

The Receiving Party agrees to:

(a) **Protection**: Protect Confidential Information using the same degree of care used for its own confidential information, but not less than reasonable care;

(b) **Limited Use**: Use Confidential Information solely for the Purpose stated above;

(c) **Limited Disclosure**: Disclose Confidential Information only to employees, contractors, and advisors who:
   - Have a need to know for the Purpose
   - Are bound by written confidentiality obligations at least as restrictive as this Agreement
   - Remain responsible for their compliance

(d) **Return/Destruction**: Upon written request or termination of discussions, promptly return or destroy all Confidential Information (except one copy for legal/compliance records if legally required);

(e) **No License**: Not acquire any license or rights to Confidential Information beyond this limited disclosure;

(f) **No Obligation to Deal**: Not obligated to proceed with discussions or transaction based on disclosure.

---

## 5. No Representations or Warranties

(a) **AS-IS**: All Confidential Information is provided "AS IS" without warranties of any kind;

(b) **No Accuracy Warranty**: Disclosing Party makes no representations regarding accuracy, completeness, or reliability;

(c) **Non-Infringement**: Disclosing Party does not warrant that Confidential Information does not infringe third-party rights;

(d) **No Obligation**: Disclosure does not obligate Disclosing Party to proceed with any transaction.

---

## 6. Permitted Disclosures

**Government or Legal Disclosures:**

If Receiving Party is required to disclose Confidential Information by law, regulation, court order, or government authority:

(a) Notify Disclosing Party immediately (unless prohibited);

(b) Cooperate with Disclosing Party's efforts to obtain protective order;

(c) Disclose only information legally required;

(d) Request confidential treatment if available.

---

## 7. Term and Termination

(a) **Initial Term**: This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and continues for [2 YEARS / OTHER] ("Initial Term");

(b) **Confidentiality Period**: Confidential Information remains protected for:
   - Trade secrets: For duration of trade secret status (may extend beyond term)
   - Other Confidential Information: [2-3 YEARS] after disclosure

(c) **Early Termination**: Either Party may terminate discussions at any time by written notice, but confidentiality obligations survive termination.

---

## 8. Remedies

(a) **Inadequacy of Damages**: Parties acknowledge that monetary damages may not adequately compensate breach;

(b) **Injunctive Relief**: Disclosing Party may seek injunctive relief and specific performance without proving damages;

(c) **Cumulative**: Rights are cumulative and not exclusive.

---

## 9. General Provisions

**9.1 No License**
This Agreement grants no licenses or rights except as expressly stated.

**9.2 No Partnership**
This Agreement does not create partnership, joint venture, or agency relationship.

**9.3 Governing Law**
This Agreement governed by laws of [STATE/JURISDICTION], without regard to conflict of law principles.

**9.4 Entire Agreement**
This Agreement constitutes entire agreement regarding subject matter and supersedes all prior negotiations and agreements.

**9.5 Amendments**
Amendments require written signatures of authorized representatives of both Parties.

**9.6 Severability**
If any provision invalid, remaining provisions continue in effect.

**9.7 Assignment**
Neither Party may assign without written consent of other Party (transfer to successor in merger permitted if successor assumes obligations).

**9.8 No Waiver**
Failure to enforce any right does not constitute waiver of that right.

**9.9 Notices**
Notices must be in writing and delivered to addresses below (or as updated in writing).

**9.10 Counterparts**
Agreement may be executed in counterparts, each deemed original, all constituting one agreement.

---

## 10. Confidentiality Undertaking

Each Party acknowledges its understanding of confidentiality obligations and agrees to be bound thereby.
