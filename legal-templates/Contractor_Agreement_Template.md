## 2. Term and Termination

**2.1 Term**
Agreement effective [EFFECTIVE DATE] through [END DATE] ("Term").

**2.2 Termination for Cause**
Company may terminate immediately for:
- Material breach not cured within 10 days written notice
- Non-performance of Services
- Violation of confidentiality or IP obligations
- Violation of laws

**2.3 Termination Without Cause**
Company may terminate at any time with [X DAYS] written notice.

**2.4 Effect of Termination**
- Contractor immediately ceases work
- Contractor returns all Company property
- Company pays for work completed through termination date (less any damages)
- Confidentiality and IP provisions survive

## 3. Compensation

**3.1 Fee Structure**
Contractor will be compensated as follows (Exhibit B):
- Fixed fee: $________ for deliverables
- Hourly rate: $________ per hour
- Milestone payments: [SCHEDULE]

**3.2 Invoicing and Payment**
- Contractor submits detailed invoices upon completion
- Company pays within 30 days of invoice receipt (Net 30)
- Late payment at 1.5% monthly interest

**3.3 Expenses**
Contractor responsible for own expenses. Pre-approved expenses reimbursable upon receipt submission.

**3.4 Taxes**
Contractor responsible for all payroll taxes, income taxes, and self-employment taxes.

---

## 4. Independent Contractor Status

**4.1 Classification**
Contractor is an independent contractor, NOT an employee. Contractor:
- Controls method and manner of performance
- Sets own work schedule
- Provides own equipment and tools
- Maintains independent business
- May perform services for other clients (non-competitive)

**4.2 No Employee Benefits**
Contractor is not eligible for:
- Health insurance or benefits
- 401(k) or retirement plans
- Paid time off
- Workers compensation
- Unemployment insurance
- Visa sponsorship or immigration support

**4.3 Tax Responsibilities**
Contractor is solely responsible for:
- Income tax withholding
- Self-employment taxes
- Estimated quarterly tax payments
- Business licensing and permits

**4.4 Business Insurance**
Contractor responsible for own business insurance, including professional liability and general liability.

---

## 5. Work Product and Intellectual Property

**5.1 Assignment of IP**
All work product, deliverables, inventions, code, documents, and materials ("Work Product") created during this engagement are Company property. Contractor assigns all intellectual property rights to Company upon creation.

**5.2 Pre-Existing IP**
Contractor retains ownership of pre-existing tools, methodologies, and intellectual property. Contractor grants Company non-exclusive license to use pre-existing IP solely as incorporated in Work Product.

**5.3 Third-Party IP**
Contractor warrants:
- All Work Product is original or properly licensed
- Work Product does not infringe third-party IP
- Contractor has full rights to assign Work Product to Company

**5.4 Registration**
Company owns and controls all IP registrations, copyrights, and patent applications for Work Product.

---

## 6. Confidentiality

**6.1 Confidential Information**
Contractor will keep Company confidential information confidential both during and for [5 YEARS] after engagement term.

**6.2 Permitted Use**
Contractor may only use Company confidential information to perform Services under this Agreement.

**6.3 Exceptions**
Confidential Information excludes information that is:
- Publicly available
- Previously known without breach
- Independently developed
- Required to disclose by law (with notice)

**6.4 Return of Information**
Upon termination, Contractor immediately returns or destroys all Company confidential information.

---

## 7. Performance Standards

**7.1 Quality**
Services will be performed:
- Professionally and with competence
- In accordance with industry standards
- Free of material defects
- In compliance with Exhibit A specifications

**7.2 Timeline**
Deliverables will be provided by agreed dates.

**7.3 Communication**
Contractor will:
- Respond to Company inquiries within 24 hours
- Provide regular progress updates
- Escalate issues promptly
- Maintain professional conduct

## 8. Representations and Warranties

Contractor represents and warrants:

(a) Authority to enter into this Agreement;

(b) Full-time availability for duration of engagement (or part-time as specified);

(c) Qualified and experienced to perform Services;

(d) No conflicts with other agreements or obligations;

(e) All information provided is truthful;

(f) Services will comply with all laws;

(g) No third-party IP infringement;

(h) No violations of confidentiality obligations to others.

## 9. Warranties and Disclaimers

**9.1 Limited Warranty**
Contractor warrants Services will be performed professionally per industry standards.

**9.2 Disclaimer**
EXCEPT AS EXPRESSLY STATED, SERVICES PROVIDED "AS IS" WITHOUT WARRANTIES OF MERCHANTABILITY OR FITNESS FOR PARTICULAR PURPOSE.

## 10. Limitation of Liability

**10.1 Liability Cap**
TOTAL LIABILITY SHALL NOT EXCEED TOTAL FEES PAID TO CONTRACTOR.

**10.2 Excluded Damages**
NEITHER PARTY LIABLE FOR INDIRECT, INCIDENTAL, CONSEQUENTIAL, SPECIAL, OR PUNITIVE DAMAGES.

## 11. Indemnification

**11.1 Contractor Indemnifies Company**
Contractor will defend, indemnify, and hold harmless Company from claims that Work Product infringes third-party IP or that Services violate laws.

**11.2 Company Indemnifies Contractor**
Company will indemnify Contractor for claims arising from Company's use of Work Product beyond scope.

---

## 12. Compliance and Legal

**12.1 Laws**
Contractor will comply with all applicable laws and regulations in performing Services.

**12.2 Licenses**
Contractor responsible for maintaining own professional licenses and certifications.

**12.3 Export Controls**
Services subject to US export controls. Contractor will not export to prohibited jurisdictions or persons.

**13.1 Confidentiality**
Contractor will not disclose Services, rates, or terms without Company written consent.

**13.2 Non-Disparagement**
Contractor will not make disparaging or negative statements about Company.
