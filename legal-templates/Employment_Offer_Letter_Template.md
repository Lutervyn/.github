## 4. At-Will Employment

Employment is at-will, meaning either you or the Company may terminate employment at any time, for any reason, with or without cause, with or without notice. Nothing in this letter restricts your or the Company's rights.

---

## 5. Contingencies

This offer is contingent upon:

(a) **Background Check**: Satisfactory criminal background check and employment history verification;

(b) **Reference Checks**: Satisfactory verification of employment and personal references;

(c) **Drug Screen**: Satisfactory drug screening (if applicable to position);

(d) **I-9 Verification**: Verification of right to work in the United States;

(e) **Paperwork**: Completion of all required employment documentation;

(f) **Clearances**: Any required security or professional clearances.

---

## 6. Required Documentation

Prior to start date, you must provide:

- Completed I-9 form with original identification
- W-4 tax withholding form
- Direct deposit authorization
- Emergency contact information
- Signed confidentiality agreement
- Signed invention assignment agreement
- Signed arbitration agreement
- Signed handbook acknowledgment
- Any other company-required forms

## 7. Confidentiality and IP Assignment

You will be required to sign confidentiality and invention assignment agreements, which are standard conditions of employment. These agreements protect the Company's proprietary information and clarify IP ownership.

## 8. At-Will Employment Statement

**IMPORTANT**: You are an at-will employee. This means employment may be terminated by either party at any time, for any reason, with or without notice, except as required by law. No oral promises or understandings modify this at-will relationship. This letter does not constitute a contract for any specific period of employment.
